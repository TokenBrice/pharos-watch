# Code Of Conduct

Pharos aims to keep public collaboration technical, sourced, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, factual, and constructive.
- Assume data and methodology claims need evidence.
- Keep disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, timing, and risk.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private keys, tokens, emails, or requester details.
- Repeated off-topic comments after a maintainer redirects the discussion.
- Spam, scams, or promotional content unrelated to Pharos.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues, limit participation, or report abuse to GitHub when needed to protect the project and its users.

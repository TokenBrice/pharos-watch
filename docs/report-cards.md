Safety Score V10 is the sole active stablecoin safety model. It publishes evidence-backed grades from A+ through F. NR denotes genuine rating unavailability (or, since v10.55, a tracked tokenized fund published with reason `tokenized-fund-not-graded`), never a low-score or missing-subfactor penalty; technical Pipeline gaps have no score or grade.

The public grade-distribution total is labeled **assets**, since its A/B/C/D/F/NR segments include not-rated assets. Rated-only supply and coverage figures keep their narrower denominator, including the hero's supply-weighted grade row and its A/B supply headline.

## V10 Model

Safety uses the asset's quote clock when supplied. Captures that omit it retain the peg summary's observation clock. An explicit null clock or a withheld reference verdict leaves the current deviation unavailable. Admitted intervals control off-peg duration, and a reviewed active discount can impose its cap before the historic peg input qualifies. The deeper eligible child or parent discount can bind a wrapper. The quiet window starts after the last affected instant when captured. An operational recovery establishes a grade-history comparison baseline.

| Backing | <!-- GENERATED-START: report-cards-backing-pillar-weight -->40%<!-- GENERATED-END: report-cards-backing-pillar-weight --> | Reserve quality, mechanism solvency, custody, assurance, and loss-bearing structure |

Current, known reserve claims that hold or redeem into the subject stablecoin earn zero reserve quality and concentration credit and keep their weights in both denominators. Live claims qualify through a producer flag or an exact source-key match to a current own-liability review with evidence bound to the row. Concentration cites the evidence for the own-liability shares it includes. Composition freshness follows the holdings observation. ### Report schema v9

Body 9 wraps publication 8 (`model: "v9"`); see the [report9 wire contract](api-reference.md#full-cards).

Age rejections use the fixed scoring clock and the rejecting path's budget. Publications whose fixed scoring clock is at or after 2026-11-20T08:00:00Z carry the diagnostic; earlier cards omit it. Since v10.48, a rejection whose only reason is `beyond-age-budget` publishes the fallback gap as `expired-reviewed-reserve-composition`, owned by the `published-evidence-expired` responsibility with cause U, when the same rows' admitted reserve score is exactly computable; the reserve component then prices at the lower of the U-bound fallback and that score. Otherwise, and for every other gate, the card keeps the missing-composition reason, cause and message, so the diagnostic can name `beyond-age-budget` beside a `missing-reserve-composition` gap.

Archive-only via explicit historical dispatch: report8 (API v8)/publication v7 retain exact exit identity. Exclusions/partial weights, pipeline-gap/null-grade and NR remain distinct. ## Accepted capture archive and historical replay

When a direct bullion evidence map is present, physical-metal exit valuation requires an eligible open-session sample from a verified generation available by the assessment clock, with a 24-hour expiry. Captures that omit the map retain their commodity reference. New in-kind vault receipts measure execution cost at market value. In a clocked generation, EUR retention requires a separately dated, session-qualified reference unless the payout row carries a verified legacy reference. Earlier FX generations and producer-verified legacy payout rows in mixed generations use the receipt’s captured reference and its original freshness budget. Tracked-asset capture and route review preserve the same per-asset legacy comparison.

After canonical persistence, the heavy-only archive attempts every accepted generation without guaranteeing coverage; held/failed attempts write none. Apply additive, blob-free/no-backfill migration `0263_safety_score_capture_archive.sql` before activation. [Storage details](https://github.com/TokenBrice/pharos-watch/blob/main/docs/process/report-cards-appendix.md#archive-storage-details) own exact encoded transports, R2-before-index ordering, identity/digest/byte checks, limits and explicit metadata.

Archive waits/continuations are bounded and non-fatal. Failed, skipped, aborted or uploaded-but-unindexed captures may leave permanent replay gaps; no durable repair/retry is promised. The read-only gap CLI reports retained journal/cron evidence of missing index coverage, not every missing object. [Archive continuation and gap details](https://github.com/TokenBrice/pharos-watch/blob/main/docs/process/report-cards-appendix.md#archive-continuation-and-gap-details) own deadlines, outcomes and exact coverage accounting.

The 180-day `captures/` R2 lifecycle expires uploaded objects; daily `prune-cron-history` separately removes index rows older than 180 days and never issues R2 deletes. Verify the heavy binding's bucket lifecycle before release: migration/config checks cannot establish external account state. Lifecycle deletion is asynchronous and based on upload age; late uploads and delayed index pruning can exceed the nominal publication window. D1 retains only the narrow index, not object blobs.

Archive tooling (`worker/scripts/export-safety-score-capture-archive.ts`) lists the index, resolves adjacent identity boundaries and exports a generation from R2 or a local source directory, verifying object digest, bytes and identities. Its cache export feeds `capture-fixed-input --accepted-cache-export`; `--cards-output` also writes codec-validated decoded cards. Replay under the recorded build checks equivalence; comparing new-build replay of those same frozen inputs with archived cards isolates release from data changes. Project both sides to the same candidate-only shape, not a replay wrapper versus a public response. Maintainer commands live in `docs/process/safety-score-equivalence-harness.md#historical-release-and-data-separation`. Retention, failed writes and pre-deployment generations remain explicit gaps.

## Capacity scaling and chunked publication (ADR-26)

To scale Safety Score V9 beyond 397 active assets toward 560 within Cloudflare's 128 MiB isolate ceiling without changing scoring semantics or public wire contracts, [ADR-26](./architecture.md#adr-26) admits heavy-only Queue-chained execution on `pharos-v9-continuation` (`WORKER_V9_QUEUE_MODE = "on" | "off"`):

- **Seed and Eval.** At `:22`, Seed pins the clock, source and accepted-baseline identities, registry, policy, and evaluation build. It hashes canonical facts one asset at a time and stages the fixed input, replay input, and compact planning and commit metadata. Eval recompiles and scores the planned assets from that input in dependency order, with up to 70 assets per chunk and strongly connected components kept intact. Eval stages public card drafts, minimal open-evidence gap fields, and upstream results. Quarantine escalation reruns the same planner at the pinned clock and atomically replaces epoch staging and advances `quarantine_epoch`.
- **Commit and Materialize.** Commit rereads the accepted baseline and verifies its generation and digest before applying grade hysteresis, measured-exit expiry, and publication assessment. It reads card shards in sequence and accepts the publication in one atomic D1 batch. Materialize streams immutable public and site representations and atomically updates their pointers while the generation remains accepted. Completed and superseded runs prune their staging rows. [Storage and resource checks](./process/report-cards-appendix.md#chunked-publication).
- **Recovery.** The `:52` second-chance slot resumes the unfinished stage under the shared `worker-memory-lane:safety-score-v9` lease when Queue mode is on. The `:38` materialization recovery still uses D1. In off mode, the schedule uses monolithic evaluation.
- **Rollout.** The heavy Queue bindings and additive staging schema are deployed with `WORKER_V9_QUEUE_MODE = "off"`. The active cap is 397. Release 3 raises it to 560 after the resource gates, a dense 560-asset run, and the production soak. An SCC above 70 assets or staging JSON above 10 MiB triggers rollback before the cap increase. [Provisioning and rollback](./deployment-process.md#safety-score-v9-queue-provisioning).

## Dated public preview

The current `scores-latest` public dataset carries a versioned `_meta.displayProjection` object (also in the NDJSON preamble, never the CSV) with the accepted publication identity, `asOfSec`, `publishedAtSec`, `publicationHealth`, completeness and source generations. The generator appends `ratingStatus`, `safetyDisplay` (NR reasons/reason codes/partial evidence plus recorded grade hold or provisional labels), `dewsComputedAt`, `liquidityUpdatedAt`, `liquidityUnavailableReason` and `liquidityMethodologyVersion` to each row. Legacy envelopes emit the projection with `safety: null` and `safetyUnavailableReason: "legacy-source-metadata-unavailable"`; nothing is inferred from scores. The projection is version 2: every v1 field keeps its bytes and it adds `openEvidence` (`{ semantics: "open-question-v1", totals }` copied from the accepted publication) and `openEvidenceUnavailableReason` (`"safety-publication-unavailable"` or `"open-evidence-unavailable"`, null exactly when the counts are present). A publication without the block emits null counts with a named reason, never three zero counts.

`/safety-scores/` uses this export as a build-time, bounded preview: the 20 highest published numeric Safety Scores among active rated registry assets, with a code-unit ID tie-break, canonical name/symbol/link and recorded grade/score/labels. The visible caption is `Grades as of <publication UTC date>` (matching the `<time>` clock, not the dataset's calendar date) linked to the dated JSON artifact, followed by `Top 20 of <eligibleRatedCount> rated active stablecoins by Safety Score` (the shown count replaces 20 when fewer are eligible). Counts come from the full admitted census, never the 20 rendered rows.

The server renders the preview into the first HTML flush, outside any Suspense boundary, so readers without JavaScript see it. After hydration, the first-flush DOM stays mounted while the first live request is actively fetching and the preview is still eligible ([teaser presentation](./safety-score-map.md#teaser-presentation)). A successful live publication replaces the preview; a newer preview is retained when the live accepted generation is older and shares the same methodology identity. A changed methodology identity takes the live publication. Initial read failure or schema rejection retains the preview with the existing error/retry notice until the calendar retention boundary (snapshot UTC midnight plus `PUBLIC_DATASET_MAX_AGE_DAYS + 1` days). The initial render uses the build clock for hydration stability; the mounted page checks the viewer's clock after hydration and schedules a check at expiry, even while live data remains unavailable. Reconnecting before expiry keeps keyboard focus on the same preview link. If the request is paused offline at expiry, the page shows ratings as unavailable. Long waits use bounded timers and re-check the same deadline. A successful empty publication removes it. The preview is display props only: it never enters TanStack query caches, localStorage or IndexedDB, and the client imports projection types rather than the dataset.

Rated read-only top-driver `reason`: binding cap, else the published peg multiplier when it moved the grade (pillar quality and the peg-discounted base score grade differently; shown as the waterfall's `Peg performance · x0.510 on pillar quality 52/100`), else weakest pillar; Control prefers recognized score-matching binding mint posture, otherwise pillar/score.

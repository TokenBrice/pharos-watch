# Funding Page

Public `/funding/` is the static, indexable ledger of running costs and approved donations. `src/app/funding/page.tsx` and `src/components/funding/funding-page-sections.tsx` render build-time JSON; there is no page-specific cron, D1 or API. Preserve canonical/sitemap/navigation/footer/LLM-index discovery and no noindex header.

## Route and crawlability

Sitemap modification time is the newest route edit, cost review or ledger reconciliation time. Copy never implies real-time reconciliation. Funding follows the editorial brand register. The hero is the shared `FeatureHeroSplit`: monthly running cost is the One Beam; the sub-metrics name who covers it (community coverage this month, stamped with the ledger date; the last full month against that month's own recorded cost; community and founder support since launch) and the visual is the monthly donations chart. Bars, cost ticks and allocation segments stay neutral ink; no frost. The independence statement (no money for listings, reviews, ratings or placement; any paid service work for an issuer or its DAO public, listed here and kept apart from that coin's score) is the hero footer and an FAQ answer, aligned with the About independence principle. No urgency banners/modals; titles are noun-led; the only call to action in the hero is the anchor to `#how-to-support`.

## Data model

`shared/lib/funding/schema.ts` owns strict build-time validation. Invalid shapes, times or amounts fail the build; never hide them with defaults.

- `shared/data/funding/costs.json`: recurring monthly line items, owner TokenBrice, review target first of month. `last_reviewed_at` remains visibly stale when missed; advance on edits only after review. A line's optional `mark` is square self-hosted artwork under `public/media/` (a vendor mark, or a 64 px team avatar resized from the About team images), rendered in a circle; brand color stays inside marks and allocation segments stay neutral. `monthly_totals` records the published monthly cost of each closed month, strictly ascending: the first-of-month review appends the month just closed. Past coverage divides by that month's row, never by today's total; a closed month without a row is unavailable (`—`), not today's cost. April to October 2026 were seeded from the published total in this file's Git history ($1,709.01). `one_time` holds exceptional spend outside the recurring total (`paid_by: "founder"` for the redesign); it renders as its own group and counts toward the founder figure with founder ledger rows. Unpaid time is named, never valued.
- `shared/data/funding/donations.json`: human-approved inbound rows after self/spam rejection, reconciled roughly weekly/every Sunday for public claim timing. `last_updated_at` advances with approved rows; no approvals leaves the file unchanged.

A zero-dollar in-kind sponsorship stays a disclosure, not revenue or imputed market-rate cost. Dwellir's confirmed Growth-plan sponsorship has no invented valuation/term; only actual paid conversion permits repricing. Keep acknowledgment aligned with confirmed terms. The cost list keeps its $0 line with a `sponsor` object (self-hosted color logo, site, blurb, optional announcement); the schema rejects a sponsored line above $0. The acknowledgment renders first in the Monthly costs card's "Covered at no cost" group (`#in-kind-supporters`), with the sponsor's logo in color, its site and the [announcement post](https://pharos.watch/blog/dwellir-in-kind-sponsorship/); other $0 lines (unpaid maintainer, founder-covered services) follow as plain rows. In-kind entries never enter donation totals, supporter counts or the allocation bar.

Commercial partner-key payments (`COMMERCIAL_API_KEY_USD_PER_MONTH` in `shared/lib/public-api-contract.ts`) arrive at the same published addresses but are revenue, not donations. The ledger has no kind for them yet: do not approve one as a `community` row; add the kind and its summary treatment when the first payment arrives.

Donation identity is `(chain, token_address)`, not `asset_symbol`. Store ERC-20 addresses normalized lowercase; native assets use null. Symbol is display-only. Receipt USD is priced once at the transfer's block date, not current quote. Qualifying USD stablecoin parity requires contract-keyed admission; non-USD fiat qualifying coins (EURC, EURCV, EUROP, ZCHF, XSGD, CADD, AUDM, MYRC) use the receipt-date ECB reference rate for their catalog peg currency, never 1:1. Unsupported tokens need user-provided USD/source. Record provenance in price_note; pricing is not spam approval. A receipt that cannot be priced carries `price_note: "manual-token-not-priced"` (`isUnpricedDonation` in `shared/lib/funding/helpers.ts`); the donor wall renders its amount as "—", never $0.

The [funding-update workflow](../.codex/skills/funding-update/SKILL.md) owns reconciliation, receipt-date pricing and explicit candidate approval. ENS display requires reverse plus forward verification once per new address; freeze verified name/custom label/truncated fallback into the row. Unmatched same-ticker tokens get contract-distinct labels and cannot qualify by symbol. Do not imply an automated ENS or historical-pricing runtime pipeline.

## Supporter key

[API claim contract](./api-page.md#supporter-key-claim-contract) and [API reference](./api-reference.md) own eligibility, current-grade admission, key lifecycle/auth and private recovery. Funding only owns the evidence ledger and disclosure links. Use shared minimum/rate/qualifying-deployment constants, not repeated terms or hardcoded token lists.

`SupporterKeyNote` derives coin names from reviewed eligibility registry, respects paused claims, and links `/api/#supporter-key`. Direct-transfer/Giveth copy must distinguish signable donating wallets from exchange withdrawals/streams. Team access points to `/api/#partner-access`; `#supporters` remains the inbound API ledger anchor. Public mentions state donations become claimable only after the Sunday reconciliation appears on Funding and ships.

Worker embeds the same committed ledger used by Pages. Donation edits require both deployments through the automation registry; cost-only edits remain Pages-only. If ledger removal/correction invalidates evidence behind an issued donor key, the operator must also deactivate it: runtime does not recheck the ledger after issuance. Adding eligible coins/deployments requires curation and funding drift-test updates, not changing a display symbol.

## Ownership & cadence

Cost review and approved ledger updates retain original review/receipt clocks. A missed review stays visible until completed; no timestamp-only freshness claim. Any future automated pipeline requires its own operations/privacy/API contracts, not an implied extension of this static page.

## Editorial and presentation contract

Ledger totals and supporter counts use the full accepted dataset, not visible donor rows. The monthly chart (`buildMonthlyFunding`) draws community donations as neutral columns with a dashed tick at each month's recorded cost, the month in progress lighter; exact values sit on the bars and in the "Monthly figures" table; unavailable evidence is not a zero donation/cost. Each figure appears once per page: the beam is the only total, and the costs card shows category subtotals and shares. The supporter table shows the ten newest rows (date, chain and asset, explorer link with an accessible name) with the rest in a fold; its footer names the full ledger row count, not supporters; distinct-supporter counts come from the shared summary. Shared helpers/schema under `shared/lib/funding/` remain the numerical authority.

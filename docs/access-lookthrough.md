# Reviewed reserve-access look-through

Safety Score methodology 10.0 publishes reviewed receipt/bridge access chains as **diagnostics, not a new score or freeze penalty**. The runtime and API retain the internal `safety-score-v9` naming. `accessPosture.freezeLookthrough` describes access to reserve claims; it never certifies a holder-transfer restriction, supply partition, bridge solvency, financial assurance or economic loss estimate. Independently priced economic controls and issuer non-disclosure charges remain unchanged.

## Contract and admission

- `shared/types/safety-score-v9-access-lookthrough.ts` owns strict reviewer, compiled graph and public summary schemas. The canonical policy JSON owns edge, coverage, capability and unresolved-reason vocabularies under `semantic.accessLookthrough`.
- `shared/data/safety-score-v9/access-lookthrough-reviews-v1.json` contains four scoped reviewed graphs: `nusd-nexus`, `nxusd-nereus`, `syzusd-yuzu`, and `yzusd-yuzu`. Their selected LP/market books or unit claims do not establish a complete priced receiving-book census; unresolved branches and unpriced remainder stay explicit. No graph is synthesized from names, `variantOf`, governance type or another chain's blacklist boolean. Without a compiled reviewed graph, existing qualitative inherited reviews retain their documented declared-parent/curated-reserve upstream attribution and bounded scoring state.
- A reviewer supplies a receiving asset/root, exact chain and contract/native-claim identities, current receipt/bridge topology, implementation, entire-claim applicability, partition disjointness/completeness and each originating authority. Every node, edge, authority, partition and unresolved branch has original ISO review time, primary-source URLs and evidence responsibility. Dynamic reserve percentages and holder-supply overrides are not authorable fields.
- The Worker joins graph positions **after** existing reserve classification, freshness and dependency admission. Reserve fractions require a matching compiled exposure, exact keyed identity (or the existing uniquely reviewed unkeyed exposure), a known reserve envelope, and an admitted complete portfolio/onchain denominator from reserve-scope admission. Standing structure or a technical bridge comparison alone is not a priced reserve denominator. The receiving-book denominator may pass through reviewed unit claims; it cannot be reused inside a second reserve partition.
- Unit receipt/bridge edges require a reviewed current implementation and an entire held-claim relationship. A named structural dependency must match the compiled serial-claim edge and exact upstream identity. Selected observations and all unpriced/unreconciled positions retain null weights.
- Review expiry uses `semantic.evidence.evidenceExpiry.accessReviewMaxAgeSec`, with the same evaluation clock as the fact set. Future reviews create explicit future-dated unknowns without fabricating earlier evidence. Stale source/classification, rejected evidence, disabled claims and mismatched identities never establish numeric coverage or `none-known`. Clock and input-generation bindings are checked by the compiled fact schema; malformed receiving graphs are asset-local quarantines.

## Propagation and interpretation

`shared/lib/safety-score-v9/access-lookthrough.ts` walks every admitted path deterministically, preserving edge keys, physical position identity and the original acting deployment/controller/failure domains. A Solana USDS freeze authority does **not** apply to an Ethereum USDS holding. A null controller records missing controller identity without erasing an independently verified capability. A reviewed negative leaf must cover current upgrade/pause/claim applicability, not merely the absence of one blacklist function.

At a proved disjoint partition, weights multiply along paths; reviewed unit claims preserve the share and physical-position identity. Repeated routes to the same position are deduplicated. Unproved overlap or a cycle produces explicit uncertainty rather than a clamped or renormalized sum. Unknown children retain their priced remainder. Without an admitted denominator, aggregate, authority and path fractions remain null while qualitative authority identities and claim paths remain visible.

The public summary carries `diagnosticOnly: true`, `coverageState`, `knownAdverseReachShare`, `reviewedNoCurrentReachShare`, `unresolvedCoverageShare`, authority rows with `knownReachShare`/`unresolvedReachShare`/paths, unresolved branches with their originating responsibility, and evidence references. Authority shares are **not additive**: two authorities can impair the same 40% position. The three aggregate categories reconcile to one only when a common denominator is admitted; numeric zero is a reviewed measurement, never a missing-value default. An absent public summary means “not published”; without a graph, the evaluator returns null and public projection omits `freezeLookthrough`.

Direct reviewed local holder freeze still projects `direct`. Verified current inherited reach projects `upstream`, including qualitative unpriced reach or a separate unknown remainder. Possible reach projects `possible`; unresolved scope projects `unknown`. `none-known` requires complete priced negative coverage, never an empty authority list. Local `transfer` remains exclusively its independently admitted transfer review. The UI explicitly labels reserve access as diagnostic and renders the unknown remainder even beside `upstream`.

A compiled reviewed graph takes precedence over the legacy largest raw reserve-parent fallback: the fallback does not run for that asset, even when graph coverage is partial, stale or unresolved. Unknown graph branches cannot be filled from raw registry percentages. Independently reviewed local freeze and declared structural-parent facts remain; graph authority/path aggregation uses only the reviewed graph. Without a graph, a current inherited verdict can still name the largest directly freeze-capable active curated reserve upstream (lexicographic id breaks ties), with a declared active parent taking precedence. That legacy attribution grants the existing structural disposition, not numeric look-through coverage or a new freeze penalty.

## Evidence gaps and local verification

The shared graph-status enumerator includes every admitted node/edge/authority/partition/branch in reference validation and the evidence-gap queue. Graph normalization does not borrow a legacy ancestor's `measured-adverse` disposition for an unresolved descendant: issuer non-disclosure remains issuer-owned. Unpriced or invalid graph components receive queueable `missing-access-review` diagnostics; existing access treatment and economic scoring are unchanged.

Use real production capture replays to prove numeric neutrality and retained reviewed scope; the registry is not empty. For a later-clock diagnostic, copy and normalize the fixed input, preserve actual observation times, build the extension and compile/evaluate through the real candidate pipeline. Do not mutate circulating supply, date-bump composition, write D1 or confuse a new diagnostic clock with accepted-publication equivalence. Review current priced census/liability prerequisites separately for NUSD/NXUSD and unresolved strategy/satellite scope for SYZUSD/YZUSD; this capability does not manufacture them.

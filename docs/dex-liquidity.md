## DEX Liquidity Score

Hourly full publication admits recovered quotes at the next `:16` instead of waiting for an even hour. Source requests and the `:46` reuse path keep their existing cadence. Full generation and active-target writes run hourly under the existing bounded persistence buffers and retention. Public history remains one reusable daily snapshot, not an hourly series. The reviewed DEX evidence maximum age remains four hours (`DEX_LIQUIDITY_EVIDENCE_MAX_AGE_SEC`), and measured-history high confidence remains three hours; operational cadence changes do not tighten those scoring bounds.

The source stage carries only stablecoin identity and admitted pools, not uncomputed score totals. Scoring rebuilds authoritative aggregates from the retained pool population before persistence; the representation change preserves published scoring and source clocks.

Global headline TVL, protocol and chain breakdowns, and volume-coverage TVL denominators describe the same admitted cross-coin deduped pool population. External protocol totals do not impose a second global clamp or rescale observed volume. Suspect fallback observations are quarantined under the named admission policy below, never scaled into published raw TVL; this is not a claim that every admitted upstream valuation is economically correct.

### Published chain-label casing

Persisted DEX rows use the canonical internal chain id from `canonicalExitRouteChain()` (lowercase registry/alias keys). The `chain_tvl_json` keys, `PoolEntry.chain`, and `top_pools_json` chain values therefore use one canonical casing per chain; aggregate stages must not re-lowercase or substitute display names. Presentation adapters map those ids to `CHAIN_META` display names only.

Coverage normalization quarantines invalid class/confidence evidence per row as `invalid-coverage-evidence`, distinct from unobserved markets; it cannot supply a rating or trend. The `/liquidity/` page separates quarantined or unclassified rows into “DEX evidence unavailable”, preserving the publisher's unavailable reason. Their raw depth and diagnostics do not enter leaderboard, coverage, trend, or pool-quality statistics; only admitted unobserved markets belong in “Not observed on DEX”. Valid observed NR markets stay in the leaderboard. Trend-baseline confidence remains 0.50, durability 0.75, and both-null legacy coverage uses its conservative default. The leaderboard keeps NR null and places NR rows after every rated row on every column and in either direction, including after observed zero. Within each rating group, missing 7d TVL change, balance, organic share, and durability sort last in both directions; observed zero remains numeric.

In the full `/api/dex-liquidity` map (not the `/api/dex-liquidity-table` projection or history), each row carries `liquidityScoreUnavailableReason`: for a null `liquidityScore`, a machine code from whoever nulled it (`LiquidityScoreUnavailableReasonSchema` in `shared/types/market.ts`): the DEC-19 activity outcome from `resolveVolumeActivityComponent` (`activity-coverage-below-floor`, `activity-missing`, `activity-stale`, `activity-completeness-unknown`), `no-retained-pools` for a placeholder or scored row that retained no pool, or `invalid-coverage-evidence` when the publisher quarantines the row. The producer records its code in `score_components_json` (no migration); earlier and rated rows publish `null`, and consumers show plain NR rather than infer a cause.

External source construction runs hourly at `:10`. Both `:16` and `:46` use the same exact source-slot state machine: `writing` waits; `ready` scores and publishes; `failed` or missing terminal output can recover; `consumed` reuses the accepted generation without rewriting it. Only successful publication closes the slot; a guard-rejected candidate stays ready, and a live producer is never overwritten. Turnover follows actual publication, including late `:46` publication, never a wait or reuse. Targets stay private until guarded liquidity publication commits. The [publication and recovery contract](process/dex-liquidity-appendix.md#publication-and-recovery) owns readiness and lease admission, heavy-Worker reconstruction and preparation's accepted-generation checks under the unchanged evidence budget, never a restamped clock.

### Pool Quality Adjustments

- **Balance health**: Continuous `Math.pow(balanceRatio, 1.5)` instead of binary threshold. Curve `balanceMeasured` requires readable USD balances for every coin and agreement with the contributing TVL scope; the ratio, details, quality weighting and measured coverage use that same evidence. A Curve enrichment row alone does not establish measured balance coverage.
- **Pair quality**: Co-token scored using Pharos governance classification (CeFi->1.0, DeFi->0.9, CeFi-Dep->0.8) + static map for volatile assets (WETH->0.65, WBTC->0.6, unknown->0.3). Known quote aliases such as `USD₮0`, `USDT0`, `aUSDC`, `aUSDT`, `USDbC`, and `.e` bridged variants are normalized to canonical symbols before scoring. Composite Curve LP aliases such as `3Crv` and `FRAXBP` inherit the best score from their underlying stablecoin basket. Multi-asset pools use best co-token score
- **MetaPool TVL dedup**: Uses Curve's `usdTotalExcludingBasePool` to prevent double-counting base pool liquidity across metapools; the live Curve response owns the inventory.
- **Effective TVL**: `poolTvl x mechanismMultiplier x balanceHealth x pairQuality`, summed across all pools
- **Zero depth**: A measured zero effective-TVL aggregate stays zero in both relative and unavailable-market-cap depth formulas; raw TVL never replaces it.
- **Source-lane consistency**: Secondary and remembered live pools use the same `parsePoolSymbols` parser as primary pools (`USDC-USDT`, `USDC / USDT`, and recognized composite names), unless a measured pair-quality override is supplied. Registry fallback preserves selected measured balance ratios and locked-liquidity fields for DeFiLlama and direct-API rows as well as CoinGecko; `balanceMeasured` accompanies the forwarded balance evidence, not an optimistic default.

For direct APIs, balance health is no longer uniformly neutral. Balancer, Raydium, Orca, and resolver-backed Fluid pools contribute measured balance ratios when their APIs provide enough token-balance and pricing context. Fluid requires both decoded collateral and debt reserve halves before summing balances; a successfully decoded zero half remains valid. An unavailable half leaves balances null, retains independent ticker TVL/volume and names the failed half in enrichment warnings. Balancer stable pools omit their own phantom BPT from the balance composition, so the published ratio and details keep the swap assets' notional scope. Missing resolver evidence or unresolved token decimals uses the existing neutral unmeasured balance fallback, not a measured ratio of `1.0`.

### Data Quality Filters

- `isBroken === true` Curve pools: skipped
- Dead/rugged/deprecated protocols: excluded from `dexProjects` set and the explicit runtime blocklist (currently including Retro and Bunni variants)
- `exposure === "single"` pools (lending deposits, not DEX liquidity): skipped
- CryptoSwap pools: correctly classified via `registryId`
- DL Raydium pools: classified from `poolMeta` (Concentrated -> `raydium-clmm`) since v6.0, because DeFiLlama ships every Raydium pool under the `raydium-amm` project slug; this lets the DL row and its direct-API CLMM twin share one pool-shape family and deduplicate instead of double-counting
- **Primary identity:** opaque DeFiLlama IDs remain provider/chain-scoped deployments; sharing tokens or a fee family does not merge their TVL. Proven physical aliases count once. Identical primary repeats collapse before coin attribution; conflicting observations of one provider/physical identity are quarantined as `conflicting-pool-observations`, with count/TVL diagnostics, while unaffected pools continue.
- **Provider numeric evidence:** Fluid ticker and GeckoTerminal-shaped TVL, prices and GeckoTerminal volume require a complete finite decimal string; numeric-prefix garbage is unavailable, not a measured value. Valid signed decimals/exponents and explicit zero volume remain supported. Invalid price or volume does not erase independently valid TVL.
- **Orca token units:** balance normalization requires integer token decimals in `0..255` for both sides. Invalid metadata emits `invalid-token-decimals`, withholds balances and normalization/execution evidence, and leaves independently valid pool identity and TVL intact. Withheld balances cannot establish measured balance coverage; valid zero balances remain observations.
- **Curve balance completeness:** missing/malformed/nonfinite raw reserves, invalid or non-integer decimals outside `0..255`, and missing/nonpositive/nonfinite USD references withhold balance ratio/details, not independently valid pool identity or TVL. A finite explicit zero reserve remains observed. Nonfinite USD legs or totals cannot establish measured balance coverage.
- **Fallback TVL quarantine:** an observed positive, finite protocol total is admission evidence, not a replacement valuation. The crawler rejects a fallback pool above that total as `fallback-pool-tvl-exceeds-protocol`, including its price contribution. After ordinary retention, each coin's fallback protocol group is rejected as `fallback-protocol-tvl-exceeds-headroom` when its raw TVL exceeds the protocol total minus trusted pool TVL (floored at zero). The rejection follows that physical fallback pool across all coin views; it never clips trusted DeFiLlama/direct-API observations. Exact-boundary groups and groups without a usable protocol total remain admitted. Admitted raw TVL and separate quality/effective-TVL model values are not rewritten.

Publication guards require readable prior coverage count, global TVL, top-ten membership/TVL and cron-history evidence. A failed required read retains the accepted generation and its original clock with `required-baseline-read-failed`; it never supplies an empty baseline to disable a guard. Successful empty reads still permit bootstrap. Affected totals/classes remain unavailable in diagnostics. Coverage-class counts, watchlist and per-coin drift reads are independently tagged optional telemetry, not additional publication gates.

Two consecutive productive runs sharing one pre-event baseline also compare per-chain retained TVL and per-source-family retained pool counts. A cohort below 90% of its baseline is reported as `chain-tvl-drop:<chain>` / `source-family-drop:<family>` in `quality.reasons` and sets `quality.degraded`, which the sentinel-backed cache-quality verdict converts into a public-health floor. Unlike the other drift series these never rebaseline, so a lasting chain or source loss stays reported until recovery.

### Pool Stress Index (0-100)

Per-pool stress metric: `35x(1-balanceRatio) + 25x(1-organicFraction) + 20xImmaturityPenalty + 20x(1-pairQuality)`. TVL-weighted average stored as `avg_pool_stress`.

### Durability Score (0-100)

Per-stablecoin durability metric combining: TVL stability from 30-day CV (35%), volume consistency from 30-day CV (25%), oldest pool maturity (25%), and organic fee fraction with sqrt curve (15%). Locked liquidity removed — no reliable data source. Stored as `durability_score`.

#### Pool Quality Formula

Pool Quality measures the venue quality retention ratio: the fraction of total TVL that survives after applying mechanism and balance-health multipliers.

`poolQuality = min(100, max(0, (qualityAdjustedTvl / totalTvlUsd - 0.15) / 0.65 * 100))`

Where `qualityAdjustedTvl` applies mechanism and balance-health multipliers to raw TVL, and `totalTvlUsd` is the pre-adjustment sum across all pools. Pair quality is reflected upstream in `effectiveTvl` and pool-stress diagnostics, not in this retention ratio. The rescaling maps 15–80% retention to 0–100: ≤15% scores 0 and ≥80% scores 100.

Exact Curve metapool joins use retained, base-pool-excluded TVL for both numerator and denominator. Balance evidence must be complete and carry the same notional scope: a full-pool ratio cannot weight excluded-pool TVL. Incomplete captures and scope mismatches omit measured balance diagnostics, publish `balanceMeasured: false`, contribute no measured-balance TVL to scoring or source-family telemetry, and use the existing unmeasured neutral fallback without inventing a replacement ratio. With a scope-matched ratio 0.5, $2M retained TVL and multiplier 0.85 yield about $601,041 quality TVL (30.05% retention, Pool Quality 23), never a $3.005M full-pool numerator.

Missing measured balance/organic TVL weights stay `null` through storage, API and snapshots; a measured zero stays numeric `0`. Aggregate ratios include a member only with both its ratio and measured-TVL weight. Locked-liquidity averages count every measured pool, including `0%`, and are `null` only when none is measured. A partial-measured card without a balance weight reports coverage unavailable, never 0%.

#### Durability Sub-Component Weights

- **35%** TVL stability — `1 - min(1, CV)` over 30-day snapshots (CV = coefficient of variation)
- **25%** Volume consistency — same CV formula over 30-day daily 24h turnover (volume / TVL). Since v6.9 a recorded day counts when its window is complete (measured volume / retained TVL) or its admitted pools cover at least `DEX_VOLUME_COVERAGE_MIN` of retained TVL (`partialGrossUsd / admittedTvlUsd`); below the floor the day is skipped, never estimated. Legacy days without a record keep their stored volume over stored TVL. Fewer than 7 qualifying days in 30 still falls back to the neutral 50 (`durabilityVolumeConsistencyDefault`).
- **25%** Maturity — oldest pool age, capped at 365 days: `min(1, oldestDays / 365) × 100`
- **15%** Organic fraction — `sqrt(organicFraction) × 100` (diminishing returns past 50%; 25% organic → 50 score, 50% → 71 score, 100% → 100 score)
- **Basis homogeneity:** each 30-day TVL/turnover series uses one retained-TVL measurement epoch, selected as the latest epoch with at least seven qualifying samples. It never mixes epochs; without seven it falls back to neutral 50. Seven calendar days need not supply seven admitted turnover samples. `liquidityTvlBasisEpoch` compares stored versions numerically (missing means earliest), and the append-only `LIQUIDITY_TVL_BASIS_BREAK_VERSIONS` registry owns breaks. Retained-TVL remeasurement must append a break in the same version-bump change; reweighting, volume admission and discovery-admission changes are not automatically breaks. The [structured changelog](https://pharos.watch/methodology/liquidity-score-changelog/) owns past cutover effects.

### Measured volume availability

**Rows written before v6.9 remain legacy rows (no availability record, unknown completeness). They are not backfilled or restamped.** Measured volume preserves actual provider values and clocks, separately from decaying inventory TVL.

- **Shared authority:** `shared/lib/dex-volume-availability.ts` owns the admission window (`DEX_VOLUME_OBSERVATION_MAX_AGE_SEC`) and coverage floor (`DEX_VOLUME_COVERAGE_MIN`) constants, window classification, storage reads, consumer interpretation, the volume-activity formula, and the composite rule. Wire vocabulary lives in `shared/types/market.ts` (`DexVolumeCompleteness`, `DexVolumeAvailabilityReason`, `DexVolumeAvailability`).
- **Identity/admission basis:** volume and its retained/admitted TVL weights use the same admitted identities. Candidate token fingerprints are not aggregate keys; distinct same-pair provider deployments retain separate readings, and cross-coin attributions of one proven pool count globally once. A quarantined fallback pool contributes neither its TVL nor its volume/coverage weight; admitted provider volume is never scaled to fit an external TVL total.
- **Producer readings:** every retained pool carries its raw provider reading and original observation clock (`volumeReading`, internal, never published). Live DeFiLlama/direct-API readings retain their source-fetch clocks; registry readings retain the selected row's refresh clock. Resolver preference is highest-trust usable reading within 24h, else within 72h, else freshest stale reading; TVL confidence decay never scales volume. Missing/unparseable trailing volume is `null`, not zero; CoinGecko Onchain stores a zero only with explicit zero volume and zero 24h buys/sells. Zero readings follow the same observation-age window as positive readings; `DEX_VOLUME_TRADE_VERIFIED_ZERO_SOURCES` remains the separate dead-pool signature authority. Since v6.93, a row after the captured registry evaluation clock is rejected whole before resolution. Legitimate refreshes between source start and registry-read completion retain their original observations and are evaluated in the same generation, without changing source/quote clocks.
- **Pool eligibility:** a reading is `measured` when it has finite non-negative value and a provable observation clock within `DEX_VOLUME_OBSERVATION_MAX_AGE_SEC` (72h, inclusive). This unchanged budget is wider than the 24h staged TVL/price freshness window because registry discovery can leave readings one to three days old. The admitted value remains the provider's rolling volume as of its original clock, not a 72h sum or an unbiased census; the record names oldest/newest observation clocks and `maxObservationAgeSec`. Older readings are `stale`, absent/invalid value or clock is `missing`, and measured zero remains valid. Since v6.93, `applyPoolVolumeEligibility`, retention predicates and coin/global summaries use `registryEvaluatedAtSec` frozen in payload v4, not a later publication/reuse clock. Every downstream `volumeUsd1d`/`volumeUsd7d` is the admitted value or `null`; flags and `volumeObservation { status, observedAtSec }` retain that admission.
- **Window completeness** over the complete retained contributing pool set (not the visible top ten): `complete` (every pool measured; an empty set is vacuously complete at 0), `partial` (some measured), `missing` (none measured, at least one missing), `stale` (none measured, every observation aged), `unknown` (legacy row without a record, or an unreadable record). Every non-complete state carries a machine-readable `reason`.
- **Evidence consistency:** a positive `measuredPoolCount` requires a non-null `partialGrossUsd`, including explicit `0`. Contradictory records are unreadable; activity cannot zero-fill an absent admitted sum. Only a complete window with all three pool counts explicitly zero admits the vacuous zero. Older optional TVL fields remain absent/unknown and cannot manufacture an activity denominator.
- **Public fields (in place):** `totalVolume24hUsd`, `totalVolume7dUsd`, history `volume24h` and pool `volumeUsd1d` are widened to nullable. The measured total is a number only for a `complete` window; otherwise it is `null` and the additive `volume24hAvailability` / `volume7dAvailability` record (history: `volume24hAvailability`) carries `completeness`, `reason`, a separately labelled `partialGrossUsd` (observed volume over the admitted pools — a lower bound, never the complete statistic), pool counts, the observation-window clock (`windowSec`, `asOfSec`, `maxObservationAgeSec`, oldest/newest observation) and, since v6.9, the bound published with that statistic: `admittedTvlUsd` (retained scoring TVL of admitted pools), `retainedTvlUsd` (all retained scoring TVL after source quarantine) and `volumeCoverage` = admitted / retained (`null` when the set carries no TVL). Records written before v6.9 omit the three coverage fields. Pools may carry `volumeObservation { status, observedAtSec }`. Rows without a record omit these fields: consumers read them as `unknown` completeness and keep the historical number, never labelling it measured.
- **Storage:** migration `0249_dex_volume_availability.sql` adds nullable `volume_availability_json` to `dex_liquidity`, `dex_liquidity_run_rows` and `dex_liquidity_history`. `NULL` marks a legacy row. Since v6.9 every current, placeholder, `__global__` and daily-history row writes a record. The NOT NULL legacy volume columns hold the complete sum, else the admitted partial gross, else `0`, and are never published as measured unless the record says `complete`. `total_volume_7d_measured` mirrors a complete 7d window. Placeholder rows retain no pool, so they record the vacuous complete-zero window. A same-day history row that is carried forward keeps its own record. An unreadable record publishes `null` with reason `availability-record-unreadable`. There is no backfill: legacy completeness cannot be reconstructed from stored totals.
- **LiquidityScore (DEC-19, coverage-gated since v6.9):** Volume Activity applies the existing log-scale formula to admitted 24h volume / admitted TVL; stale and missing pools enter neither the numerator nor the denominator and nothing is estimated, zero-filled or decayed for them. The component — and therefore the composite — is rated when the 24h window is `complete` or `volumeCoverage >= DEX_VOLUME_COVERAGE_MIN` (0.50, exported from `shared/lib/dex-volume-availability.ts`; the exact floor is rated). A complete measured zero scores 0 activity under the full weight denominator. Positive admitted volume over zero admitted TVL has no activity denominator and is unavailable (`activity-completeness-unknown`), never a measured zero. Below the floor (including a coin with no admitted reading, `missing`/`stale`, or `unknown` completeness) the component is unavailable (`scoreComponents.volumeActivity: null`) and the composite LiquidityScore NR (`liquidity_score = NULL`). No renormalization, no denominator shrink, and no earlier score is carried forward. The 7d window is display-only. Valid components remain displayable beside the NR component. Coverage is an evidence-sufficiency floor, not an unbiasedness proof: unobserved pools may trade differently from observed ones, which is why the floor, the admitted TVL and the observation clocks are published with the score. Publication coverage guards and same-day history reconciliation count NR rows as observed (a rated score, or a non-`unobserved` coverage class), not as placeholders.
- **Downstream readers:** the DEX API/history handlers, Depeg Resolver context (current/30d volume and onset TVL baselines), public dataset snapshot and durability volume-consistency series apply the storage read; durability volume consistency takes coverage-qualified days only (see Durability Sub-Component Weights). Safety Score does not consume the composite LiquidityScore or volume: its native DEX capture carries `updatedAt`, exit-route observations and their coverage, so an NR composite alone does not make its Exit evidence unavailable. An NR composite reaches score-dependent readers as unavailable, not as a low score: the Selector's trading profile requires `liquidityScore`, so an NR coin is a coverage skip there (`shared/lib/selector/exclusions.ts`); the Depeg Resolver's K5 liquidity triggers (`liquidityScore < 20` / `< 30`) cannot fire for an NR coin, and its current and 30-day volume inputs are `null` for non-complete windows, so K6 wind-down and calm-catastrophic paths that need them cannot fire (`shared/lib/depeg-resolver/resolution.ts`, `worker/src/cron/depeg-resolver/context.ts`); and the DEWS liquidity signal is unavailable while `liquidityScore` is `null` (`worker/src/lib/dews/signal-families.ts`).
- **Frontend display:** `src/lib/dex-volume-display.ts` renders a non-complete window as an em dash with a `Partial ≥ $X` label in card cells. The bound is suppressed in favour of the completeness label when it would print as $0.00 (`DEX_VOLUME_BOUND_MIN_USD`). The `/liquidity/` leaderboard shows the admitted partial gross in-cell as a muted `≥ $X`, and its hero 24h DEX volume row headlines it as an explicit lower bound (`≥ $X`, detail `Partial lower bound: pools holding N% of TVL`); neither drops the `≥` or presents the bound as the complete total (R1/R7). DEX-implied USD prices use magnitude-based precision so sub-cent pegs do not round to $0.0001 or $0.0000.
- **Exit portfolio completeness is a separate policy:** `isDexExitRouteCoverageWithinRouteBudget()` in `shared/lib/p4-exit-route-capability-policy.ts` owns the existing gap-accounting carve-outs; exact-route scoring keeps the stricter `isDexExitRouteCoverageComplete()`. Neither borrows the volume coverage floor or Economic Control's circulating-supply materiality threshold. A small missing pool count, top-ten TVL, or missing liquidity value cannot establish an immaterial unobserved remainder. Pools without admitted exact observations add no executable capacity, but an unresolved coverage gap retains its own scoped cause.

## Discovery Cron

Census authority belongs to a successfully completed, exhaustive same-run query, not a provider's advertised chains or a missing next-page marker. Capped/failed queries retain observed pools but cannot certify no pools; supplemental venue indexes cannot prove chain-wide absence. The [source/scope contract](https://github.com/TokenBrice/pharos-watch/blob/main/docs/process/dex-liquidity-appendix.md#discovery-cron) owns provider routing and resumable-window mechanics.

Discovery remains bounded cron-native continuation. Missing rows receive census maintenance, not measured-zero status; provider observations and exact-route admission retain independent clocks and proof gates. A deployment that was certified `observed_pools` and is then certified `verified_no_pools` is a census flip; two or more flips attributed to one provider in a single run add `dex-discovery-census-collapse:<provider>` to `quality.reasons` and degrade the run, while a failed prior-outcome read reports `censusFlipReadFailed` rather than a clean count. [Discovery and source-stage boundaries](https://github.com/TokenBrice/pharos-watch/blob/main/docs/process/dex-liquidity-appendix.md#discovery-and-source-stage-boundaries) owns provider-specific quarantine, reviewed coverage, continuation, fair enrichment and capture/valuation details.

## DEX Price Cross-Validation

DEX-implied prices publish hourly with the liquidity consumer. Genuine pool prices come only from the deduplicated admitted pool set after quality filters and source quarantine, weighted by observed liquidity and source confidence. Exchange tickers are a separate price-only channel weighted by observed flow; they supply no synthetic pool TVL or executable orderbook depth. Missing trusted primary price withholds DEX publication rather than bypassing plausibility/outlier checks.

Individual pool challengers use the actual price-observing source family and support bounded by the smaller of retained pool TVL and the price observation's own TVL evidence. The same amount governs minimum admission, ranking, duplicate choice, 95% coverage packing and stored challenge weight; unrelated inventory value cannot strengthen a cross-source quote. Protocol-diversity representatives and the 50-row cap remain unchanged. Immutable publication-keyed membership makes same-source-clock shrink and empty replacements exact; readers never expose staged rows or combine two publications.

Retained prices preserve original source clocks independently of TVL/volume and publication. The [consumer-clock contract](https://github.com/TokenBrice/pharos-watch/blob/main/docs/process/dex-liquidity-appendix.md#consumer-clocks-and-unavailable-evidence) owns wire fields, strict-reader rollout and affirmative depeg freshness admission.

Price and liquidity have separate clocks. API price fields become unavailable for invalid/stale/future or publication-mismatched evidence; a fresh price cannot make stale liquidity fresh. Depeg corroboration uses a stricter 75-minute/≥$1M source-liquidity gate, while the summary display uses 60 minutes/≥$250K. Ticker-only zero-TVL evidence clears neither floor. A contradictory DEX price can suppress a new false-positive event under the confirmation rules, not automatically close an existing event.

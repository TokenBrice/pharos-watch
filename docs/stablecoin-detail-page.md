## Route Shape

- **Client entrypoint / compositor:** `src/app/stablecoin/[id]/client.tsx` delegates to `src/app/stablecoin/[id]/detail-content.tsx`, which composes the adjacent section-group modules under the same route
- **Yield subroute:** `src/app/stablecoin/[id]/yield/page.tsx` and `src/components/stablecoin-detail/yield-analysis-client.tsx` for yield-bearing coins and curated auto-lending workbenches; known tracked coins without a static workbench redirect to `/yield/?compare=<id>&from=detail-fallback&workbenchFallback=<id>`
- **Primary hook:** `src/hooks/use-stablecoin-detail-view-model.ts`
- **Pure view-model facade:** `src/lib/stablecoin-detail-view-model.ts`
- **View-model owners:** `src/lib/stablecoin-detail-query-view-model.ts`, `src/lib/stablecoin-detail-hero-view-model.ts`, and `src/lib/stablecoin-detail-view-model-types.ts`
- **Section components:** `src/components/stablecoin-detail/*`

Secondary taxonomy, case-study, mechanism, compare, AI-policy, Explore peer chips and research-context destinations use `IntentLink`: no viewport prefetch, with prefetch enabled only after pointer hover or keyboard focus. Local passport and safety-rail hash jumps never prefetch the current route. Primary parent/chain navigation keeps its existing policy; anchors and static research links remain crawlable.

`detail-lazy-sections.tsx` declares one literal client import per section instead of a shared section barrel. The Webpack `recharts-vendor` group still owns chart-library deduplication. Market charts, distribution, yield, DEWS history and the reserve treemap renderer mount only at their viewport gates, with persistent height reserves; chart/yield anchor wrappers exist before the chunks load. Reserve text, freshness and evidence remain outside the treemap's chart boundary. The yield workbench imports its history chart and chart-free attribution card independently.

The remaining distant reference islands (in-flow Price Transparency and the mobile coin timeline) also wait for their own reserved viewport gates; existing Market/Activity/History chart gates are not nested again. Zone IDs exist in the gated placeholder and transfer to their single content owner on mount, so hash alignment can scroll the reserve into view. Explore next is not gated: its links need no live data, so it prerenders inside the client tree, in the ready dossier and the in-client loading dossier alike, and is crawlable before hydration. Shared rail/pillar projections retain identity across parent navigation/feedback renders and rebuild when query evidence changes.

The reserve composition figure and its full accessible slice description are synchronous. Single-slice and dominant-slice baskets retain their CSS bars without loading Recharts; multi-slice baskets keep the labelled figure, risk legend and fixed-height chart fallback while only `reserve-treemap-chart.tsx` waits for the viewport.

Frozen archived-row projection lives in `src/lib/frozen-snapshot-live-summary.ts`, outside the global query descriptor registry. Descriptors retain lazy schema validation before query-cache admission; a frozen projection is not a newly generated live observation.

`generateStaticParams()` prebuilds one page per tracked stablecoin ID from `TRACKED_STABLECOINS`. The server route also:

- builds metadata through `buildStablecoinDetailMetadata(...)` (see [Metadata](#metadata))
- injects static logo data from `data/logos.json`
- injects static AI summaries from `data/ai-summaries.json`
- passes the page heading (`{name} ({SYMBOL})`, or the name alone when `hasRedundantName`) to the client: the identity header's visible title is the page's only `h1`, as is the in-client loading dossier's title while that state shows; descriptions live in metadata and Dataset JSON-LD
- keeps a visible dossier-style `Suspense` fallback with coin identity, classification, section rail placeholders, and score-card scaffolding while the full client boots. The fallback carries no content or links, because revealing the streamed dossier discards it in every renderer
- renders `StablecoinDetailSeoContent` and the collapsed FAQ (with its single FAQPage JSON-LD) after the `Suspense` boundary, outside it, so both are plain HTML for non-JS crawlers and stay below the dossier through the reveal and hydration
- passes `ExploreNextSection` into `StablecoinDetailClient` with the related peers, every `getStaticComparisonPagesForCoin(id)` brief and a **Depeg history** list: the coin's five newest permanent event pages from `DEPEG_EVENT_ENTRIES`, with anchor text `{SYMBOL} depeg {Mon YYYY}`
- adds a USDC-specific March 2023 depeg answer to that FAQ and its matching JSON-LD; the static Answer First fallback also links to the existing event timeline and SVB case study, without changing the hydrated hero's case-study link or creating a new route
- emits N-level `BreadcrumbJsonLd` plus a Dataset JSON-LD payload for active assets

Rated safety FAQ answers open with one dated sentence ("As of {Mon D, YYYY}, Pharos grades {SYM} {grade} ({score}/100) on its Safety Score.") built from the release's `scores-latest` mirror and `PUBLIC_DATASET_CURRENT_DATE`, so both visible prose and FAQPage JSON-LD remain dated assessments even when the live grade changes. The mirror carries no pillar breakdown, so the second sentence names the most specific build-time fact instead: recorded freeze controls and current reviewed redemption eligibility, falling back to the backing classification. Unrated coins say no grade was published and make no safety call. The interactive Safety Score stays independent and supplies the current risk read; archive and inactive answers retain their lifecycle-specific wording.

"What backs X?" leads with the authored reserve mix (top three positive slices plus a counted remainder, dated by `reserveReview.compositionAsOf` or its review date), then the proof-of-reserves type, attestor name and latest report period when authored. Active coins with a `REDEMPTION_BACKSTOP_CONFIGS` route add "How do you redeem X?": output, documented eligibility, reviewed minimum, settlement and fee range, dated by the route's `reviewedAt`. The answer is omitted once that review is older than `V9_REVIEW_EVIDENCE_MAX_AGE_SEC` (`src/lib/redemption-review-currency.ts`), and it states that the route's own terms, not the summary, decide whether a holder qualifies.

When two or more tracked records share a ticker (case-insensitive), the static profile and the pre-launch body list the others under "Other coins using the {SYM} ticker" with lifecycle, peg and the first clause of each `oneLiner` (`src/components/stablecoin-detail/same-ticker-stablecoins.tsx`). Parent, child and displayed sibling variants are left out because the Variant relationship block already links them. Coins with an authored `identifiers.isin` show a static "ISIN" fact, and the nested `about` Thing (never the Dataset) carries each code as a `PropertyValue` with `propertyID: "ISIN"`.

Active stablecoin Dataset JSON-LD describes the build-time markdown profile, not live analytics: `variableMeasured` contains peg reference, backing, governance, and listing status. Its `dateModified`, when present, is the editorial summary update date, not a live observation clock. Dataset nodes inline the Pharos `Organization` for `creator` / `publisher`, expose the CC BY 4.0 license URL, carry the stable Pharos coin URN in `identifier`, and set `sameAs` to the canonical Pharos detail URL. Provider/profile URLs belong to the nested `about` Thing's `sameAs`. Frozen assets use archive-specific historical variables, quarantined and delisted records use inactive-listing variables without live claims, and pre-launch assets use conservative `WebPage` / `Thing` JSON-LD. Every profile with a logo, pre-launch, quarantined and delisted included, declares the logo's original (largest) source as `image` and as the `primaryImageOfPage` of its `#webpage` node (the Dataset's `mainEntityOfPage` on dataset profiles), a candidate for Google's mobile result thumbnail; `og:image` stays the text share card. `buildStablecoinBreadcrumbItems()` in `src/lib/stablecoin-detail-json-ld.ts` owns the BreadcrumbList: Home, then the coin's peg hub ("US Dollar Stablecoins" → `/stablecoins/usd/`, from `buildPegLandingUrl()`) or `/stablecoins/` when no hub is built, then the coin. Frozen, quarantined and delisted profiles get the same middle crumb; pre-launch profiles keep Upcoming. Google shows that crumb on the desktop result URL line. The profile Markdown alternate (`scripts/lib/markdown-renderers.ts`) opens its Overview with the dated release grade (`formatSnapshotSafetyScoreLabel()`, the FAQ's scores-latest source and date) for active coins and appends a `## Quick answers` section built by `buildStablecoinFaqItems()` for every non-pre-launch profile; `public/llms.txt` coin lines carry the same "Safety Score {grade} ({score}/100) as of {date}" label.

Static HTML and Markdown overviews with supported live claim placeholders are withheld when their inputs are unavailable, using the shared `hasAvailableAiSummaryClaims` eligibility check rather than asserting an `N/A` grade or score. The static profile and summary provenance remain visible. The interactive summary resolves claims from live values. While the live report card is pending (the prerender included), the grade claim reads the build snapshot's dated release grade. A claim with no input withholds the overview behind the same `AI_SUMMARY_UNAVAILABLE_NOTICE`, never `N/A`. Markdown projects glossary markup to its readable labels, never internal `{{term:…}}` delimiters.

### Metadata

`buildStablecoinDetailMetadata()` (`src/lib/page-metadata.ts`) owns every profile `<title>` and meta description. Titles keep a 51-character core so the full title with the root ` | Pharos` suffix fits about 60 characters; frozen archives keep their earlier 61-character core. Descriptions are composed from whole sentences with `composeDescription()` at 180 characters or fewer and never end in "…".

- **Brand.** The optional coin field `brand` (20 characters or fewer) is the searched issuer short name (Circle, Paxos, JBFD). It is set only where `name` omits the issuer, repeats the symbol, or is too long for a title. Titles use `brand`, else `name` unless it only repeats the symbol.
- **Active titles (cohort).** Treatment: first fit of `{SYM} Stablecoin ({brand}): Safety Score & Risk`, `{SYM} ({brand}): Safety Score, Reserves & Risk`, `{SYM} Stablecoin: Safety Score & Risk`, `{SYM} Safety Score`. NAV tokens drop "Stablecoin" and use "Backing & Risk". Tickers shared by two or more tracked records keep the `{SYM} ({name})` form in every option. Control keeps the earlier "Risk Profile" chain under the same budget, with `brand` replacing a name that only repeats the symbol. No title carries the grade value.
- **Cohort definition.** Treatment is every second id (1-based positions 2, 4, 6, …) of `shared/data/stablecoins/canonical-order.json` as of commit `a23e4ced8`, frozen in `src/lib/stablecoin-title-cohort.json` on 2026-10-25 so catalog edits cannot reshuffle the arms; later ids are control. Measure treatment and control id regexes in Search Console for six weeks after recrawl, then adopt or revert the whole cohort by setting `STABLECOIN_TITLE_COHORT_MODE` in `src/lib/stablecoin-title-cohort.ts` to `"treatment"` or `"control"`.
- **Active descriptions.** `Safety Score {G} ({Mon D, YYYY}).` from the `scores-latest` release grade and `PUBLIC_DATASET_CURRENT_DATE` (the date the safety FAQ cites), then the curated `oneLiner`; the undated grade is the shorter fallback, and coins without a rated grade use the `oneLiner` alone. A differentiator sentence is appended only when it fits. Live price, peg deviation and supply never enter descriptions.
- **Volatile oneLiners.** A `oneLiner` with a supply, AUM/TVL or magnitude amount ("~HK$527k", "$1.2B", "5M USDC", `hasVolatileAmount()`) is replaced by a fixed-structure sentence built from the classification flags, so snippets do not cache a stale figure. Curators should keep amounts out of `oneLiner`.
- **Pre-launch.** Title: first fit of `{SYM} Stablecoin ({brand}): Launch Date & Status` (`Beta Access & Status` for `launchPhase: "beta"`), `{SYM} Stablecoin: …`, `{SYM} Launch Status`. Description: `oneLiner`, then `Latest milestone: {Mon D, YYYY}.` from the newest day-precision milestone, then `Target: {expectedLaunchDate}.`; fuzzy dates are never promoted to a day. The pre-launch `WebPage` JSON-LD carries that milestone date as `dateModified`. A `launchPhase: "launched"` row ("Launched – awaiting data") uses `Status & Tracking` in place of the launch intent (fallback `{SYM} Status & Tracking`), ends the description with `Launched {launchDate}; not yet tracked.` instead of the target, drops "pre-launch" from the fixed structure sentence, and its JSON-LD names a "Status & Tracking" page whose description says the coin launched and its market data are unavailable, not zero (no "Planned" or "begin only after launch").
- **Quarantined and delisted.** Title `{SYM} ({name}): Profile & Listing Status`. Description: `oneLiner`, "Not currently scored by Pharos: {category}." and "See history and documents.", where the category is "price or supply data under review" (quarantined), "outside the listing scope", "issuer wind-down", or "removed from the active listing". The raw `listingStatusReview.reason` stays on the page only.

If the ID is not tracked, the server shell returns a not-found-style fallback instead of mounting the full client.

If the ID is tracked but `coin.status === "pre-launch"`, the server route returns `PreLaunchDetail` instead of mounting the normal detail client.

If the ID is quarantined or delisted, the server route renders a static read-only profile with `ListingStateBanner`, the sourced lifecycle reason, FAQ, breadcrumbs, and conservative structured data. It does not mount live hooks. The Worker detail endpoint may return retained cache for historical context, but it never refreshes providers for a known non-active record; without retained cache it returns `404`.

The `/stablecoin/[id]/yield/` subroute is statically generated only for active coins that are intrinsically yield-bearing or have a curated deterministic auto-lending override. Those durable workbench routes use `noindex,follow` metadata. A lending opportunity can still appear dynamically for another active coin; when no static workbench exists, the Pages stablecoin function redirects that known coin to `/yield/?compare=<id>&from=detail-fallback&workbenchFallback=<id>` (existing yield query state is preserved) instead of serving a dead link. Unknown IDs retain normal static 404 handling. This policy removes low-value empty workbench pages from the Cloudflare export while preserving a useful handoff for runtime discoveries; see [yield-intelligence.md](./yield-intelligence.md) for the per-source APY history contract.

### Pre-launch detail variant

`PreLaunchDetail` (`src/components/pre-launch-detail.tsx`) is the server-rendered variant for tracked assets whose metadata status is still `pre-launch`. It is a deliberate variant of the live dossier, not a separate page style, and shows registry facts only: no market figure, no invented date.

- **Identity header** (`HeroIdentityHeader` grammar): logo, ticker and name in the visible `h1` (an `sr-only` suffix keeps the "pre-launch stablecoin tracker" wording), the one-liner, up to three link actions and a `Follow @pharoswatch` header action (`PreLaunchAlertAction`, an external link to the Telegram channel whose digest lists newly tracked stablecoins). Per-user launch alerts are retired, so no `/subscribe launch` command is shown.
- **Readiness hero:** a chip rail (pre-launch status, peg, backing, governance, infrastructure) over four cells: Phase (`Unphased` when missing), Target launch (`No target date` when missing), Target drift (shared `resolveDriftDisplay` vocabulary: amber for any push, red only when overdue; neutral `No current target` with "<old> target retired <date>" when `dateHistory` exists but no current target does, because a retirement may be Pharos curation rather than an issuer act) and Since announcement. Window countdown, overdue drift and day counts (`src/lib/pre-launch-dossier.ts`) share one browser-clock snapshot with the launch path through `usePreLaunchNowMs`: it refreshes at UTC day boundaries and on resumed visibility, without fetching. Inclusive target periods remain open until the following UTC midnight; the clock continues aging announcement days and the path after expiry. Server and first hydration render placeholders, never the build date. The shared timer/listener are removed when the last clock consumer unmounts. Fuzzy announcement dates prefix the day count with `~`.
- **Live surfaces:** one `EvidenceStateStrip` in the `pending-launch` state ("Activates at launch") names the surfaces that turn on at the first post-launch sync, instead of empty live cards.
- **Editorial summary:** the shared `AiSummary` card and its AI disclosure. It carries no source list: every claim must be backed by Pharos data or a sidecar module that carries its own provenance.
- **Launch path** (`EvidenceModule`): one UTC time axis drawn after mount with retired targets (dashed; the most recent labelled in-plot), the current target as a band spanning its whole fuzzy window, milestones (dots, clustered at a 16px gap; delays as amber diamonds), the announcement mark and today with the current phase. The first axis tick always carries its year. A structured verdict follows (no fact grid: the hero owns those figures); `Target history (N)` and `Launch status narrative` are `ModuleDisclosure` folds (the full `launchPhaseDetail` stays in the server HTML).
- **Launch status: quick answers** (`src/lib/pre-launch-quick-answers.ts`): server-rendered Q&A built only from registry fields, with matching FAQPage JSON-LD. "When will {SYM} launch?" states the phase and current target, or that no target date is set (never an inferred date); "Who issues {SYM}?" is the first clause of `oneLiner` (omitted without one); "Is {SYM} live yet?" states the pre-launch listing and that no price and market-cap source is admitted yet, with no exchange or buying advice; "Which chains will {SYM} use?" appears only when `contracts[]` records deployments.
- **Activity** (`EvidenceModule`): the newest five milestones with per-entry sources; older entries fold under `Earlier activity (N)`. Narratives and milestone descriptions wrap unbroken addresses so the page reflows at 320 CSS pixels.
- **Launched rows** (`launchPhase: "launched"`, `isLaunchedAwaitingData()` in `src/lib/pre-launch.ts`): the coin is live, so the readiness hero swaps Target launch, Target drift and Since announcement for Launched (`launchDate`), Tracking review (`listingStatusReview.reviewBy`) and Since launch; no countdown or overdue badge renders even past a stale target. The chip reads "Launched · no market data yet", the live-surfaces strip uses the `awaiting-data` state ("Awaiting admissible data") with the review's missing-lane reason, the verdict reads `Launched {date}, awaiting admissible data`, quick answers ask "When did {SYM} launch?" and "Which chains does {SYM} use?" and answer that price and supply are unavailable, not zero, and the rail card is "Profile" with "Chains".
- **Rail** (22rem from `xl`, in flow below): Planned profile facts and planned collateral, Key links, Related stablecoins (active and pre-launch candidates, so same-currency upcoming siblings rank first). Featured coverage follows the main column.

## View-Model Contract

`useStablecoinDetailViewModel()` gathers the detail page's shared query state, then delegates all derived formatting and fallback logic to `buildStablecoinDetailViewModel(...)`.

The query contract carries `liveSummary: DetailQueryResource<StablecoinLiveSummary>` directly, not a synthetic one-row stablecoin list. Market metrics consume that admitted summary; presentation receives only its quote/restored-supply fields plus registry-derived peg type and contract chains. Live, retained and frozen-archive selection preserves the original metadata and source clocks. The archive remains outside live health, and annual price history remains independent.

The Safety presentation facade exposes rendered pillar details, evidence summary and primary reasons only. Construction and Access posture call the canonical attribution/access builders directly; they do not depend on duplicate facade payloads or a separate weakest-pillar flag.

The health banner distinguishes an old producer snapshot, a failed refresh using saved data, and a source/quality advisory on otherwise fresh data. Liquidity advisories use the selected coin's nullable `warning` field; an unrelated coin's TVL cliff does not degrade this page. Global source failures and legacy responses without coin-scoped metadata remain visible, and suppressing an unrelated advisory never resets the producer clock.

Mint/Burn health likewise follows the rendered coin lane: a successful aggregate with no matching coin row contributes no nonexistent flow section. Loading/failure and retained coin flow totals remain monitored. The card does not render market-wide flight-to-quality, so its health projection removes only the typed `flightToQuality` Safety Score dependency and its matching advisory; all other warnings, transport failures and original flow clocks/budgets survive. FTQ's independent accepted-publication clock and 2h consumer budget belong to the flows-page FTQ disclosure, not per-coin ingestion health.

The detail endpoint waits for its on-demand refresh when an active coin's cached generation exceeds the shared stablecoins freshness budget. This prevents the first visitor after an idle period from retaining an avoidably stale response until the next poll. Still-fresh expired cache entries retain background refresh; genuine provider failures retain the stale-data warning and original producer clock within the 24-hour fallback ceiling.

Live-summary and supply-history queries use metadata-aware cache envelopes. The HTTP producer timestamp/status and warnings survive the compact projection and stale-query inputs; a successful HTTP-200 stale read or refetch never substitutes its browser receipt time for the source clock. Supply-history header chips, including expanded 1Y/All reads, use that same producer timestamp. Explicitly unavailable clocks remain unknown, not freshly received data; frozen archive fallback remains excluded from the live freshness banner.

Failed summary refreshes keep a matching saved dossier ready with page health and retry; only missing matching data takes the route error. Filtered directories independently disclose peg, liquidity and rating query freshness/errors while retaining saved cells; held-rating publication is a separate notice.

### Query inputs

The hook currently wires these sources:

- `useSupplyHistory(id, 90)` for initial charts; eligible non-USD/commodity, non-NAV assets independently request 380 days for an observed annual price anchor (±14 days). The annual metric populates when that anchor arrives, independently of chart-range selection; it does not block the hero. Ineligible assets do not fetch it.
- Wide chart selections are local, separately keyed reads: `useSupplyHistory(id, 365)` for `1Y`, and `useSupplyHistory(id, "all")` for every retained daily observation via `range=all`. Hover prefetch and build hydration remain 90-day reads; neither warms nor substitutes for the complete-history key.
- the registered `stablecoinLiveSummary(id)` query for the compact coin-detail price/supply projection, including the native token-count `nativeSupply` checkpoints behind the hero Supply trend (never a partial write to the global stablecoins cache). For frozen coins the server page also passes `archivedLiveSummary`, projected by `projectFrozenSnapshotLiveSummary()` from the coin's `shared/data/stablecoins/frozen-snapshots.json` row (supply observed at `capturedAt`; the archived row is USD-only, so its native checkpoints are unavailable). A frozen coin selects that archive directly with its original dates and null live metadata: the hook disables the live summary query (and `StablecoinDetailLiveSummaryStart` skips its prefetch, alongside the server page's existing bootstrap guard), and the archive is excluded from the freshness banner because it has no producer clock.
- `usePegSummary()` for peg score and depeg metadata
- `useDexLiquidityCoin(id)` for liquidity score and full coin-scoped DEX context, shared with the liquidity and distribution sections
- `useReportCardsV9Detail(id)` for the main Safety Score card and shared Safety/Context neighborhood; [compact website representations](./process/report-cards-appendix.md#compact-website-representations) owns its completeness and generation contract
- `useRedemptionBackstopCoin(id)` for the immutable completed-run redemption row and methodology, gated by the Risk zone's viewport proximity
- `useYieldRankingsCoin(id)` for yield-row availability and embedded detail context, retaining global rank/coverage metadata
- `useStressSignals()` for DEWS detail context
- `useBlacklistSummary()` for blacklist-support and summary badges
- `useMintBurnFlows()` for flow-surface availability checks
- `useStablecoinReserves(id, enabled)` for live reserve presentation, reserve-local retry state, and fetch progress when `coin.liveReservesConfig` exists

For `coin.status === "frozen"` the hook also disables the current-only liquidity, report-card, redemption, yield, stress, flow-summary and reserve lanes regardless of viewport controls, so they add no health entries and Retry All never refetches them; history lanes (supply history, depeg/flow/grade history) stay available. The compositors omit the current DEX liquidity card, `DistributionSection`'s DEX child and `ContagionSnapshot` (keeping the static variant relationship and chain context). Retained cached flow membership becomes `historyEligible`; `hasFlowHistory = hasFlows || historyEligible` gates the History zone's flow section, and missing membership is never recovered with a live fetch.

These three scoped transports use distinct paths/query keys and retain full-row values, nullable evidence, warnings, producer clocks and methodology identity. Hero liquidity/yield observers do not force full-cohort requests. The separate yield workbench keeps its cohort read because its peer rail compares other assets.


Depeg events are not part of the main view model. The `useInfiniteDepegEvents({ stablecoinId, autoLoadAll: true })` hook is called inside the lazily-imported `DepegHistory` component (History zone), which itself only mounts for non-NAV coins. Once mounted, it follows pagination until the complete per-coin event history is loaded. The rendered list opens folded to the newest 6 incidents with pagination hidden (`ShowAllToggle`, "Show all N incidents"); expanding restores the 25-per-page client pagination. `FlowEventFeed` (behind `FlowHistorySection`) applies the same fold to its fetched page — 6 events visible, "Show all N events" restores the server-paged view.

Historical Worst Depeg uses every fully loaded eligible incident, never the 90-day chart seed or folded rows. Its boundary combines uncapped producer `observationStartedAt` with the oldest loaded incident; legacy missing clocks fall back to observed incidents. Backend `historyCoverage` and score-window metrics stay separately labelled.

### Returned states

The builder returns one of three states (unknown IDs remain the server route's responsibility):

- `loading`
- `list-error`
- `ready`

`ready` contains the derived market, peg, Safety Score, liquidity, redemption, reserve, history, yield, DEWS, Mint Authority and variant payloads, expanded stale-query inputs and section-availability flags. `featureStates` has only two consumed lanes: reserve presentation and redemption read state. Redemption distinguishes deferred, loading, unavailable, retained, successfully empty and ready reads; it feeds one shared evidence projection for the main Exit board and desktop index. Other optional sources retain their availability logic and independent stale/error health inputs rather than unused facade states.

Client variant lookups live in `src/lib/client-variant-registry.ts` and read only the active compact client registry. Parent/relationship lookups require both variant fields and a resolvable active parent; unknown IDs and orphans return no relationship. Child lists match the direct parent ID in registry order, and siblings exclude the selected child without traversing indirect relationships.

The client `loading` state now mirrors the server fallback more closely: it keeps the coin identity, classification line, and dossier framing visible instead of dropping back to anonymous skeleton blocks.

## Section Order

### Evidence composition and responsive ownership

Shared shell, summary, fold and provenance rules live in [Design Language](./design-language.md#stablecoin-detail-module-contract); visual encodings and unavailable-state behavior live in [Data Visualization](./data-visualization.md#stablecoin-detail-primitives).

Plain non-folding cards use `DetailModuleCard` in `module-title.tsx`, retaining their existing Card/header/title/body elements and caller-owned section anchors. This shell does not adopt EvidenceModule folding or rail density. Access posture selects `variant="rail"` or `variant="strip"` explicitly; Mint Authority's scoring fold uses `ModuleDisclosure` directly.

The Risk spine reads Mechanism → Safety Score ‖ Reserves → DEWS → `#backing-evidence`, `#exit-evidence`, `#control-evidence` → Mint & Burn Flows. Safety Score and Reserves share an `items-start` row so expanding reserves never stretches the score card. DEWS is the live stress layer; NAV tokens and frozen archives receive a Not applicable strip. Each board opens with a plain `h2` kicker and trailing hairline; its grade/decomposition stays on Safety Score. Board contents run full-width signature modules → tile grid → full-width strip/absence lines. `resolveBoardGridPlacement` leaves no empty final-row track at one, two or three columns; hide a board only when no child is visible at the current breakpoint.

Mount each evidence module once in flow under its scoring pillar: mechanism/reserves in Backing, access enums in Exit, oracle/bridge evidence in Control. The `xl` rail is a normal-flow compact companion with its tail bottom-sticky (`xl:sticky xl:bottom-4 xl:self-end`), never top-sticky or nested-scroll. Its order is Safety summary → Backing KPI → Access posture → Regulatory standing → Price Transparency → Key links → Contracts → News → Evidence index. The index is last, lists modules in pillar order and flags limiting Control inputs. Access posture likewise mounts only with published rows or a pending grade read, never as an unavailable card. Below `xl`, Backing KPI and Regulatory standing mount in-flow anchor-owning EvidenceModule tiles, Access posture becomes a strip, the index disappears, and reference cards follow pillar evidence rather than precede Safety Score. The rail twins carry `data-anchor-twin`, not a duplicate anchor id.

Frozen dossiers omit Custody, Redemption, Bridging and Regulatory absence states, retain Mint Authority · Not reviewed in its slot, and state that DEWS does not apply. Other missing archetype-supported evidence uses an explicit neutral EvidenceStateStrip rather than silently vanishing.

Whole-module folding is phone-only: below `md`, a tile/strip uses the APG heading/button accordion, with CSS-only body hiding to avoid hydration mismatch or desktop shifts. `expandEvidenceModuleFor` reveals a folded hash target. Mechanism, Safety Score, Reserves, Redemption, Mint Authority, multi-branch Collateral pricing and DEWS never whole-fold. At `md` and up all summaries remain visible; detail disclosures remain collapsed by default at every width. The Control board's Mint Authority, Price feed, Bridging and Freeze & seizure modules put their visuals in initially open `visualFold`s labelled Control map, Pricing path, Routes by chain (N), and Power and scope, respectively, leaving verdicts first.

Economic Control is minimum-binding-component scoring: `binding: true` means eligible, not necessarily limiting. Compute `resolveControlComponentRoles` once per render and share it across Mint Authority, Price feed, Bridging and the rail index. All eligible scored components at the pre-adjustment minimum are limiting (including ties, outlined); eligible components above it stay solid without a tag. `binding: false` is diagnostic (muted/dashed, never outlined); null scores are excluded and read “–”; an empty eligible set outlines nothing. Use `ControlRoleTag` and `CONTROL_COMPONENT_ROLE_LABELS`, default size in module headers and compact in the index/deployment legend; never invent a local marker.

## Fallback And Staleness Rules

On the worker side, `GET /api/stablecoin/:id` now uses a small strategy layer:

- `worker/src/api/stablecoin-detail.ts` handles cache lookup, fresh/stale-cache hit decisions, and single-flight refresh de-dupe, then delegates provider selection
- `worker/src/api/stablecoin-detail/router.ts` (`routeStablecoinDetail`) owns provider selection, branching by commodity / coingecko-only / cache-backed / DefiLlama
- `worker/src/api/stablecoin-detail/commodity.ts`, `coingecko-only.ts`, `cache-fallback.ts`, and `defillama.ts` own provider-specific upstream behavior
- `worker/src/api/stablecoin-detail/shared.ts` owns cache writes, supply-history fallback loading, shared response helpers, and stale-cache vs hard-error response policy

For provider-refresh-eligible records, detail API stale-while-refresh is bounded: rows at least the 5-minute D1 TTL old but younger than 24 hours are served with their original `X-Data-Updated-At`, `X-Data-Age`, and `Cache-Control: no-store` while an isolate-local single-flight refresh runs in the background. Being due for cache revalidation does not itself mean the displayed data is stale: scheduled-refresh responses classify `X-Data-Freshness` with the current-market freshness bands (80 minutes fresh, 120 minutes degraded) and emit `Warning: 110` only beyond the fresh band. A failed-refresh response keeps `Warning: 110` and `no-store` but classifies `X-Data-Freshness` by the served generation's own age against those same bands (degraded through 120 minutes, stale beyond). Retained-history fallback responses keep their stale verdicts. Rows 24 hours or older are not served as stale fallback; the Worker refreshes synchronously and returns the normal upstream/supply-history fallback result. Known non-active records are different: retained cache can be served stale regardless of age, with no provider refresh; no cache returns `404`.

Cache clocks are admitted with the shared timestamp policy before any cache-hit decision. A clock more than 60 seconds in the future cannot grant a fresh response, extended edge TTL, or retained-cache fallback; active records refresh synchronously instead. Permitted clock skew produces age zero with the ordinary bounded TTL.

For every peg currency, including USD, DefiLlama detail history preserves observed `totalCirculatingUSD` and native `totalCirculating` buckets independently, including explicit zero. Native `circulating` aliases normalize to `totalCirculating`; missing unit representations stay unavailable rather than being filled with today's price or nominal parity. Required history has a `tokens` array, admitted observation dates and at least one valid observed supply representation per checkpoint. Malformed/missing history tries D1 supply history and retained cache, then returns an unavailable error. Empty or older-than-72-hour history uses the retained-fallback contract: original observation clock, explicit stale reason, `Warning: 110`, `no-store`, and no detail-cache publication. Admitted provider passthrough fields remain available. Canonical `/api/stablecoins` supply enrichment is unchanged.

`worker/src/api/stablecoin-detail/price.ts` overlays admitted `currentCirculatingUSD`, `currentCirculatingPrevDayUSD` and `currentSupplyObservedAt`. Canonical restoration provenance becomes `supplyRestored`, retaining the hero's neutral restored-supply date and a `Supply` page-health lane on that original clock; provider fallback never inherits it. Compact USD 7D/30D checkpoints anchor to the chosen current observation, not the latest provider row. Native trends pair their own current and checkpoints: canonical USD/finite positive observed price when canonical supply wins, otherwise provider native history. Matching native anchors must lie at/before the current-relative target within one daily bucket; missing clocks/anchors stay unavailable. The hero never mixes independently derived current supply with older paired checkpoints. Missing previous-day buckets remain unavailable. Without canonical supply, current falls back to provider USD, then native valued at an observed price, otherwise `{}`; nominal par never converts supply. Both snapshot acquisitions share this projection; no first-paint list request is added.

For USD-to-native history conversion (D1 supply history, cached checkpoints, CoinGecko market caps, and commodity TVL), an absent `totalCirculating[pegType]` bucket means native supply is unavailable because there is no finite positive conversion price or the division cannot produce a usable finite quantity. It never means measured zero. The authoritative `totalCirculatingUSD` buckets and history dates remain intact; commodity TVL is retained even when its price series is empty. Nearest-price lookup returns unavailable for an unusable nearest observation rather than manufacturing a zero quote. A genuine zero USD observation can still convert to zero native units when a usable price exists.

Both D1 supply-history and stablecoins-cache detail fallbacks retain finite nonnegative observations, including zero-only histories and a newer zero after positive supply. Missing or invalid supply remains unavailable and cannot displace the latest measured zero checkpoint.

### Build snapshot hydration

The server-to-client coin projection includes only the current coin's mint-authority summary, not parent summary payloads. Inheritance retains the parent ID for its symbol/link; server-side parent lookups still support archetype resolution, reserve look-through and inherited blacklistability.

The post-refresh generator `scripts/build-data/build-stablecoin-detail-snapshots.ts` validates `/api/stablecoin/:id`, projects current price, four USD supply checkpoints, three native token-count checkpoints and timestamps, and fetches 90-day supply history. Acquisition covers active/frozen coins only; other tracked lifecycle states receive empty envelopes without provider reads. A lane's `404`/`410` omits that lane, but both lanes missing for an acquired coin fails generation. Provider token history and passthrough research fields never enter the compact summary, and no global list or peg-summary cache is seeded. One gitignored JSON file per tracked coin is written under `src/generated/stablecoin-detail-snapshots/`. Supply-history `circulatingUsd` and `price` are stored at 10 significant digits (nulls stay null, nonzero values never round to zero), so the display-only `safetyAssessment` lane below fits beside both live lanes. The 8 KiB hard serialized-envelope cap drops supply history first, then live summary; a size-capped empty envelope remains valid. A display-only `safetyAssessment` lane carries the coin's committed `scores-latest` row (grade, score, DEWS score/band, liquidity score; read through `getScoresLatestSnapshotRow` in `src/lib/safety-grade-snapshot.ts`, the same mirror the FAQ cites), dated by `updatedAt.safetyAssessment` from the dataset's `asOfISO`. Bootstrap includes it because the mirror is committed data. It joins only the capped envelope, so it never displaces a live lane, and an undated row is omitted. It is never seeded as a query: the view model shows it only while the matching live read is pending. Pages preparation runs only `compile-input`; release acquires snapshots once after the Worker gate and release-data refresh. Standalone `npm run prebuild` runs `compile-input,post-refresh`. The initial charts select 90 days; selecting `1Y` enables a distinct 365-day query, while `All` requests `range=all` with no sliding cutoff. Both charts switch to available expanded observations when they arrive, without a first-paint complete-history request.

Credentialed local runs read the authoritative API (`PHAROS_API_KEY`, or `SITE_API_SHARED_SECRET` via the site-API origin). Offline CI bootstrap uses `PHAROS_DETAIL_SNAPSHOT_BOOTSTRAP=1` and acquires no live data. Without a configured base or credential, standalone generation uses the public GET-only `/_site-data` lane (`https://stablecoin-dashboard.pages.dev/_site-data`), whose allowlist includes detail and supply history. Production Pages release instead requires `SITE_API_SHARED_SECRET` in its build step and fails before generation when it is blank; it does not silently use credentialless per-coin acquisition.

`PHAROS_DETAIL_SNAPSHOT_SOURCE=per-coin|bulk` selects acquisition only. The generator defaults to `per-coin`; standalone `bulk` without `SITE_API_SHARED_SECRET` falls back to per-coin, while Pages release explicitly requires the secret and selects `bulk` (`.github/workflows/pages-release.yml`). Bulk batches at most ten active/frozen IDs through `/api/stablecoin-detail-snapshot-inputs` on the credentialed site API or trusted preview, even when ordinary per-coin reads use a public key. The bulk route is not mapped through browser Pages `/_site-data`. The Worker reads existing caches and 90-day D1 history only, freezes one canonical publication and completed supply marker per batch, and projects with `shared/lib/stablecoin-live-summary.ts`, returning independent lane clocks and freshness budgets. It never cold-materializes or schedules provider work. Unavailable entries name a reason and fall back to detail-plus-history acquisition for that coin; malformed batches or unaccounted IDs fail closed. Both sources share validation, catalog completeness, lane omission and 8 KiB capping.

After bulk acquisition, the generator selects available detail-summary rows strictly older than the UI stale band: `API_FRESHNESS_MAX_AGE_SEC.stablecoins × FRESHNESS_RATIOS.DEGRADED` (currently **120 minutes**, not the 10-minute endpoint advisory budget or 24-hour bulk admission ceiling). At most `DETAIL_SNAPSHOT_STALE_REFRESH_LIMIT` (**24**) rows per run, oldest first with canonical-ID tie breaking, use the ordinary credentialed per-coin detail-plus-history path. This bounds extra reads rather than refreshing the entire corpus. Unavailable bulk entries retain their existing per-coin fallback independently of that stale-row cap; malformed batches, schema failures, upstream failures other than explicit lane `404`/`410`, and missing-both-lanes failures remain blocking.

The generator runs at most six bulk requests concurrently, consuming each response body before that slot takes another batch. Stale-row refreshes and unavailable-row fallbacks begin only after all bulk bodies finish and share one six-request phase; each coin consumes detail before fetching history. Results retain deterministic catalog ordering. The detail route may serve its original generation while scheduling background revalidation, so a refresh-path read is not proof of a newer producer generation. Still-old responses and rows beyond the refresh cap remain seeded with their honest source clocks; neither successful HTTP status nor artifact creation re-stamps them.

Structured `detailSnapshotAcquisition` build-log evidence records every acquired coin's actual path (`per-coin`, `bulk-cache-only`, `bulk-unavailable-fallback`, or `bulk-stale-refresh`), returned lane clocks, and, for bulk reads, original lane clocks/cache-publication-supply sources or the unavailable reason. It names the assessment time, UI stale budget, refresh cap and full stale-candidate count. Preserve this log with release/equivalence evidence: it is run-scoped acquisition provenance, not a claim that the provider refreshed successfully. Keeping it outside the seed JSON preserves the exact snapshot byte contract.

`check:pages-release` includes an advisory **Snapshot producer age** line for emitted `liveSummary` lanes: max, nearest-rank p50/p95, count strictly above the named 120-minute UI stale budget, assessment time and oldest producer clock. Missing/invalid/future clocks and absent summary lanes are counted separately, never converted into zero-age evidence. A failed local read reports `snapshot-age-read-failed` without changing the release gate's blocking outcomes. Daily rebuilds and merge deploys bound artifact age, not producer lag; the advisory does not promise freshness until the next visit.

Any change to either acquisition path must be re-proven with `npm run verify:detail-snapshot-sources -- --keep-dir=agents/detail-snapshot-proof` against production after the Worker route and Pages proxy changes deploy. The GET-only proof fixes `generatedAt` and the local fallback clock, acquires bulk first to shorten the comparison window, compares every output file byte-for-byte, and exits nonzero on any difference. Its JSON report includes each pass's wall time and field-path/value/order diagnostics for the first ten differing files (`--diff-limit=<files>`). Output trees are removed by default; `--keep-dir=<parent>` retains both under a unique reported `keptDir` without replacing generated checkout artifacts. Retain both acquisition logs alongside the report/trees and inspect the refreshed, cache-only, cap-exhausted and unavailable cohorts: only acquisition evidence may differ by design, never snapshot fields, lane clocks or key order. Repeat the `dd6f3b7` / `65d4aee` cutover proof: require zero snapshot differences, or an immediate repeat establishing that remaining differences are non-reproducible live drift, before accepting this acquisition-policy change for release. A moving publication or a background refresh settling between passes is a real mismatch, never normalized away; a stale-refresh path label alone is not parity proof.

During static export, the server page reads only the current coin's file and passes it through the client boundary. The client seeds React Query only under the registered `stablecoin-live-summary` and per-coin supply-history keys, using independent millisecond clocks from `snapshot.updatedAt.liveSummary` and `.supplyHistory`. `generatedAt` records artifact creation only. Both acquisitions prefer body `_meta.updatedAt` / `updatedAt` seconds, then the Worker's absolute `X-Data-Updated-At` seconds, forwarded unchanged by Pages. A malformed present header leaves the source clock unavailable without legacy fallback. Only when that header is absent may a valid `Date` minus strictly validated nonnegative decimal `X-Data-Age` and edge `Age` establish a legacy clock; all three headers are required. Cloudflare rewrites `Date`, so this reconstruction subtracts edge residence but can still lose seconds at HTTP boundaries. Neither `Date` alone nor acquisition time establishes generation. Detail enrichment preserves the older real detail/publication timestamp; bulk uses the same response helpers without fabricating HTTP dates. Unproven bulk lane clocks make that asset `unavailable` with reason `source-clock-unavailable`; available entries still require both numeric clocks. Per-coin acquisition retains valid payloads but omits unproven lane `updatedAt` fields. An older source cannot replace newer live query data; clock-less seeds stay unknown and immediately refetchable, never fresh from artifact creation. The full `stablecoin-detail` key is not seeded, and producer-derived `staleTime` / `refetchInterval` remain unchanged. Peg summary remains an unseeded global query; the snapshot is not labelled live.

Hydration seeds `{ data, meta }` envelopes rather than plain lane values. Each lane's metadata clock comes only from its independent `snapshot.updatedAt` field; a missing/invalid clock yields explicit unavailable-clock metadata and an immediately refetchable query. Seed precedence compares the existing envelope's producer clock, so a recent request receipt cannot hide an older generation or let an older artifact overwrite a newer one.

For both seeded lanes, a seed awaiting its first live read is not evaluated by the shared stale banner while that read is fetching. Success or error permanently settles seed provenance; the served producer clock and any error then determine health as usual. Paused/offline reads and seeds with no fetch remain evaluated, and neither the producer clock nor the hero's neutral update stamp changes.

The first live refresh reads the site-only `GET /api/stablecoin-live-summary/:id` transport (browser `/_site-data/stablecoin-live-summary/:id`), not the history-rich public dossier or `/api/stablecoin-summary/:id`. The Worker runs the same detail provider/cache and canonical enrichment path, then applies `projectStablecoinLiveSummary` server-side. Its compact body retains the exact USD/native checkpoints, separate price/supply clocks, restoration/quarantine state and unavailable values; `_meta.updatedAt` is the original combined producer clock, never request receipt time. History and provider research passthrough fields do not cross this summary boundary. The full `/api/stablecoin/:id` remains available for public integrations and per-coin build acquisition.

The raw parser request remains early, but explicit query-cache adoption runs in the client boundary's mount effect, not during render. An already-resolved transport must not replace seed provenance before the saved dossier's first hydration render; only the mounted tree transitions from the saved-refresh slot to settled live/stale health.

For live-enabled detail pages, a nonce-bearing route-local script starts the canonical compact summary transport at HTML parse time. The mounted query adopts it once under `["stablecoin-live-summary", id]`, including recent build seeds; concurrent observers share validation. Frozen, inactive, pre-launch and unknown routes do not start it; later polls/SPA entries use ordinary transport. Equal aggregate clocks admit validated incoming values: the oldest contributing timestamp is not a payload identity, so canonical supply quarantine, price and history changes must replace saved values. Older/unknown aggregate clocks retain newer evidence with an explicit stale verdict. No receipt/adoption clock becomes producer time. [Browser evidence handoff](./process/worker-infrastructure-appendix.md#browser-critical-data-and-retained-evidence) owns transport disposal and bounded, versioned detail browser retention (display-only, post-hydration; 20-coin LRU, 72-hour producer-age ceiling).

Report cards, liquidity, yield and stress load eagerly because they supply the visible hero. Redemption, flows, blacklist and reserves remain section-viewport-gated; approaching a section is sufficient, without requiring a scroll, pointer or keyboard event first. Offscreen child modules retain their own lazy-render gates. The shared `useQuerySlice` projection preserves explicit `enabled` flags, and caller-local `useMemo` groups those slices with literal dependencies so interrupted renders cannot alter the committed projection identities. Intentionally deferred lanes are omitted from the page health banner rather than reported as missing initial data. The page-wide retry action includes only failed eager or currently enabled supplemental lanes.

The detail API can enrich a missing provider price from the current published canonical stablecoins row without altering provider supply/history or storing the quote in the history cache. Display enrichment does not apply the depeg detector's 30-minute observation cutoff or discard a publication merely because its nominal 600-second freshness budget elapsed. It preserves the original price timestamps, exposes the older of history/publication ages, bounds cache reuse by publication freshness, and retains stale-publication warnings/no-store behavior. Existing confidence, provenance, inactive/frozen-asset and invalid-price guards remain authoritative; unavailable canonical prices remain unavailable rather than being inferred from historical supply ratios. The compact summary preserves price source/confidence, observation/update/sync timestamps, observation mode and consensus/agreement source lists; older snapshots may omit the added provenance fields.

The route's freshness banner labels this combined generation `Detail snapshot`, not `Prices`: its retained history clock can be older than the independently observed quote. The label changes no clocks, budgets, warnings, or retry behavior.

`shared/types/market.ts` owns the public `StablecoinDetailResponseSchema`, consumed by both client parsing and the generated OpenAPI response contract. The public price can be `null` and its source/confidence/update/observation provenance is typed; provider-specific fields still pass through. The Worker retains its separate stricter upstream DefiLlama intake validator.

### Reserve presentation

The detail page prefers live reserve data when the coin is live-enabled:

- `liveReserves` API result wins when available
- otherwise it falls back to `getReserves(coin)` from curated/template metadata
- authoritative `live` / `live-stale` reserve responses can carry a separate reserve badge taxonomy: `Live`, `Curated-Validated`, or `Proof`

`ReservesSection` translates reserve modes and reserve badge semantics into a header chip plus disclosure rather than body notices:

- `live`
- `live-stale`
- `curated-fallback`
- `template-fallback`
- `unavailable`

Live-reserve fetch failures do not take the full page down. `buildReserveFeedStatus` (`reserve-presentation.ts`) turns a pending first run, or a failed fetch with no retained reserve read, into one ops chip (`Live sync pending first run`, `Live reserve feed unavailable`) whose tooltip names the reason code; activating it opens the `Feed status` fold, which lists `Reason: <code>` (never raw adapter error text) and a reserve-local retry action when the failure came from a fetch. Per [ADR-42](./architecture.md#adr-42), degraded or errored syncs over a served book (including a source-age-only stale disclosure) and failed refetches over a retained read get no chip: a partial book already publishes its unreadable share as the unavailable residual slice, dated footers stay neutral (no `Stale` suffix or `Stale feed` chip), and the operator Telegram (`Reserve sync degraded`) carries the per-coin transition. The page health banner's `Live Reserves` entry (`reserveStaleQuery`) selects the fetch or source clock whose public band (`FRESHNESS_RATIOS.DEGRADED`, 12× that clock's budget) expires first and ages that clock: a report merely past its budget is operator signal, and readers see `Showing an older snapshot` only past the 12× band; an invalid source clock stays stale. The module renders whenever reviewed reserve-quality data exists, independently of report-card data.

The page freshness lane uses reserve fetch/source clocks and their published `sync.freshness` budgets; whichever public band expires first owns client aging, so a longer source budget cannot hide a publicly stale fetch. It preserves `live-stale`, sync failures and uncertain writes in health classification; only the stale band reaches readers. Curated/template fallbacks or missing clock/budget authority remain unknown, never a fresh producer generation inferred from React Query receipt time.

Reserve quality labels `reserveReview.knownUnknownExposurePct` as **Unresolved reserve exposure**: it is the share of unresolved reserve dispositions, not a measurement of individually identified obligors. Recorded obligor names or classes are visible beside the metric; generic classes do not establish individual counterparties. Scores and adapter classifications are unchanged.

Custody summaries name a leader only when a positive known share is uniquely largest among the recorded shares. Unknown shares never confer leadership by provider order.

Shared failure-domain percentages read `nominalExposureShare` from the published V9 trace. When its scoring `exposureShare` is capped, that value appears separately as **Modeled contribution (capped)**. The dependency-map board reports member-supply subtotals and published point adjustments, not capped exposure percentages.

Static FAQ answers and their JSON-LD keep whole sentences within the prose budget. If the first sentence alone exceeds it, the whole sentence is retained rather than removing qualifying clauses.

### Shared stale banner

Shared health projection lives in `src/lib/data-health.ts`; `DATA_HEALTH_PRESETS` in `src/lib/data-health-config.ts` projects `API_FRESHNESS_MAX_AGE_SEC`. Backend `_meta.status`, `X-Data-Age` and stale `Warning` evidence override browser-fetch freshness: receipt of HTTP 200 cannot renew an old producer. `FRONTEND_API_QUERY_DESCRIPTORS` owns polling (normally producer interval stale time and twice-interval refetch), separately from endpoint/banner freshness budgets. Each route owns its monitored `StaleQuery` set; homepage PSI/mint-burn/DEWS and Compare supply-history/per-coin flows are not automatically page-banner queries.

Like an empty first fetch in flight, a seed awaiting its first live read is not evaluated while fetching: the detail summary and seeded supply-history entries opt in through `awaitingLiveRead`. The exclusion ends on settlement or pause, never hides an error, and leaves ordinary retained-data refetches and all freshness thresholds unchanged.

The page-level stale banner starts with the five page-defining shared presets:

- `stablecoins`
- `pegSummary`
- `dexLiquidity`
- `reportCards`
- `redemptionBackstops`

It also tracks supply history, enabled annual price history, yield rankings, stress signals, and enabled mint/burn flow, blacklist, and live-reserve sources. Optional section components use the same source status to show unavailable notices with retry actions; supported zero-result and unsupported states remain distinct. Depeg history manages its own local loading and error state.

Local distribution, blacklist, freeze-usage and flow notices reuse that health projection and its hydration-safe budget-boundary aging. A null producer timestamp never becomes “just now”; only missing legacy metadata permits the receipt fallback. Failed refreshes retain both the data and its original generation without a reader notice; compact freshness pills stay neutral until the public stale band (`FRESHNESS_RATIOS.DEGRADED`, 12× the producer interval). The redemption footer's “Updated” stamp receives the page's `redemptionBackstops` lane and passes its `deriveDataHealth` verdict to `FreshnessIndicator`'s optional `health` input: generation, amber state, announcement and tooltip budget (producer `degradedBudgetSec`, else 12× = 4d) follow `isPublicWarning`; retained degradation below that band is a neutral timestamp and null authority stays unknown.

Expanded market history is owned by the Market data block, not the page's 90-day seed lane. Its loading, failed and healthy-empty states without expanded rows remain locally visible with bounded-coverage disclosure and a selected-key retry; retained expansions warn only past the public band. A healthy seed never masks an expanded-read failure.

Static-export freshness notices render deterministically: initial age classification uses the saved query receipt time and timestamps use `en-US`/UTC. After hydration, the existing hydration hook switches to the browser clock/local timezone. Material staleness already present at receipt remains visible in server HTML; only subsequent wall-clock aging waits for hydration.

Public warnings follow [ADR-42](./architecture.md#adr-42): the page-level notice above the identity renders only when a monitored lane is past its stale band, or is missing with nothing retained; quarantine and delisting keep their own listing-state banner. Source advisories, dependency warnings, failed refreshes over saved data, catch-up and mild lateness are `degraded` and reach operators through Telegram and [/status](./status-dashboard.md) instead. The stale notice names the affected datasets, the last successful update and links to /status. Reader-facing copy never contains protocol prefixes such as `199 -`; raw transport metadata remains available to health classification.

The identity header reserves a separate Bluechip cell beside the actions and retains the mobile grade's dated-release height after the live read settles. Its neutral **“Updated &lt;age&gt; ago”** stamp uses the detail snapshot's producer generation: bottom-right at `lg+`, level with the one-liner, and in a reserved row below the actions at smaller widths. Server HTML shows “Updated —” until hydration supplies the reader clock. The stamp carries the absolute time as its title, never changes colour and is hidden when the producer clock is unknown.

Visible supplemental geometry is likewise reserved rather than gated on V9: the mobile grade has one shared track for pending/failed/unscored/scored states, and its verdict has a separate persistent lane above the static taxonomy row. The grade's partial-evidence line retains its footprint. Desktop collateralization slots are admitted by static metrics or live-reserve capability; access posture always retains its rail slot. Unavailable evidence stays explicit rather than collapsing cards above regulatory/price evidence. Compact passport cells use stable-width tracks, including non-interactive, accessibility-hidden reserves for unresolved redeemability, minting and peg-record facts. Mobile metric cells reserve wrapped reference/subline space and keep numeric values in their own track; exact evidence remains available without truncation.

### Retry behavior

`handleRetryAll()` retries only queries with an error, and only eager or currently enabled lanes:

- supply history
- compact stablecoin live summary (not the global stablecoins list), plus enabled annual price history
- peg summary
- dex liquidity
- report cards
- redemption backstops
- yield rankings
- stress signals
- mint/burn flows when their viewport gate is enabled
- blacklist summary when supported and its viewport gate is enabled
- live reserves when configured and their viewport gate is enabled

That shared retry is used by the page-level error surfaces.

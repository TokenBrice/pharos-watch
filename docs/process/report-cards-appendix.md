#### Exact NAV partition and quality source

At one finalized block, let `T > 0` be the source-accounted investment book in raw loan-token units: VaultV2 `realAssetsRaw`, otherwise MetaMorpho ERC-4626 `totalAssetsRaw`. Let `I` be idle cash actually included in that accounting and `a_m` each included interest-accrued lender claim. Admission requires **`I + Σ a_m = T` exactly**, using decimal-string raw integers, without tolerance or an unassigned dust plug. VaultV2 additionally proves **`totalAssets = min(realAssets, _totalAssets + floor(_totalAssets × elapsed × maxRate / 10^18))`** at the same pin, where `elapsed = block timestamp − lastUpdate`, and records **`undistributedAssets = realAssets − totalAssets`**. This exact source cap equation reconciles distributed ERC-4626 NAV to the measured book; the undistributed buffer is neither deducted from particular markets nor relabelled idle. MetaMorpho includes its withdraw-queue-accounted accrued claims and verified idle sentinels, not skimmable donated wallet cash; positive supply-only claims outside that accounting queue fail reconciliation.

Let `D = Σ a_m`, `idleShare = I/T`, `deployedShare = D/T` and, for `D > 0`, `w_m = a_m/D`. Backing and oracle materiality use the same complete source-accounted denominator, including any VaultV2 undistributed buffer. These are current **NAV-accounted book** weights, not curator caps, borrower debt shares, adapter limits or all lifetime positions. Off-list residual positions excluded by verified source accounting remain historical disclosures, not an invented NAV contribution or a claim of zero raw shares.

`P` is the existing evaluator's effective **parent Backing pillar**, not the parent's final Safety Score. Collateral quality `q_m` is hybrid: exactly one active catalog ID matching the collateral's chain and token contract uses that token's eligible Backing from the **previous accepted publication**, frozen at capture. Tracked quality takes precedence whether higher or lower than its class. Evaluation never fetches or recursively scores collateral, imports a curator's letter grades, or looks up today's registry during replay.

The accepted source must match its publication/health identity, be current rather than held, not future-dated and fit the existing inclusive **3,600-second** consumer budget. Its card must be rated with included, finite Backing; generation, clock, policy/build identity, card digest, cause and stable source-gap IDs remain attributable. Missing, stale, held, excluded, NR or otherwise ineligible source scores fall back to the authored class, never zero. Exact identity does not alias intermediary receipts: siUSD and PT-siUSD do not inherit iUSD's score.

An accepted-publication source failure is independent of book availability: optional compiled `lenderBook.acceptedSourceStatus` retains its referenced A gap and evidence, and publishes an accepted-source diagnostic with null score and zero effective weight. A successfully observed book still scores its authored class fallback; an unavailable score source does not turn that book into unavailable A or discard its lender claims. Legacy facts without the optional status remain interpretable. A stale diagnostic is `stale` only when it retains the prior publication's original clock, generation and digest under the independent one-hour budget; a capture that already reduced the source to `accepted-source-stale` unavailability with no identity publishes `missing` with that reason, never fabricated stale history. Either way the compile proceeds with the authored class, so one failed or late publication cannot block the next.

#### LLTV transmission and parent-ceiling blend

For a collateralized market let `l_m = LLTV_raw / 10^18`, validated in `[0, 1]`:

```text
t_m = l_m²
L_m = 100 − t_m × (100 − q_m)
Q_book = Σ (w_m × L_m)
bookAdjusted = P × Q_book / 100
R = min(P, idleShare × P + deployedShare × bookAdjusted)
```

The exponent **2** is a methodological calibration: transmission rises continuously with LLTV as liquidation headroom narrows. At LLTV 62.5%, 77%, 86%, 91.5% and 94.5%, transmission is 0.390625, 0.5929, 0.7396, 0.837225 and 0.893025 respectively. This scales the collateral-quality deficit; it does not equate actual borrower LTV with LLTV. Utilization, debt and stored balances remain diagnostics, with no added utilization haircut, oracle multiplier or historical-bad-debt penalty.

A genuine collateral-free, debt-bearing Blue market has no liquidation buffer: use structured-credit class and `t = 1`, even if LLTV is zero. Verified zero-parameter queue sentinels represent idle cash, not unsecured lending. A position that cannot be distinguished from such a sentinel is unavailable, not idle. Exhaustively measured `D = 0` with `I = T` retains `R = P`, with book quality/adjusted book quality not applicable, not zero. `T = 0` is unavailable (`zero-nav`).

The adjustment can only preserve or lower inherited reserve quality: `R ≤ P`. Both the existing inherited reserve contribution and its concentration mirror use `R`; their existing normalized weights sum to that quality without applying the haircut twice. A valid adjusted quality below the generic bounded-unknown floor remains binding, not replaced by a more favorable generic path. Mechanism evidence, wrapper-local charges, capital reuse, parent references/causes/caps, Exit, peg, structural caps and final aggregation remain unchanged.

Admitted Morpho lender assets also admit **complete same-parent multi-slice reserve envelopes**, not just a singleton. Reserve status and every slice must be required, known and live; every slice must map to the sole serial wrapper parent, all weights must be finite and nonnegative and sum to one within `SCORE_EPSILON` (`10^-6`), and every residual weight must be zero. Existing parent availability, wrapper-edge, cycle, mixed-parent and dependency checks still apply. These paths previously used generic reserve scoring: effective reserve quality is **`min(previous generic reserve quality, lender-adjusted quality)`**, retaining generic structural reasons, unresolved evidence and rateability. Only the binding path has scoring weight; the other path's factors remain zero-weight diagnostics. Unavailable books preserve the previous generic numeric path, and non-Morpho behavior is unchanged.

Nonfungible deployments are scored separately. For gauntlet's three `gtusdcp-gauntlet` deployments, for example, catalog-level lender reserve quality is the **minimum deployment quality**, never a supply-weighted mixture of unrelated share contracts. Every required deployment must be admitted; an unavailable deployment cannot be discarded in favor of a successful safer one. Lender factors show the limiting deployment's idle contribution at `P` and every included claim at `P × L_m / 100`, with weights reconciling to lender-adjusted `R`; all deployments remain in source metadata/evidence. If a lower generic multi-slice constraint binds, those lender factors are diagnostic rather than an additional scored haircut.

#### Live producer, freshness and unavailable behavior

The on-chain book phase runs inside the existing `erc4626-single-asset` adapter on `sync-live-reserves`, every four hours (`11 */4 * * *`), after ordinary denomination/redemption observations settle. It is not a new concurrent cron child or score-preparation RPC phase. It reads exact root/parent identities, current adapters and queue membership, market parameters, lender-only positions, accrued claims and actual NAV-accounted idle cash at one finalized chain pin. MetaMorpho interest, fee shares, virtual shares and rounding follow the source's accounting; nested vaults count only the outer adapter's actual ownership. New adapters/markets enter the current census; unsupported or unread entries cannot be omitted.

The reader retains block number/hash/time, acquisition clock, code hashes, accounts/core/adapter identities, selector inventory, raw amounts, LLTV, original stored diagnostics, reconciliation and content generation. Serial RPC batches of 32 fit the existing adapter I/O limiter; bounds are 16 adapters and 128 unique included markets per deployment. The book phase ends at the earlier of its own 20-second allowance or the runner's authoritative attempt/run deadline minus a 250-ms return margin. With no remaining allowance it returns unavailable (`deadline`) without starting I/O, preserving the completed ordinary result rather than extending the outer deadline. Bounds, cancellation, hash changes and required-read failures yield typed unavailable outcomes rather than a truncated denominator. The reserve slot topology and existing two-connection adapter declaration are unchanged; resource declarations do not themselves prove production behavior.

Book admission judges the older of block and acquisition timestamps at the fixed scoring clock against **28,800 seconds inclusive** (eight hours/two reserve cycles), with no future allowance or prepare-time clock refresh. On a later failed book read, the fenced reserve writer retains the last successful, reconciled, admission-valid book for the same asset/deployments and unchanged reserve configuration **while its original clocks remain within that budget**. Its pin, acquisition time, content digest and generation are unchanged; optional typed `lenderBookLatestAttemptFailure` records the actual failed attempt and compiles to a separate zero-weight A diagnostic. With other inputs frozen, the retained book keeps the book-adjusted Backing rather than rewarding the failed read with a higher inherited score. Once the original budget expires, or no admissible prior book exists, unavailable books retain **pre-v10.19 reserve scoring** (inherited-parent or generic multi-slice, as applicable) and expose the book factor as unavailable A with null score and zero effective scoring weight. This owner-authorized fallback is not measured safe collateral-book quality. A successfully measured unknown collateral contributes U35 instead. A failed book phase cannot erase independently valid denomination reserves or redemption certificates, manufacture measured danger or alone exclude the old whole Backing pillar.

Typed `lenderBookObservation` metadata is stored in existing reserve composition/history JSON and explicitly projected into native fixed input; preparation freezes the compact previous-accepted collateral score bundle alongside it. Old v3/v4 captures remain interpretable with named `legacy-unobserved` availability, not fabricated historical books. There is **no D1 schema migration**, new endpoint, public card field, API wrapper version or reserve-principal change. Public `backingFromLiveReserves` still means denomination reserve coverage, not a certificate of complete lender-book coverage. Old Workers ignore additive reserve metadata; an old strict prepare reader may require recapturing its old input shape while the last accepted publication is held on rollback.

Synthetic missing-book availability rows in native capture carry `fetchedAt: 0`; they diagnose absence without refreshing provenance. Only actual reserve fetches establish aggregate live-reserve observation freshness.

#### Separate Morpho 1%-of-NAV oracle materiality

For an admitted fresh complete book only, group lender claims by deployment + Morpho core + market ID, aggregating accounts before testing materiality. A borrower branch is material exactly when **`100 × branchIncludedAssetsRaw ≥ (deploymentRealAssetsRaw ?? deploymentTotalAssetsRaw)`**. Exactly 1% is included; smaller branches are excluded from aggregate worst-tier selection and binding weak-oracle caps but remain disclosed and **fully weighted in Backing**. The denominator is measured deployment NAV including idle cash (`realAssetsRaw` for a max-rate-capped VaultV2 book, otherwise `totalAssetsRaw`), not deployed-only share, borrower debt or rounded percentages.

Use the worst reviewed tier among remaining material branches, then the worst across deployments. Reviews may identify a borrower branch by `market-<hash>`, a bare market hash, an `oracle-<address>` ID, or the chain/core/market key; every join must also bind the exact chain-local reviewed path to the observed market oracle. Unknown reviewed weights remain material. An unmatched or ambiguous positive lender claim disables the NAV exception and retains the pre-v10.19 reviewed aggregate with a diagnostic; it never invents an opaque tier or changes existing authored missing-path/kind obligations. The exception can only exclude sub-1%-NAV branches, never make the aggregate worse than the pre-v10.19 computation. Preserve all authored branches, the full five-kind completeness census and wrapper-local NAV control evidence. Only the exact reviewed vault-share-accounting NAV path matching the observed root may bypass the top-level-only guard; unrelated pricing paths and unresolved applicability still block relief. Sub-1% branches emit neither a binding weak-oracle cap nor the otherwise binding `subMaterialWeakBand`. With no material borrower exposure after an exhaustive all-idle read, retain the authored aggregate tier rather than infer oracleless. Missing/stale/unreconciled books keep existing oracle behavior; CDP/non-Morpho materiality and all material control paths are unchanged.

Scalar settlement requires a dated current `supports: ["settlement"]` source establishing the completed endpoint, not processing, initiation, an average, epoch or earliest claim. Conditional/target calendar terms and rejected scalar/calendar overlays cannot revive captured generated SLAs. Spiko normal D+1 dealing, Aegis/Avant cooldowns, Hyperbeat processing and USN's five-Business-Day clause retain explicit settlement gaps rather than invented elapsed maxima. Exact funded post-maturity claims retain their separate evidence lane.

Per-point costs retain their own request identity. An observation-level scalar applies only to matching `requestedNotionalUsd` **and** `maxCostBps`; other points resolve from current bounded config terms. A minimum charge is not an additive fee or a maximum. Fixed issuer zero does not prove zero bank/network/partner deductions, full net proceeds or a differently scoped branch's cost. Curation cannot backdate fee reads; positive uptake needs a new producer observation and accepted capture.

`exit-routes` and `exit-portfolio-coverage` are separate from local cost/settlement gaps. Empty inventory requires complete deployment/supply-chain and absence evidence to establish a measured absence. For a populated DEX surface, portfolio completeness requires zero retained pools or admitted budgeted exact-route coverage; missing observations, missing discovery integration and unsupported exact venues retain their distinct causes. Redemption fee/settlement changes cannot clear the DEX portfolio denominator or alter failure-domain/resource diversification.

Coverage diagnostics distinguish **gap instances** from **affected cards**. Clearing a suffix is field coverage, not positive route admission or portfolio completeness.

The coverage audit accepts an optional `--report-cards` capture `{ asOfSec, cards: [{ id, localCauseGaps }] }`. Its `reportCardDiagnostics` is null without a capture; otherwise it reports the capture clock, `evaluatedCards`, `costGapInstances`, `costAffectedCards`, `settlementGapInstances`, `settlementAffectedCards`, `factorGapAffectedCards` (cost/settlement union), and `portfolioCoverageAffectedCards` (literal portfolio cause). Instances count distinct normalized local route-factor keys per card; affected cards count each card once. Static config disposition counts remain separate, and a fee-only correction cannot change required/admitted DEX-pool coverage.

The frozen replay/mover record lives in [v10.11 structured history](../../shared/data/methodology-changelogs/safety-score/v10.json); it does not certify fresh production observers.

Consumer rejection retains the captured execution evidence as rejected with its admission reason, withholding that route's capacity, cost and output credit without quarantining an otherwise usable asset.

Newly registered synchronous endpoints use an `unquantified` baseline: immediate, scoring and eventual capacity stay null until real execution evidence is available, never full supply or a manufactured zero. The reviewed Keyrock USDC, Steakhouse USDG and Steakhouse EURCV baselines intentionally do not consume reserve-derived liquidity as redemption capacity; their explicit unused-telemetry policies preserve the same-notional execution and, for EURCV, valuation prerequisites. Legacy Spark sUSDC's registered endpoint is its USDC `redeem` payout only; the separate `exit()` payout in sUSDS is not conflated with that route.

A proven-empty route surface stays measured-adverse (`no-viable-exit-path`, Exit 0) only when captured verified-no-pools coverage reconciles to every supply-bearing chain in the captured distribution. Unknown, unsupported, provider-inaccessible, missing or partial coverage is bounded uncertainty, never global exhaustion.

Reviewed above-budget costs retain their exact public trace but yield zero executable capacity within that budget, not a producer quarantine. A frontend pause observation alone establishes neither an incident onset, holder loss nor a suspension record; unresolved issuer routes remain unknown.

Disclosed processing terms do not by themselves replace a reviewed modelled completion horizon. Unavailable recovery, physical-exit reader boundaries and other integration gaps need scoped cause proof: A/B is excluded, C/U remains bounded where applicable, never measured holder loss. [Per-asset Exit rulings](report-cards-evidence-log.md#exit-rulings) retain programme terms, pause observations and owner decisions.

The [physical-to-USD contract](../redemption-backstops.md#physical-to-usd-exit-capability) owns lot sizing, throughput, cost/admission denominators, spreads, taxes/logistics, settlement assumptions, freshness and public traces. Physical routes remain modelled terms, not execution certificates; they use the common supply-sized request grid and independent-resource rules.

Every identity field, result digest, projection/publication clock, accepted health clock and health-row attempt clock must agree. Current-registry token resolution subsets the full eligible inventory; a missing asset row is ineligible, never a zero-quality card. There is no full-publication fallback.

Bootstrap requires absence of publication, both projections, score index, either accepted replay row and a published last-attempt, with health absent or valid held-bootstrap. Lender unavailability preserves `accepted-source-unavailable` and class fallback without claiming previous-accepted backing. Abort signals propagate rather than becoming absence.

Deploy strict readers only after Release A is active on heavy and public, one normal accepted cycle has seeded and verified both rows, no pre-writer publication lease remains in flight, and memory telemetry is live. A hold/failure extends the wait; no elapsed timer proves readiness. If restored/deleted/skewed state breaks projections, follow [accepted projection recovery](../runbooks/cron-slot-abandonment.md#safety-score-accepted-projection-recovery): temporarily switch both `capture.ts` lender extraction and `publication-runner.ts` baseline loading to the full-publication path on current head, retain atomic writers, let a normal accepted cycle reseed and verify both rows with the runbook's read-only identity/clock SQL, then revert that temporary change to restore strict readers. The cutover spans multiple commits; do not blindly revert a historical reader commit or replace whole files. Never hand-write projections or delete accepted state. Public API, index schema 2, archive transport and methodology identity are unchanged. Baseline schema consolidation touches the pinned evaluator closure and requires an authorized build refresh plus [neutral equivalence](safety-score-equivalence-harness.md#when-to-use-it); these reductions alone do not establish memory runway.

## Model admission details

The [V10 model](../report-cards.md#v10-model) owns the model overview. These detailed producer and valuation gates preserve its scope and unavailable-versus-adverse distinctions.

Published redemption schedules may resolve a conservative cost bound through a dated, fee-scoped `v9RouteCostTerms.publishedFeeSchedule` review: use the binding top tier/cap, or evaluate complete flat-plus-percentage terms at the request with admitted fresh captured FX. Partial tariffs, private-price overrides, unknown additional deductions and sampled quotes remain unresolved. Bounds above the request budget yield zero within-budget capacity without proving exhaustion. Business-day guarantees use reviewed jurisdiction calendars over the full 365-day submission horizon, never days × 86,400; completed KYC/delivery prerequisites remain access conditions. An explicitly published receipt-day rule needs no invented intraday cutoff, while payout gates, targets and unresolved notices remain unbounded.

Wrapper-local `measuredUnwind` accepts a fresh, admitted, exact-request transaction-simulation receipt proving at least 95% completion as a sufficient lower bound, even when the remaining route inventory is incomplete. A smaller successful receipt, an idle balance, an ERC-4626 preview or a reverted request never proves exhaustion or an adverse unwind assessment. Below that threshold, only exact-complete measurements across the entire admitted route inventory can establish the adverse completion bands.

Exact-request execution-model review expiry is inclusive in both certificate admission and missing-certificate gap enumeration; see the [execution-review boundary contract](../redemption-backstops.md#exact-request-execution-certificates-local-v10). A current review never substitutes for fresh execution evidence.

Repeated DEX execution history contributes capacity and maturity only while each original quote remains source-fresh at the DEX consumer's pinned assessment clock. A fresh selected quote cannot renew older constituents; expiry may remove a historical minimum or drop maturity without proving an execution failure or zero capacity. The [measured-history contract](dex-liquidity-appendix.md#curve-capture-and-measured-profiles) owns source budgets and independent cycle/failure accounting.

Measured EVM Exit profiles require coherent chain/number/hash provenance, independently checked at shared admission. Older number-only profiles remain unavailable until fresh exact-hash evidence replaces them. CryptoSwap's absent kill getter requires an executed inner refusal, not an RPC outage; unavailable proof retains operational history semantics and never manufactures zero depth or positive execution evidence.

Measured-target non-USD peg fallbacks require fresh per-peg FX evidence with an original source clock no later than capture. Missing/stale/future provenance withholds that valuation path, not a measured zero; current independent market/NAV and admitted same-run spot paths retain their existing gates. The [measured-target contract](dex-liquidity-appendix.md#measured-evm-joining) owns cadence-aware reassessment and retained diagnostic references.

For a reviewed synchronous ERC-4626 rail with an absent or stale share-price observation, the execution producer may instead value input shares from same-block `convertToAssets(10 ** shareDecimals)` and a separately fresh observed underlying USD price. The derived gross-NAV reference rounds down at the six-decimal USD boundary, binds the block hash and complete underlying-price provenance into the input generation, and inherits the older of their clocks. It establishes current native-share accounting value, not a market quote, financial assurance, fiat-redemption guarantee or exhaustion; stale/missing underlying prices and failed identity/NAV reads remain unavailable.

Source-reviewed ERC-4626-compatible custodian rails may burn a separately identified share token. Their exact canonical-request witness changes only the counterfactual holder balance, executes ordinary token approval, and verifies actual output transfer, holder-share decrease and total-supply burn at one pinned block. Reviewed gross-preview or gross-return conventions change only receipt decoding; costs always use actual net output. Ethereum custodian witnesses also require same-block transaction-gas estimation and fee-history pricing plus a fresh, identity-bound native-USD feed; those observations expire and never become published fee bounds. An admitted certificate may replace the exact reviewed primary rail, while failed execution cannot overwrite retained terms or establish exhaustion. External-share rails cannot synthesize a stale input market price from custodian conversion.

Exit output valuation preserves the existing admitted current-deviation and captured-NAV paths, including their retained route/source clocks and 365-day review budget; old fixed inputs need not contain raw-price provenance or `exitOutputPriceById`. The optional exact current USD-price census is an additional path for otherwise unvalued outputs, not a veto or replacement for independently admitted existing values. That new path retains the original quote clock and source, uses the 1,800-second current-price budget, and requires guarded protocol NAV for NAV outputs. Non-USD outputs compare against their independently current FX or commodity reference, not $1; captured GOLD/SILVER observations are not fiat FX aliases. Quiet peg history and explicitly unavailable or nominal-only prices never substitute for a missing observation; a rejected/missing current-census member supplies no new value. Single-output live valuations use the same exact identity and freshness gates as complete baskets. Every basket leg must have an admitted valuation or an independently reviewed exact conversion; a priced subset or USD-symbol accounting fallback cannot value the whole output. Prices resolve valuation only, not execution, capacity, fees, settlement or exhaustion. This adds no fetches, schedules or connection pressure.

Redemption output impairment follows the selected reviewed payout, not reserve allocations. A single stablecoin endpoint has full output weight; baskets need admitted exact-set payout weights to quantify an impaired share. Without those weights, the affected output identity still constrains route availability with an unquantified share. Token endpoints never inherit synthetic fiat valuation, and identifying a payout does not close its missing price, capacity, cost or settlement facts.

Captured peg rows that explicitly report an unavailable current price, and nominal-only references rejected by the observed-price reader, carry exact scoped A producer/unsupported-reader proof rather than U. Stale supply and peg observations similarly bind A to their retained observation clock, source generation and existing freshness budget. This does not refresh carried data, admit nominal parity as a market observation, clear adverse history, or classify a wholly absent row as a failed attempt.

An explicit failed-current-price result remains scoped A even on a zero-event peg history, with null current deviation. This supersedes only the clean-record exception of the 2026-07-29 P4 ruling: independently admitted adverse records and quiet histories without an explicit failed-current-price flag retain their existing treatment.

Wrapper-local reviews in `wrapper-allocation-reviews-v1.json.localReviews` are admitted independently of wrapper form and aggregate mint/oracle completeness. Pinned, exhaustive deployment reviews can establish Vault V2 share accounting or adverse root-code mutability; published instrument terms can establish fixed-face liability accounting, emergency impairment capabilities, and deployment-scoped withdrawal restrictions. Published fixed-face accounting retains moderate local risk rather than claiming a verified NAV oracle or backing; published emergency powers retain at least high risk. Withdrawal reviews preserve alternative routes, output assets, fees, eligibility cooldowns, and prerequisites; missing settlement bounds or fees retain high risk, and a cooldown never becomes an end-to-end settlement bound. These reviews cannot create executable capacity, measured unwind, custody completeness, borrower-oracle relief, or an aggregate controller census. Future, expired, stale, duplicate, unsourced, wrong-asset, and mismatched deployment reviews fail closed.

A source-pinned, admitted `routeSuspension` is a measured adverse **exact-channel** state (D), not an unresearched executable-route factor census. The withdrawn rail stays diagnostic and ineligible: it contributes no capacity, score or backup credit, and its deliberately masked access/output/cost/settlement fields do not create U/C obligations. Its adverse witness retains the review and source binding; the structural fact status remains non-known because it is not an execution observation. This does not prove zero total exit, exhaustion, asset-wide danger, or an executable recovery offer. Other redemption, physical-delivery, recovery and DEX rails remain independently evaluated; future or identity-mismatched suspension reviews are not admitted.

When a suspended channel has no independently admitted alternative that closes limited same-notional Exit evidence, a separate scoped U whole-Exit route-census witness remains attached to the limiting runtime/same-notional reasons. This includes measured alternatives that are immaterial at the canonical request under the existing Exit coverage/absolute-capacity breakpoints. Their measured routes and capacity remain visible; the witness does not assert exhaustion. Removing the closed channel's synthetic factor obligations cannot clear genuine Exit uncertainty or bypass NR. Material independent executable evidence can clear that census witness without reopening the suspended channel or inferring whole-asset danger.

## Publication journal implementation

`worker/src/lib/safety-score-v9/publication-journal.ts` retains compact attribution evidence in two D1 tables:

- `safety_score_publication_journal`: an accepted publication generation × coin row at first sight, when the compact digest changes, or when methodology version, policy digest or evaluation-build identity changes. The compact card includes nullable score/grade, rating status and causes, partial evidence, pillar scores/inclusion/causes, peg multiplier, binding cap, and selected primary Exit route identity, executable capacity and confidence evidence. Route generation keys and observation clocks stay in lineage, outside the compact change digest; a quote refresh or generation re-key alone is not a score movement.
- `safety_score_publication_attempts`: a publication-level row for each accepted or held attempt, with candidate generation, attempt/publication clocks, the same three-part identity, sorted hold-reason codes and input lineage. Accepted attempts retain changed/unchanged card counts; held attempts retain null counts and never journal candidate cards. Lineage names the base/source generations, DEX/redemption/transfer-materiality generations, registry fingerprint and input freshness; per-coin rows additionally retain supply and primary-route key/clock.

The heavy Worker's `publication-runner.ts` calls the journal **after** canonical persistence: accepted publication/health/attempt state first (then the non-fatal alert-envelope projection), or held health/attempt state first. The journal uses the already-typed publication and fixed input rather than decoding a full cache publication. Its failure is isolated: `compute-safety-score-v9` cron metadata exposes `journal.status` (`written`, `failed` or `skipped`), `reason` and `rows`; a journal error reports `journal-write-failed` and never fails or rolls back publication. A failed publication skips journaling with `publication-failed`.

Alignment brackets backward from the safe head, stopping at the first hash-bearing header in the inclusive oldest-pin/oldest-pin-plus-skew window; only overshoot requires refinement. It reuses that header for quantity collection but retains the final canonical recheck. CCIP runtime/token/peer/ramp reads use deduplicated serial chain-local batches of ≤32 canonical-hash requests. Each lane validates its values and historical peer pin; uncached unavailable prefetches fall back to lane reads with exact failure attribution. State, finality and failures never cross attempts; concurrency, timeout, skew and freshness budgets are unchanged.

OFT side identity and pathway peer/nonce reads likewise use deduplicated ≤32-request current-canonical-hash batches, with original validated-read fallback and no cross-attempt reuse. CCIP persists authenticated history before indexer/receipt corroboration; all discovery validations and the source-order digest still gate admission. Completed CCIP/OFT prefix writes are commit barriers: child cancellation cannot interrupt the D1 commit or suppress its progress receipt. Cancellation is rethrown immediately afterward; the asset stays rejected and partially authenticated pages are never flushed.

Dense CCIP pages commit up to 32 authenticated event blocks; whole-block suffixes resume from the saved cursor, never as a partial census.

Only authenticated finalized pins carry recent-state metadata and original clocks. Lag-only and different historical pins remain historical.

## Archive continuation and gap details

The awaited archive phase is capped at 15 seconds and the earlier publication/scheduled execution deadline minus a 2-second finalization margin. Caller cancellation settles promptly as `capture-aborted`; upload timeout is `capture-upload-timeout`, ordinary upload failure is `capture-upload-failed`, and an index failure is `capture-index-write-failed`. R2 PUT itself cannot be cancelled. A still-pending operation is handed to scheduled `ctx.waitUntil` with an independent 30-second continuation budget: late upload success indexes the same narrow row with `INSERT OR IGNORE` and logs `safety_score_capture_archive_late_indexed`; late failure/deadline expiry logs `safety_score_capture_archive_late_failed`. Run metadata retains the original timeout/abort verdict and `outcome: pending-continuation`, not a retroactive success. Without an ExecutionContext, it records `outcome-unknown` and does not promise late indexing or operation lifetime; continuation expiry also leaves outcome unknown.

Failed/skipped archives, terminal timeouts/aborts and uploaded-but-unindexed orphan objects can create **permanent historical replay gaps** after live cache keys advance; no durable repair/retry handoff is promised. Run metadata exposes these outcomes. `export-safety-score-capture-archive.ts list --gaps` additionally reports generations with **no archive index row**, unioned from two read-only sources and deduplicated by generation: the retained accepted-attempt journal (120-day evidence, including unchanged publications) and productive `compute-safety-score-v9` runs (`cron_runs.productive = 1`) whose metadata names a `publicationGenerationId`, bounded to the retained `cron_runs` window. Each gap names its `evidence_sources`; `journal` carries the attempt's publication clock, methodology/policy/build identity and attempt, while `recent_cron_runs` carries the run id, start clock and status. A generation observed only in a cron run therefore shows `journal: null` — the CLI never invents a publication clock or identity digests from run metadata, and recent-cron evidence is selected by run start, not publication time. Cron generation ids must match the producer's `report-cards:v9:v1:<sha256>` format; absent or malformed ids are excluded and counted. `recentCronEvidence` states the effective window, the imported retention bound and its `rows` accounting (productive, valid generation, already indexed, missing, malformed, distinct gap generations); when the requested window starts before the retention floor the coverage is `incomplete-window` and the absent older cron evidence is never reported as complete. The supplement reads only; it never repairs, retries, uploads or mutates R2. It reveals missing index coverage, not every missing object or a repair path. A late successfully indexed object may close a gap, but an orphan remains undiscoverable through index-driven export until lifecycle expiry.

## Archive storage details

`worker/src/lib/safety-score-v9/capture-archive.ts` **attempts to archive every accepted generation** after canonical persistence, beside the compact journal; this is best effort, not complete coverage. The heavy-only `SAFETY_CAPTURE_ARCHIVE` binding writes to the existing `pharos-measurements` R2 bucket at `captures/safety-score-v9-accepted/<YYYY-MM-DD UTC of publication>/<generation_id>.json`. Schema 1 retains publication/methodology/policy/build identity and the exact committed cache `key`, `value` and `updatedAt` for accepted replay base, delta, and `report-cards:v9` cards. Publication storage returns the already-encoded strings directly; the archive does not re-read overwriteable cache keys, re-encode input transports, or decode a full card set. All three values are already gzip-base64 codec envelopes, so the outer object stays plain `application/json` rather than being gzipped again.

R2 PUT runs first with object SHA-256 and identity custom metadata; only a successful upload is followed by the narrow `safety_score_capture_archive` D1 index row (generation, publication clock, methodology/policy/build, object key/SHA-256/UTF-8 bytes and archive clock). Migration `0263_safety_score_capture_archive.sql` contains **no blobs or backfill** and applies before Worker activation. R2 errors and index errors remain non-fatal: `compute-safety-score-v9` metadata includes `captureArchive.status` (`written`, `failed`, `skipped`), optional `reason`, `bytes`, `outcome` and confirmed `uploaded` state. Held/failed attempts write no object/index; missing binding or missing accepted replay transports skip explicitly. Each value uses the codec's 1,900,000-byte stored budget; the complete JSON object is capped at 8,000,000 UTF-8 bytes, otherwise `capture-too-large`.

## Settlement Calendar Admission

Business-day settlement uses `shared/lib/business-calendars.ts`, never business days × 86,400. Reviewed jurisdiction tables name their coverage, source, review date and weekends; `BUSINESS_CALENDAR_BOUND_POLICY` defines a 365-day rolling submission horizon and the existing reviewed-evidence age budget. Every submission day is walked, including closed days and requests just after the local cutoff; the latest completion uses the whole completion day, timezone/DST and all intervening holiday clusters. An explicit `cutoff.basis: "receipt-day"` contract instead counts from the received notice day, with no invented intraday cutoff. Any walk beyond reviewed coverage, an otherwise unknown cutoff or stale calendar leaves the SLA unknown, without shortening the horizon or extrapolating holidays. Only binding guarantees without unresolved payout conditions qualify. `accessPrerequisites` retains completed holder/receipt requirements before `startEvent`: EURI's condition 7.1 five-day bound applies to verified holders with burn-wallet delivery, and condition 13.1 binds notice to the receipt day. It is not a bound for an initial unverified request. BRLV/FUSD targets, Midas gates/disruption deferrals and PATH's conditional banking-hours reserve liquidity remain non-scalar; no generic conditional-term waiver or B reclassification is authorized.

## DEX Liquidity Score

The scorer builds the global headline, protocol/chain breakdowns and retained/admitted volume TVL denominators from one admitted deduped pool graph. It never applies an additional global protocol clamp or scales volume to match external TVL. `sourceCoverage.globalProtocolOverCapUsd` is the report-only sum of positive excesses over available external protocol totals; it does not reduce any published statistic. Named fallback quarantine replaces per-coin TVL scaling; trusted raw observations and admitted quality/effective model values stay unchanged.

`sourceCoverage.protocolTvlQuarantine` reports `fallback-protocol-tvl-exceeds-headroom` with unique physical fallback `poolCount`, normalized `protocolCount` and excluded raw `tvlUsd`, counted once across coin attributions. It replaces the old reduction diagnostic rather than claiming a partial pool reserve was observed. The crawler separately emits `dex-liquidity-source-quarantine` with `fallback-pool-tvl-exceeds-protocol` before admitting liquidity or price. Status distinguishes excluded observations from report-only global excess. Neither diagnostic reconstructs a pro-rata reduction.

On the first accepted publication after a TVL-basis release, compare the global headline with the protocol sum, chain sum and each volume window's `retainedTvlUsd`; `admittedTvlUsd` must equal that total only for complete coverage. Inspect the separate over-cap observation, named quarantine and existing fraud diagnostics. Verify suspect fallback pools contribute no TVL/volume/price, while admitted raw trusted values are unchanged. Methodology-driven changes are not market inflows/outflows; do not rewrite historical rows or renew incompatible source stages.

#### Publication and recovery

`worker/src/cron/dex-liquidity/orchestrator.ts` composes source construction and scoring/publication with the lifecycle modules under `worker/src/cron/dex-liquidity/lifecycle/`: `source-slot.ts` owns slot mapping and transition policy, `consume.ts` owns consume/reuse decisions, and `recovery.ts` owns readiness and recovery admission. Recovery receives the source-stage producer from the orchestrator, so the lifecycle has no import back into composition. `scoring-stage.ts` remains the sole manifest/storage/codec authority.

Staged CG/GT-compatible pools share one field projection, preserving original volume/price clocks and measurements. CG retains its fee-percentage conversion and separate first merge; `cg-pools-merged` (including measured-zero balance counts) and `gt-pools-merged` remain distinct.

Source staging is hourly at `:10`; both `:16/:46` consumers share one exact source-slot decision. The manifest moves `writing → ready → consumed`; failed writes become `failed`, and terminal/missing stages can recover under the source-job lease. A ready stage whose source clock already identifies the accepted publication is reused even if consumed marking failed. Only accepted publication marks consumed; rejected candidates stay ready. The readiness deadline remains scheduled delivery plus 90 seconds, with logical-slot fallback, signal and inherited slot deadline. A missing exact stage plus a successfully observed live source-job lease at expiry yields nonproductive neutral `scoring-stage-producer-running`; failed probes are errors, never positive lease evidence. This wait does not advance freshness or satisfy publication. Turnover follows actual publication at either tick; preparation independently verifies an accepted-generation fallback under the unchanged DEX evidence budget, never restamping source clocks. Heavy has Graph credentials for exact missing/terminal-stage reconstruction under preflight, source-job lease and remaining deadline; it never overwrites a live producer or admits a reduced graph. The [deploy/rollback contract](#v69-deploy-and-rollback-operations) owns compatibility and legacy-volume invalidation. Keep physical consumer aliases hourly; CPU and wall deadlines are independent.

D1 stage layout schema v1 and JSONL payload v7 are distinct. Payload requires nonnegative integer `registryEvaluatedAtSec`; old payloads/missing clock reject, never infer/restamp. Scheduled reads require the exact slot, ready/consumed state, matching chunk/record/byte totals and source age ≤55 minutes. Unslotted direct reads select the latest manifest within two-minute future tolerance, validate it, and never search older ready stages after rejection. Chunk/finalization writes are idempotent across ambiguous commits. Pruning protects the just-written stage, removes at most two others (consumed first, then writing/ready/failed older than two hours). Publication precedes the best-effort consumed marker; marker failure does not invalidate output. Source graph headers contain only `stablecoinId` and `symbol`, followed by streamed pool records; dormant aggregate totals and empty chain/pair sets are not serialized. Only scoring constructs `LiquidityMetrics` from the retained-pool rebuild, and persistence consumes that scored map.

Current publication is active-universe scoped, including zero-pool placeholders and `__global__`. Private `dex_liquidity_run_rows` must meet exact candidate coverage before one final two-statement batch mirrors the public table and marks the generation published. Depth stability joins that same generation from the scoring pass's pre-snapshot history sample; later price/history failures cannot separate it from the accepted liquidity fields. Tagged readers require published state; untagged compatibility rows keep stored methodology, never reconstructed from time. Failure cannot advance freshness. Target descriptors remain private until all source/coverage/value/major-asset guards and full liquidity persistence succeed; rejected candidates preserve prior active/shadow catalogs. Normal reuse writes neither; shadow cadence stays daily.

#### Source failures and diagnostics

`ok` means required sources and coverage are healthy. Critical DL Protocols/Yields loss returns degraded and preserves the last source-complete dataset, never capless secondary liquidity. Evaluate all near/hard coverage/value guards even then, but suppress a secondary hard abort when critical failure already prevents publication. Catastrophic DL+Curve failure or unexpected invariants throw; hard guards otherwise abort. Baselines use the median of six trailing productive publications (persisted fallback alone if necessary), with a source-complete baseline for source-complete recovery.

Optional native-API outages alone need not degrade healthy published coverage. `failedSources` means no usable output; partial usable output gets `*-partial`, warnings and failed-chain identities. Headers carry those diagnostics, target enrichment and Graph presence (boolean only). Primary malformed-row reasons are `invalid-pool-identity`, `invalid-pool-tvl`, `invalid-pool-volume`, with full rejected count/TVL and ≤20 samples each. $10K lost TVL is material; dust stays telemetry. DL identity validation precedes matching; transport/envelope/cohort failures stay source-wide. Unexpected exceptions never become ordinary rejections.

`orchestrator-metadata.ts` owns terminal projection, separate from the source DTO. Counts survive count/byte compaction; removed detail is unavailable. Target funnels count opportunities versus deduplicated directed targets, not conserved physical pools; resolved descriptors are not certified proof. Missing Graph names skipped sources, not healthy empty enrichment. Recovery forwards only allowlisted stage identity/status/clock/cost diagnostics. Route-selection compares final persisted IDs after continuity holds; failed prior reads mean unavailable, not stability. Removal cause precedence is payload overflow, representative change, actual missing/stale/invalid decisions, else unknown—aggregate coincidence is not a route cause. TVL steps use latest prior publication; failed baseline reads cannot fabricate missing-cohort counts.

D1 cost `queries` counts statement attempts from returned metadata, including batch members and automatic retries, not network round trips or inventory row counts. Additive `bindingCalls` counts observed wrapped data-operation invocations, with each batch counted once and `batchCalls` as its subset; automatic D1 retries increase `queries` without pretending to be separate observed calls. `awaitElapsedMs` sums operation waits through success or failure, including overlapping waits, so it is neither SQL-only time nor whole-run wall time. `sqlDurationMs` sums only available SQL-duration observations (`timings.sql_duration_ms`, otherwise `duration`); it is null with no observation, and `sqlDurationResults` names the contributing result count rather than claiming complete coverage. Region and primary/replica counters describe only explicitly returned database metadata; missing fields remain unobserved, and mixed regions are marked rather than enumerated or mislabeled as Worker colo. These fixed-size diagnostics add no queries or per-query logs. DEX-body row-cost coverage remains partial (outer lease/progress, cron logger/terminal writes and failed-run cost excluded); unavailable metadata and failed attempts retain their existing row-cost reasons. Nested recovery snapshots describe overlapping scopes and must not be summed as invocation totals. Report-only `fallbackCounters` counts defaults/exclusions/adoption without formula effect. Daily shadow collection cannot retire until every C01–C03 writer has an accepted deployed disposition, including six Curve observers' funded-capacity decision; missing targets/deadlines/read failures are not closure. Use the [acceptance capture/archive rules](../scripts.md#dex-liquidity-acceptance-capture) before cron pruning; corrected-input or score-stability claims require actual acceptance.

The same constant-size `cron_runs.metadata.d1Cost` summary is emitted by live-reserve sync, project tape, Telegram pulse snapshot, stablecoin sync (including returned fallback/abort outcomes), yield sync and DEWS compute (both including their own retention/prune DELETEs and early returned outcomes), and status self-check. Each tracker wraps the lane's existing D1 binding before its reads/writes and snapshots only after existing lane work settles; reserve adapter-context D1 bindings share the run's accumulator. Outer scheduler lease/progress/accounting writes remain excluded (`outer-cron-lifecycle-excluded`), and self-check also explicitly excludes D1 work behind external HTTP probes (`external-http-d1-excluded`); internal-router probe D1 work is included. These scopes therefore retain partial coverage rather than claiming whole-invocation billing. Thrown errors still propagate unchanged and do not acquire fabricated terminal cost snapshots. No extra awaits, SQL, per-query rows, logs or persistence calls are introduced.

#### Subgraph authority

`worker/src/cron/dex-liquidity/constants.ts` owns `SUBGRAPH_PER_CHAIN_TIMEOUT_MS`, schema/page/chain limits and `UNISWAP_V4_SOURCE_PROVENANCE`; git history owns repairs. The structured v6 changelog owns versioned 6.92/6.93 semantics, not operational repair diaries. Pass the chain deadline to transport for broad and exact pages; all pages/retries share it without widening global defaults/concurrency.

Failed V3 chains may use identity-only last-known-good candidates from `univ3-candidate-snapshot.ts` within `UNIV3_CANDIDATE_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_SEC`. Successful chains replace snapshots; carry never clears source failure, supplies old retained TVL/quotes/prices or overwrites live candidates. Readmission marks `carriedFromSnapshot`; the marker survives target construction and source staging. A carried spot cannot imply an output USD reference: independently admitted current token references are required, otherwise the target remains unresolved. Same-run candidates may still supply the existing spot-implied fallback. Rebuild targets from current retained TVL/references and quote live. Missing/stale/invalid/read/persist failures remain explicit. With no Graph key snapshots are untouched; prices are never carried.

Failed V4 chains carry the same way from `uniswap-v4-candidate-snapshot.ts` within `UNISWAP_V4_CANDIDATE_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_SEC` (7 days); a chain persists only when both its broad listing and exact reads answered. Exact pool ids a completed exact batch answered (present, absent or invalid) are current evidence and never readmitted from the snapshot. A carried V4 candidate builds only exact-pool-id targets: its stored TVL cannot satisfy the token/fee 2% affinity. Decisions are `uniswapV4CandidateCarryForward` in the stage header and `measuredTargetFunnel`. Every failed V3/V4 chain read is persisted as `subgraphFailures` (`source`, `chain`, `phase` broad/exact, `reason`, `timedOut`), so timeouts and bad answers are distinguishable from run metadata. An exact batch that fails with a GraphQL data error (not a timeout or transport error) is bisected sequentially, one request at a time, within `UNISWAP_V4_EXACT_BISECT_MAX_REQUESTS` extra requests per run and the chain deadline. Pools that still fail alone are excluded (`isolatedPoolIdCount` plus up to `UNISWAP_V4_EXACT_ISOLATED_ID_SAMPLE` ids) and the rest count as answered. They are isolated only if another part of the batch answered, so a source-wide error, an exhausted cap, a timeout or a transport failure still fails the chain.

BSC V3 stays execution-only (`UNIV3_EXECUTION_ONLY_CHAINS`), never fee enrichment or DEX price consensus. Separate recovery census retirement needs two source-exclusive accepted retained-pool generations, useful isolated loss/outage acceptance and mature publication, not aggregate TVL. Messari normalization requires ordered input tokens, exact fixed-fee conversion and decimal-adjusted tick spot; USD-reference valuation uses balances/spot where provider USD values are unreliable. Candidate TVL cannot replace retained target TVL.

V4 enrichment supplies identity, never USD TVL/reserves/price authority. Exact PoolKey hashing, independently retained TVL, pinned StateView/quoter/runtime and live execution remain mandatory; token/fee fallback retains 2% affinity. Logs expose provenance and bounded broad/exact failure classifications, never authenticated URLs. Protocol-inclusive fee mismatches cannot waive immutable hashes.

Uniswap `token0Price` is token0 per token1; `token1Price` is token1 per token0. With token1 USD reference, token0's USD price uses `token1Price`; constant-product denominators must follow that orientation. `DexApiPool.price` consistently means token[1] per token[0], consumed as `spotToken1PerToken0`.

### Additional Liquidity Metrics

- **Concentration HHI**: Herfindahl-Hirschman Index computed from the full retained pool set after filtering/caps but before top-10 display truncation. Range 0-1 (1.0 = single pool). Stored as `concentration_hhi`.
- **Depth Stability**: Coefficient of variation of daily TVL over 30-day rolling window, inverted to 0-1 scale. Requires >=7 days of data. Stored as `depth_stability`.
- **TVL Trends / DEWS anchors**: 24h and 7d trends use the nearest positive-TVL daily observation with `coverage_confidence >= 0.5` inside an inclusive tolerance (`12h` daily, `36h` weekly). Otherwise the API returns `null`. DEWS live hydration and historical reconstruction apply the same weekly admission, keeping score/TVL/date paired and both anchors null when no row qualifies; DEX query bounds cover the full 36h tolerance.
- **Depth Stability / Volume Consistency inputs**: durability history uses only snapshots with `coverage_confidence >= 0.75`; fewer than 7 confident rows fall back to neutral durability defaults.
- **Public evidence versus admission**: weak daily observations remain in the public history. A baseline admitted at confidence 0.5 need not be trendworthy at 0.75; digest pairs retain trendworthy evidence, methodology-basis and daily-continuity guards. No blanket promotion, source-shadow activation or archive relabeling follows from these consumer-specific thresholds.
- **Daily Snapshots**: One snapshot per active stablecoin per day in `dex_liquidity_history` (migration 0010, confidence fields added in 0061). A run reuses today's snapshot only when its active-ID set is exact, has no duplicate identities, and its scored-ID set covers the incoming active scored IDs. If coverage expands or the active universe changes, the writer preserves richer same-day scored rows, overlays new observations, and replaces the UTC date through one bounded atomic D1 batch (`DELETE` plus multi-row inserts), so a failed replacement leaves the prior date state intact. Successful DEX liquidity persistence also prunes history to the public 365-day window.

`shared/lib/classification/liquidity-concentration.ts` owns shared concentration/crowding thresholds and nonfinite fallback; consumers import, never restate a second table. Structured v6 history owns past threshold changes.

#### Price population and publication

Collapse physical pool prices once; canonical source families, not claimed protocol names, set confidence: DL/direct 1.0, CG/GT 0.85, DS/ticker/Horizon 0.55. Median weight is effective retained price-evidence TVL × confidence, or ticker observed flow × confidence. Load FX/gold/silver reference once per entrypoint and scale commodity ounces; nominal par is not a universal USD conversion.

`computeDexPrices()` requires the source owner's admitted primary map as a named argument and reads it without copying, mutating or loading the stablecoins cache. Missing keys remain intentional: outlier/deviation/display-ratio guards cannot run against null. Withhold `primary-missing` with bounded diagnostics/omitted count and warning. Ticker-only zero-TVL rows still pass every guard and clear no DEX-depth trust floor. Per-protocol aggregates populate `price_sources_json`; successful full publication retires old rows with no current admitted observations, not last-seen coverage.

Challengers come from full retained pools. Readers left-join each pointer/exact payload in one snapshot, preserving authoritative empty; concurrent cleanup cannot manufacture missing evidence. #### Consumer clocks and unavailable evidence

Retained prices keep the price-observing registry row's `refreshedAt` as `priceObservedAt`, independently of the selected TVL or volume row. Affirmative depeg source admission requires valid, nonfuture original clocks within the inclusive 35-minute family and 75-minute aggregate budgets; missing clocks cannot inherit the publication time. Observe an accepted clocked-writer generation before activating the strict reader. Conservative challenger vetoes do not establish fresh positive recovery.

Depeg evidence requires `DEX_FRESHNESS_SEC` (75 minutes) and ≥$1M aggregate source TVL. Trusted at-peg DEX may suppress a new primary depeg; missing/stale/confirming evidence follows primary detection. Pending/extreme/peak/recovery paths use their own confirmation rules; one contradictory row never auto-closes existing events.

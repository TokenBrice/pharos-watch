## Active Price Coverage Health

Price coverage is evaluated independently from active-row publication. `evaluateStablecoinActivePriceCoverage()` compares the final payload to the current active registry and counts a row as observed/priced only when `isObservedPrice()` admits its finite positive price. A valid published `nominalPriceReference` without an observed price is a separate reviewed category, not a missing gap: `nominalReferenceCount`, `nominalReferenceIds`, and `nominalReferenceMarketCapUsd` expose it with `nominalReferenceReason: "reviewed-nominal-reference"`. The nominal market-cap total is null if any constituent supply is unreadable. A trusted observed market quote takes precedence over its nominal sidecar for coverage. Rows with neither remain missing even though their supply and lifecycle data remain publishable. Nominal-only assets do not accumulate missing streaks, enter missing-price alerts or duration bands, or inflate observed-price counts. Status evidence records an informational `active_price_coverage_nominal_reference` cause; it is a standing notice (see [Status dashboard](../status-dashboard.md)), kept in `/api/status` causes without becoming a Triage item.

Every published main and CoinGecko-supply-fallback `sync-stablecoins` run writes `activePriceCoverage` into cron metadata with the expected, present, and priced active counts; exact priced and missing IDs; the positive raw USD circulating value affected; and per-gap price, source, observation-time, confidence, market-cap, consecutive-generation count, rejection class, and last accepted provenance. Compact streak state survives the cron metadata size guard. The producer compacts below 60 KiB before scheduler enrichment, reserving 4 KiB for lease and slot metadata so coverage remains top-level evidence in the persisted row. Because the compacted shape still grows with the missing-asset count, the guard walks an explicit degradation ladder — it first drops the attempt ledger's duplicate of the coverage missing-ID list, then verbose per-gap details, then attempt records, keeping exact ID sets, full compact streak state, counts, and truncation markers at every rung — and, when no rung fits a catalog that has outgrown the budget, fails closed with a scalar-only envelope marked `sizeGuardEvidenceDropped` instead of letting the global 64 KiB persistence compaction destroy the evidence unmarked. A gap does not downgrade a successfully completed cron execution or block the otherwise valid `stablecoins` cache; its warning eligibility is governed by the persistence gate described below and by the price-gap review registry. A gap may be acknowledged by a dated, owned, expiring review (`STABLECOIN_PRICE_GAP_REVIEWS` in `worker/src/lib/stablecoin-publication-coverage.ts`): the gap still publishes as missing in every count and ID list, but it is never alert-eligible while the review is valid. A review dated after the evaluation clock is invalid (`invalidGapReviewIds`) and acknowledges nothing. Acknowledgement is recomputed from the registry on every evaluation and every read — never persisted as truth — so adding, renewing, removing, or expiring a review takes effect immediately, and an expiry re-arms the alert even before the next producer run. Publication omission waivers are a separate mechanism (`STABLECOIN_PUBLICATION_WAIVERS`); acknowledgement never waives an active row, and never applies to a depeg or to an asset that has a price.

Size-guard compaction drops `priceSourceHealth`, including its `supplyCoverage` counts. Consumers must treat missing exposure coverage as unverified, never as complete or observed zero.

`/api/health` reads this state from the latest `sync-stablecoins` cron row. It reports `complete` only when the recorded registry size, disjoint observed-priced plus reviewed-nominal ID sets, and zero-gap counts all match the current active registry. Missing metadata is `unknown` and degrades public health. An incomplete assessment emits `active-price-coverage-incomplete` only for unacknowledged, alert-eligible IDs: a measured streak must reach `ACTIVE_PRICE_COVERAGE_ALERT_GENERATIONS` (two), while unknown continuity is conservatively alert-eligible. Gaps are warning-only (an operator Watch item) whatever their duration or market cap; public health degrades only when coverage is unknown or when missing active IDs reach the shared breadth threshold, ⌈5% × expected active⌉ (`getStablecoinPublicationServiceBreadthThreshold` in `shared/lib/status-thresholds.ts`). The critical band (one week) still adds the `active-price-coverage-critical-duration` warning for every unacknowledged alert-eligible gap; it does not change public status. Valid acknowledgements suppress these gap warnings, not missing counts. The JSON payload retains exact counts and IDs, acknowledgement owner/reason/sources/expiry, and expired/invalid review IDs. The admin `/api/status` data-quality layer records coverage, ratio, and duration causes independently; without an alert-eligible unacknowledged gap, its coverage cause is informational. Review acknowledgement is recomputed at read time, so expiry re-arms alerts before another producer run. Both publication paths persist per-asset ID, symbol, rejection class, streak, and last accepted source/time in `activePriceCoverage`; there is no separate cron-event or webhook delivery today. Triage uses `/api/health`, `/api/status`, and the cron metadata. `activePublicationCoverage` separately answers whether active rows are present.

Reviewed source market hours are a separate category from acknowledgements ([ADR-43](../architecture.md#adr-43)). `STABLECOIN_MARKET_HOURS_CALENDARS` owns the exact Mento asset/lane scope and annual source-review deadline. Missing CADm/AUDm/COPm (`mento-broker`) and CHFm/JPYm (`mento-fpmm`) enter `scheduledClosureGapIds` with per-gap `scheduledClosure.reason: "market-closed"` and owner, source URLs, review date and deadline during Friday 21:00–Sunday 23:00 UTC, December 24/31 from 22:00 UTC, December 25 and January 1. Closures are start-inclusive/end-exclusive, with no DST or substitute holidays. After the most recent actual closure end, eligibility waits `ACTIVE_PRICE_COVERAGE_ALERT_GENERATIONS × CRON_INTERVALS["sync-stablecoins"]` (currently 30 minutes), then uses the ordinary missing-generation gate; this deterministic equivalent excludes closure-only generations without rewriting measured streaks. These prices remain missing and their streaks advance; closures suppress operator alert eligibility, never the public breadth count or a depeg. Priced assets enter neither missing category. Applicability is recomputed at write and read time, even from compact legacy state. Future, malformed or overdue calendar reviews suppress nothing and publish `invalidMarketHoursCalendarIds` or `expiredMarketHoursCalendarIds`, with admin-only `price_market_hours_calendars_invalid` / `price_market_hours_calendars_expired` info causes. Off-calendar gaps use the unchanged persistence gate; the calendar does not emit short-lived price-review expiry/reminder causes. Revalidate source rules and exact lane coverage at the annual deadline under [review renewal](../runbooks/review-renewal.md#market-hours-calendars).

In-use price-gap acknowledgements emit the admin-only info cause `price_gap_reviews_expiring` in their final 48 hours (inclusive, `STATUS_REVIEW_EXPIRY_REMINDER_WINDOW_SEC` in `shared/lib/status-thresholds.ts`), linking to [review renewal](../runbooks/review-renewal.md) without changing price admission, public health, or status transitions.

An acknowledgement does not establish that a rejected or dislocated quote is harmless. A bounded review can record unresolved venue divergence (including HBD's native/centralized-market disagreement) only while retaining the rejection, missing-price state, source-depth requirements and explicit owner decision deadline; it cannot admit the rejected quote, conversion parity or an under-depth alternative source.

Continuity reads distinguish an absent prior generation from a failed read or malformed prior metadata. Only successful absence starts a new missing streak at one. Read failures persist `consecutiveMissingGenerations: null` with `streakUnavailableReason` (`previous-coverage-read-failed` or `previous-coverage-malformed`), including in compact state; subsequent missing generations retain that uncertainty until a priced generation establishes a new boundary. Current measured counts remain available. Unknown continuity conservatively makes unacknowledged missing assets alert-eligible and projects escalation priority into the provider queues, without treating that priority as a measured streak. Valid acknowledgements still suppress escalation. Material gaps with unknown duration retain a degraded warning but never claim a week-long critical duration. The shared `ActivePriceCoverageHealthSchema` owns the public contract; missing, failed, or malformed current coverage reads publish null counts, affected market cap and maximum streak with `unavailableReason`, never synthetic zero.

Compact tuples (including legacy six-slot entries) project into the verbose detail decoder: compact state has no current quote and uses the registry symbol. Both readers build the same compact-first detail map, with later duplicate entries winning and verbose entries overriding compact ones. Schema admission and exact missing-ID selection remain reader-specific: continuity requires every selected detail, while health tolerates absent details and falls back to detail-map IDs only when the missing-ID list is empty. Neither path trusts persisted acknowledgement decisions.

Absent rows retain diagnostic supply evidence, not a synthetic current cap or price: `lastKnownMarketCapUsd`, its original `lastKnownMarketCapObservedAt`, and `lastKnownMarketCapSource` (`publication` or `supply_history`) survive compact continuity. Both publication paths seed from the already-loaded previous published cache row (`supplyObservedAt`), then prior gap evidence; legacy prior current-cap evidence uses the prior cron's publication clock. If an absent ID still has no usable evidence, the producer makes one bounded read for those IDs against daily `supply_history.circulating_usd`, with `snapshot_date` as the observation clock. It does not multiply DefiLlama USD circulating supply by price, perform another outbound fetch, or add a D1 query to `/api/health`.

Publication incompleteness degrades only for unknown coverage, an unnamed count mismatch, or missing IDs reaching 5% of the expected active cohort (`getActiveAssetServiceBreadthThreshold`; 20 of 396); market cap plays no part since the 2026-10-21 retirement of the $1B rule. Every incomplete publication keeps the unchanged `stablecoin-publication-incomplete:<ids>` warning and payload. Retained cap evidence is admitted for `STATUS_LAST_KNOWN_MARKET_CAP_MAX_AGE_SEC` (30 days inclusive) as diagnostic payload; the original clock is carried, never renewed by a missing generation. This changes severity only: missing IDs, streaks, acknowledgements and critical-duration warnings stay visible.

Independent Kinesis supply verification does not renew stock from a successful numeric response alone: declared current-ledger clocks pass the shared on-chain freshness budget before admission. A refused clock preserves the prior accepted row so status naturally ages it; an old last-change record alone is not a freshness refusal. The [onchain_supply contract](../supply-snapshot.md#onchain_supply) owns ledger-clock reasons and the explicit undated legacy policy; these checks do not establish observed price coverage.

## Primary Consensus

### Native Hive order book

`worker/src/lib/hive-market-price.ts` owns `HIVE_MARKET_POLICY` (generation 1); `shared/types/hive-market-price.ts` owns the packet and rejection vocabulary. The launch policy is owner-approved, not statistically calibrated for continuous availability. The source is an ordinary primary observation, never a witness-feed/conversion-price override.

- Read `condenser_api.get_dynamic_global_properties` → `get_order_book([500])` → `get_ticker([])` → `get_recent_trades([1000])` → head serially. One node supplies the entire sample. `api.hive.blog` precedes one `api.deathwing.me` failover for transport, malformed-response or stale-head faults only. No Dwellir Hive market capability is established; no third node or cross-node evidence join is admitted.
- Requests use Workers-supported `redirect: "manual"`; the shared fetch helper rejects non-2xx responses and cancels their bodies before failover, so redirects never add an unapproved node or physical call. Workers rejects the Fetch-standard `"error"` mode during request construction, before any node is contacted.
- Per-call headers/body deadline is three seconds, whole observer allowance twenty seconds under inherited cancellation/optional absolute deadline, maximum ten physical calls and one MiB per response. Sample duration must be at most thirty seconds; heads must be non-regressing, at most sixty seconds old and at most fifteen seconds future-dated. This is bounded moving-state evidence, not an atomic book.
- `real_price` is decimal HBD/HIVE on both sides; validate against the HBD-base bid/HIVE-base ask orientation. Depth sums remaining integer `hbd` milliunits once, never original `order_price` inventory or both equivalent legs. Spread must be at most 1%; each side must prove 500 HBD inside the inclusive ±2% midpoint band. A still-open band at the 500-row cap below the floor is `depth_unproven`; a completed below-floor side is `insufficient_depth`.
- Chain dates parse as UTC. Count the HBD payment leg once, exclude self-trades and count distinct full fill tuples, not distinct timestamps. The API has no fill ID: identical tuples retain an ambiguity count and contribute once to the conservative lower bound, not fabricated independent turnover. The inclusive trailing-day lower bound must reach 1,000 HBD; a truncated/duplicate-ambiguous below-floor window is `volume_unproven`. Latest non-self trade must be within fifteen minutes and within 2% of midpoint. Ticker volume is diagnostic only: no equality tolerance can make separate-block/self-trade-inclusive reads substitute for the required proof.
- Require the current invocation's exact `hive` CG/USD parent, finite/positive with explicit upstream time, signed age at most 300 seconds and future skew at most sixty seconds. Recheck at composition. Missing/stale dependency gives `hive_usd_unavailable` / `hive_usd_stale`, never nominal USD/USDT parity. Effective `local_fetch` time is `min(bookRetrievedAt, parent.observedAt)`; ordinary source freshness caps age at 900 seconds.
- Operator `providerDiagnostics[].nativeMarket` and top-level `nativeMarket` preserve source/node/methods, head/book clocks, depth/completeness, trade age/volume, parent, formula, policy/generation and rejection reason on success as well as failure. Size-guard rungs retain the bounded packet; the terminal envelope explicitly marks evidence omitted. No raw arrays/credentials persist. One aggregate Hive circuit outcome follows a physical attempt; valid shallow-market rejection is not transport failure and does not poison CG.

The shared rejection schema additionally names RPC timeout/error, rate limit, invalid response, stale head, expired sample, empty/crossed book, excess spread, insufficient volume, stale/divergent trade, abort and deadline. Registration or acknowledgement never clears missing-price coverage. The native observer only prices an admitted supplemental candidate: HBD intake uses the reserve adapter's two-node bracketed native ex-treasury supply proof, independently of CoinGecko's cap. The [HBD supply contract](../supply-snapshot.md#supply-pipeline) defines its perimeter, valuation and bounded restoration on failure. Supply intake completes before the unchanged native market observer, so admitted supply and a native quote can publish in the same generation even when CoinGecko reports zero cap. Its expired gap review was retired without renewal; HBD stays unacknowledged and alert-eligible until admitted supply and a native quote publish in the same generation.

Production acceptance joins deployed Worker version, run/generation, packet and HBD row for the first **public** `sync-stablecoins` execution and the next 24 hours. Recompute contemporaneous cross-rates, verify every gate and conservative confidence/clocks, measure admission versus transport success/rejections, and verify unchanged 4/6 peak, bounded duration/body cleanup and no new abandonment/CPU-memory regression. Capacity and rollback authority: [ADR-41](../architecture.md#adr-41). No migration or historical backfill.



### Reused Solomon provider identity

Provider IDs are not instrument identities. Legacy `usdv-solomon` uses its exact Solana mint through fail-closed supplemental `getTokenSupply`, with no `llamaId` or `geckoId`; the replacement alone owns recycled provider IDs. `worker/src/lib/solomon-usdv-identity.ts` rejects aliased DefiLlama list/contract-price families, unknown/cached provenance and contaminated agreement lineage in publication/replay; intake clears contaminated prices and cached detail bodies are sanitized before canonical enrichment. Exact-mint market quotes still need ordinary admission. Distinct mint decimals, custody/reserves and local history stay instrument-specific; no guessed migration date partitions mixed provider history.

The replacement `usdv-solomon-v2` alone owns `geckoId: solomon-usdv` and uses the existing CoinGecko supplemental current-price/market-cap path. The recycled provider history is current-only: CoinGecko market-history, direct price backfill, depeg-history audit, hourly price history, caller-provided history seeds and their DefiLlama proxy history are withheld. New detail charts use local supply/cache history, and forward local collection continues. No historical rows are deleted and no guessed migration date divides the mixed provider series.

### Challenger storage views

The DEX bridge and the pool challenge now deliberately read from different storage views:

- `dex_prices.price_sources_json`: one aggregate per protocol, used for primary-price promotion
- `dex_price_challenger_heads_v2` + publication-keyed `dex_price_challenger_payloads_v2`: accepted immutable individual challenger membership, selected from the full retained DEX pool set with protocol-first diversity and bounded price-evidence TVL coverage. Retained v1 snapshots are read only before a coin has a v2 head; old tables otherwise remain rollback storage.
- `dex_liquidity.challenger_pools_json`: the same TVL-bounded challenger projection stored with each publication for the legacy fallback; `NULL` on rows written before it existed
- `dex_liquidity.top_pools_json`: display-oriented top pools for UI detail, no longer the canonical challenger source; the legacy fallback reads it only when a row has no challenger projection

## Provider-Specific Normalization

Primary provider implementations are normalized before their prices can enter the active 15-minute `sync-stablecoins` consensus. The exact-address rule applies only to hourly corroboration.

- **Source freshness gate:** primary candidate admission runs every timestamped source through a registry-backed freshness check before the source can enter consensus. The gate enforces each source's `maxTrustedAgeSec`, required observed-at metadata, default observed-at mode, and a 10-minute future-skew ceiling. This is the final shared admission check on top of provider-local stale filtering.
- **CoinGecko simple-price freshness:** `/simple/price` requests `last_updated_at`; when CoinGecko supplies it, rows older than the source trust window are rejected before consensus instead of being stamped as fresh local fetches. If the field is absent despite the request, the row can still enter as local-fetch provenance for backwards compatibility with partial responses. A malformed entry is quarantined per key; healthy siblings from the same batch continue.
- **CoinGecko simple-price precision:** every Worker `/simple/price` request uses `precision=full`, including primary, supplemental, native-fiat, confirmation, status, and auxiliary pricing paths. Threshold decisions therefore receive CoinGecko's unrounded value instead of a display-rounded quote.
- **Supplemental detail providers:** `detailProvider: "coingecko"` assets admit a fresh CoinGecko price and aggregate market cap through the existing supplemental lane even when the DefiLlama live list lacks the row; the retained `llamaId` preserves provider identity, and when an admitted list row exists its primary supply wins over a duplicate supplemental row. NAV/yield-bearing detail-provider assets may observe prices above one dollar; no nominal-par valuation or bridge-deployment supply sum is introduced.
- **CEX identity and clocks:** `BINANCE_MARKETS`, `KRAKEN_MARKETS`, `BITSTAMP_MARKETS` and `COINBASE_PRODUCTS` in `shared/lib/pricing-provider-config.ts` own membership; `worker/src/lib/cex-tickers.ts` owns explicit request/response aliases. Never infer identity by slicing symbols. Coinbase HONEY is Hivemapper, not Berachain's token; reviewed AUDD-USDC binds exact Novatti identity and treats Coinbase-convertible USDC at par, and XSGD-USDC follows that FX-stablecoin precedent with Coinbase's XSGD asset pinning the tracked StraitsX contract. Binance publishes configured direct-USD marks without tracked quote conversions and suppresses overlapping aggregate DEX evidence.
- **CEX capability:** registry metadata distinguishes Binance last-trade marks from bid/ask-derived Kraken/Bitstamp/Coinbase spots. Kraken uses local-fetch time; nonempty API `error` is failure. Bitstamp/Coinbase keep upstream per-pair clocks and reject missing, invalid, stale or future times. Quiet last-trade books never receive fresh fetch-time provenance. Bitstamp PYUSD/USD is not configured while its listed book is untraded and publishes only a placeholder bid/ask.
- **Kraken exact markets:** SoFiUSD's `SOFIDUSD`, Frontier Stable Token's `FRNTUSD`, CASH's `CASHUSD` and USDGO's `USDGOUSD` are curated bid/ask markets in the shared batched ticker request with local-fetch clocks. CoinGecko ticker sets (`sofiusd`, `frontier-stable-token` for the Wyoming token, `cash-4`, `usdgo`) identify these markets, and the CASH/USDGO uppercase symbols each have one tracked owner, so those joins are identity-safe. The 2026-10-30 additions `ZCHFUSD`, `USDSMUSD`, `USDSUIUSD`, `USDPTUSD`, `EUROPUSD`, `QCADUSD`, `AUDXUSD` and `BRL1USD` follow the same rule: each market's CoinGecko ticker set names the tracked coin and every uppercase symbol has one tracked owner. A stale last-trade quote does not become fresh merely because the book remains quoted. Failed or absent responses stay unavailable rather than publishing par; the issuer's dollar redemption target and reserve balances never substitute for an observed price.
- **Jupiter V3:** sparse no-quote responses are healthy empty coverage. Quoted rows require `usdPrice`, decimals and a block ID checked against a fresh sequentially obtained Solana slot reference; complete reference failure rejects. Optional liquidity adds a guard only when present; `createdAt` is not freshness.
- **FX/metals reference admission:** admitted FX is a validation dependency, never a market quote or proof of peg stability. Generation integrity, cadence, TTL, overlay/fallback and historical references are owned by [FX and metals references](worker-infrastructure-appendix.md#fx-and-metals-references).
- **Native-peg prices:** supported direct CoinGecko native pairs may derive `native quote × admitted FX` USD marks for missing or materially divergent weak/mixed publications. Unsupported pairs retain secondary FX/bounds, never invented native quotes. `coingecko-native-implied` is fallback-validation evidence, not replay-safe primary evidence or `price_cache` continuity. Historical native-fiat replay compares against native `1.0`, with daily points and two-point confirmation across 36 hours before USD-history fallback.
- **Peg-aware bounds:** validation policy owns numeric sanity bands. USD/fiat FX share the `1.19 × reference` upside limit; metals retain `2 × reference` and fractional `commodityOunces` scaling. Taxonomy/no-reference bands never supply prices. Authoritative downside relaxations stay scoped.
- **No inline GT probe:** its disabled heap-boundary marker is provenance only; a new probe needs a separately budgeted producer. Retired identities remain for historical rendering, not live execution.
- **Availability versus admission:** Successful CoinGecko ticker transport with stale/unusable rows does not fail its availability breaker, although quotes reject. DexScreener discovery records aggregate success if any request succeeded; later hard refusal stops work without erasing success, while zero-success refusal fails.
- **Curve state:** `worker/src/lib/curve-onchain.ts` sizes `get_dy` so floored output spans at least 10,000 quanta, avoiding low-decimal bias; implied-price sanity stays `< 10,000`. Quotes share one numbered block and its timestamp; state older than five minutes rejects. Direct/one-hop/trusted-wrapper/explicit-chained routes resolve the via asset's USD value, never par; dependency/shape errors and cycles reject, and chaining needs explicit opt-in/depth. The crvUSD oracle independently pins time and uses its own circuit.
- **Curve USDT cross-rates:** USAT, Quantoz USDQ and USDx depend on the existing USDT/USDC quote rather than treating USDT-denominated ratios as USD. USD3's, DUSD's and trUSD's frxUSD pools reuse the existing frxUSD route, reUSD's fxUSD pool reuses the existing fxUSD route, scrvUSD chains through reUSD, and MUSD's 2pool metapool reads `get_dy_underlying` against its USDC underlying. Each hop requires an admitted dependency and propagates the oldest contributing block timestamp; no new fetch lane or par substitution is introduced. The 2026-10-31 depth sweep removed the Spark sUSDS/USDT (~$1.2k TVL) and PYUSD/USDS (~$459) routes and their now-unresolvable dependent hops (sUSDS, USDS, DOLA, stUSDS, sUSDe); the registry's `>$1M TVL` rule is pinned by a measured-pool test.
- **Reserve NAV:** the [primary contract](../pricing-pipeline.md#primary-consensus) and `worker/src/lib/reserve-nav-price.ts` own matched successful configuration/snapshot and independent fetch/source-age admission. Non-USD NAV needs trusted FX; adapter identity alone is no quote.
- **Exact-address trust:** retained CoinGecko Onchain uses canonical chain/address targets, including reviewed hints, never symbol search. `ADDRESS_PRICE_PROVIDERS_ENABLED` is explicit (unset enables nothing), and the provider also needs its API key. VUSD's override stays metadata/network-bound. Unreviewed targets need parseable liquidity at $50K. Corroboration adds bounded weight only on agreement, never replaces current prices or independently authorizes depegs. [Fallback Enrichment](#fallback-enrichment) owns refresh/hint/source-age behavior.
- **Binance availability:** mirror-to-main fallback short-circuits answered 5xx/429/403/451 rather than retrying a refusing host. All-host 403/451 records a six-hour environment block then one probe: no contribution, not source outage. Network exceptions remain failures. One invocation promise serves consensus/confirmation; successful probes clear the block.
- **DEX independence:** registries own promoted membership; each protocol contributes at most one elevated observation. Any promoted candidate suppresses the overlapping aggregate even if all promoted candidates reject. Lone protocols need matching hard evidence; independent protocols need candidate-scoped agreement. One protocol on multiple chains is not independence.
- **DEX aggregate:** absent promoted candidates/Binance overlap, it stays soft under its $1M gate (reviewed VUSD may use $250K, never depeg authority). Beside another primary leg it obeys `DEPEG_PRIMARY_PRICE_MAX_AGE_SEC`; alone it retains row eligibility and its own clock. Consensus uses the oldest agreeing observation. Each protocol's stored time is checked independently; row time is only legacy fallback, never permission for a fresh parent to hide stale/future lanes.
- **Dataset versus rows:** `CACHE_FRESHNESS_LANES.dexLiquidity.endpointMaxAgeSec` owns `dex_pricing_bridge_stale`, distinct from `DEX_FRESHNESS_SEC` row admission. Structured exclusions distinguish stale/malformed/missing-registry/below-floor/uncorroborated candidates; `priceSourceConfidenceProfile` explains reliance.
- **Price-only tickers:** exact non-stale/non-anomalous USD-equivalent exchange marks need positive finite price/flow, $1,000 flow, non-future evidence and the 24-hour registry window. Weight is flow × confidence (0.55); pools use real TVL × confidence. Null TVL/no pool model and ticker-only zero-TVL publication establish no liquidity, challenger, execution or elevated trust. `source_pool_count` counts observations; primary/peg/outlier/display guards still apply.
- **Retained surface/clocks:** genuine pool prices come only from final deduped/capped/scored pools. Direct evidence for a retained unpriced pool needs canonical identity and $50K, with weight capped by smaller evidence/retained TVL. Exact unique-derived identity keeps precedence at zero volume. Future registry rows reject whole at post-read `registryEvaluatedAtSec`, carried through publication/reuse; publication never refreshes original observations.
- **Acquisition/merge:** the [DEX owner](../dex-liquidity.md) owns serial bounded pagination, partial-source outcomes, unit normalization and dedupe. Addressed unknown tokens never fall back to symbol; addressless matching must be chain-unique. Diagnostics retain accepted lanes/exclusions rather than silently truncating under sort drift.
- **Quote dependencies:** fresh authoritative, high-confidence multi-family or guarded NAV tracked prices precede peg references. Weak/stale or unknown addressed dollar-like symbols get no unconditional par. NAV tokens require `isGuardedNavReferencePrice`, never generic market/peg substitution; non-USD counterparties need peg-aware conversion.
- **Coherence/publication:** `POOL_PRICE_COHERENCE_POLICY` in `worker/src/cron/dex-liquidity/pool-price-coherence.ts` rejects whole pools for incoherent pair-ratio/counter-leg USD or absent ratio despite USD fields. Rejected pools provide no TVL, price or challenger; zero volume/transactions alone never reject. Primary-anchored publication without usable primary withholds as `primary-missing`, never compares against null. [DEX Liquidity](../dex-liquidity.md#discovery-cron) owns acquisition/coherence detail.

Source configuration owns exact membership, capabilities and request limits; this is not a second provider inventory.

## Authoritative Overrides

After market/oracle consensus, the provider registry under `worker/src/lib/authoritative-price-sources/` can replace or recover the chosen live price for source-reviewed assets whose executable value is better represented by direct protocol redemption, an instantly redeemable tracked base asset, a protocol-native oracle, or an identity-bound exact market route.

### Current Scope

`AUTHORITATIVE_PRICE_PROVIDERS` in `worker/src/lib/authoritative-price-sources/index.ts` owns the current route roster. `ERC4626_NAV_VAULTS` in `erc4626-nav.ts` owns reviewed share/parent bindings; `INHERITED_TRACKED_PRICE_CONFIGS` in `inherited-tracked.ts` owns parent edges and scoped opt-ins. Consult those registries rather than a copied asset table.

Route families are deliberately distinct: ERC-4626/Aave/Idle NAV and direct redemption resolve executable parent-denominated value; inherited wrappers and issuer conversions reuse explicitly trusted tracked parents; nominal references are unobserved. Exact market recovery and protocol accrual retain their own trust classes. Kava (`kava-pricefeed.ts`), Mento (`mento-fpmm.ts`, `mento-broker.ts`), BD (`bd-aerodrome.ts`), USDaf (`usdaf-uniswap-v4.ts`), USDU (`usdu-uniswap-v3.ts`), AZND (`aznd-curve-pool.ts`), Solayer (`susd-solayer.ts`) and dEURO (`deuro-eurc-bridge.ts`) own the reviewed identity, code-pin and execution checks described below; none is implied by the vault/parent registries.

When a live override validates successfully, direct protocol/NAV quotes and high-confidence tracked-base inheritance are written with:

- `priceSource = "protocol-redeem"`
- `priceConfidence = "high"`

Nominal par routes are the exception: they never publish `protocol-redeem` provenance (see the nominal par references below).

Scoped tracked-base inheritance from a fresh replay-safe single-source parent keeps the parent `priceSource` and
`priceConfidence = "single-source"` so downstream publication guardrails continue to see the inherited quote's soft
upstream provenance instead of treating it as depeg-authoritative protocol redemption.

Vault NAV routes (ERC-4626 `convertToAssets`, Aave `previewRedeem`, Idle CDO `virtualPrice`) also persist their
last-good assets-per-share rate to the durable `authoritative_vault_rates` table on every successful live read. When the

**Precedence.** `applyProtocolPriceOverrides()` sets `nominalPriceReference: { price, source: "protocol-par", mode: "nominal_reference" }` (plus the FX fields for non-USD par) on each routed asset and publishes it as the price only when the incumbent is not a trusted market quote. `isTrustedMarketQuote()` admits the incumbent only when the depeg detector would act on it without confirmation — `classifyPrimaryDepegTrust()` returns `authoritative` — and it has no protocol-override provenance. That requires an observed price within `DEPEG_PRIMARY_PRICE_MAX_AGE_SEC` that is not cached, fallback or `low` confidence, plus registry source authority: at `high` confidence two depeg-authoritative sources or one depeg-authoritative source with upstream observation time, and at `single-source` a source registered as single-source depeg-authoritative with an upstream observation time. Soft aggregators (CoinGecko, DefiLlama, CoinMarketCap) are never depeg-authoritative, so a thin single mark or CoinGecko + DefiLlama list agreement at `high` confidence cannot displace par. The gate applies no liquidity or venue-depth threshold of its own: trust comes from the registry source class (hard CEX, oracle and on-chain protocol sources are depeg-authoritative; aggregator and DEX lanes are not), consensus confidence and freshness. A trusted market discount is therefore published as the observed price with par kept alongside, while soft-aggregator marks, stale quotes, cached/fallback fills and legacy `protocol-redeem` par rows never displace par. `settleNominalPriceReferences()` re-applies the rule after post-enrichment validation and cached fallback, so a market quote rejected late falls back to par rather than to a fallback mark. Precedence is re-decided every run without hysteresis.

**Nominal publication.** Without a trusted market quote the row publishes `price` = par, `priceSource = "protocol-par"`, `priceObservedAtMode = "nominal_reference"`, `priceConfidence`, `priceObservedAt` and `priceUpdatedAt` null, empty consensus/agree sources, and `priceSyncedAt` = the publishing sync. Post-enrichment observation validation skips nominal rows (par was validated when applied). References and nominal prices are derived every run and never carried forward: a restored or previous row loses them unless this run's route produced par, so a non-USD route without a usable FX reference publishes neither. Nominal evidence is excluded from depeg observations and confirmation, V9 price facts, NAV captures, observed-price coverage (nominal-only assets are counted in the separate `nominalReference*` coverage category, not as missing — see the coverage paragraph above), comparison/export prices, peer references and the replay `price_cache`. Readers label it “Nominal par reference (not an observed price)”, without observation freshness or observed-price confidence. Rows published before v6.38 keep their `protocol-redeem` provenance.

**Legacy replay rows.** Pre-6.38 `protocol-redeem` par rows in `price_cache` are never rewritten, because par is never staged; they remain in the table indefinitely and are not deleted. The cached-fallback lane admits a cached row only inside its source's registry budget measured from the row's write clock (`protocol-redeem`: 15 minutes), so such a row can be republished — labelled `cached` / `fallback`, and only for an asset that has no price and no nominal reference that run — for at most 15 minutes after its last pre-cutover write, and never afterwards. Other readers of `price_cache` apply their own age rules.

**Replay.** Every configured route resolves historical replay to `protocol-par` with no series, so depeg backfill preserves existing event rows instead of synthesizing USD 1.0 history or replaying unadmitted market history. `usdk-kast` and `usdn-noble` are the exception by configuration, not by par: their tracked-parent provider still replays the reviewed wM/M market series, so only their live missing-price gap resolves to par.

The early pass keeps known redeemable wrappers and extension assets out of unnecessary fallback-source work, while the final pass preserves the rule that fallback enrichment cannot overwrite a validated redemption price. This lets a child such as `usdn-noble` see a same-run `m-m0` repair without repeating any RPC-backed vault or redemption calls.

## Fallback Enrichment

CoinMarketCap fallback requires the configured exact slug in its complete category response, without a symbol-equality gate on that slug; only slugless assets may use a symbol unique both locally and in the provider response. An absent configured slug stays eligible for bounded targeted validation, never a different same-symbol category token. This section owns targeted chain/address identity and verified-cache admission.

The hourly observation handoff expires after one hour plus one 15-minute publication interval, providing a bounded handoff envelope across hourly replacement. ### CoinGecko low-volume lane


Some tracked stablecoins trade at low enough volume that CoinGecko's upstream `last_updated_at` for the ticker sits hours-to-days behind real time. The strict 15-minute freshness gates used elsewhere reject these prices, leaving the assets with `priceSource: "missing"` even though CoinGecko has a valid USD quote.

`fetchFiatCoinGeckoTokens` in `worker/src/cron/sync-stablecoins/supplemental-assets/fiat-cg.ts` runs a relaxed fallback for CoinGecko-only supplemental assets:

1. Try `resolveSupplementalPrice` first (the standard 15-minute gate).
2. If there is no `geckoId` but the asset has one unambiguous supported supply contract, try the same DefiLlama coins endpoint with an exact `chain:contract` key. Accepted quotes must include a matching DefiLlama symbol, confidence of at least `0.8`, a fresh upstream timestamp, and pass the shared peg-aware reasonableness bounds before publishing as `source: "defillama-contract"` or normalizing the same on-chain total-supply fallback.
3. If that returns null but `cgData[geckoId].usd` is a positive finite number, build a resolution with `source: "coingecko-low-volume"`, `priceConfidence: "fallback"`, and `priceObservedAtMode: "upstream"` when CG returned `last_updated_at` (otherwise `"local_fetch"`).
4. NAV/yield-bearing assets (`flags.navToken || flags.yieldBearing`) are never par-valued. An admitted reserve NAV
   may use trusted FX conversion for its denomination; an FX reference alone never values supply. When all three
   market lanes return null, an asset with a registered vault NAV route
   resolves a supply-valuation-only price through `resolveVaultNavSupplyPrice`
   (`worker/src/lib/authoritative-price-sources/erc4626-nav.ts`): the exact protocol-redeem route — parent-trust gate
   on a run-local parent row (the parent source's own freshness budget, 900 seconds for CoinGecko), live `convertToAssets(1 share)` read,
   and the bounded 24-hour `protocol-redeem-cached-rate` degradation lane — reused pre-intake. The resolved price
   values on-chain total supply only; the row publishes with `price: null` and the live override stage re-prices it in
   the same run. Without a trusted NAV the asset stays out of the payload (fail-closed). For vaults whose config admits
   one fresh replay-safe single-source parent (`getFreshSingleSourceNavParentId`, including syzUSD → yzUSD and sBOLD/yBOLD → BOLD), the existing
   CoinGecko batch also requests the DefiLlama-owned parent; a positive, reasonable direct `coingecko` quote with a
   fresh upstream clock becomes an unpublished `single-source` parent stub that takes precedence over the previous
   payload's row. Mirror, low-volume and par values never qualify, and the trust gate is unchanged.
5. Assets configured for a registered reserve NAV adapter can also use a matched successful snapshot through
   `loadReserveNavSupplyPrice` (`worker/src/lib/reserve-nav-price.ts`) before they have a previous cache row.
   The decoder checks fetch age and source age independently; positive native supply is valued at observed NAV,
   with trusted FX conversion for non-USD classes. Primary reserve-NAV consensus supplies the published live price.

`LOW_VOLUME_CG_FALLBACK_IDS` in `worker/src/cron/sync-stablecoins/enrich-prices-coingecko-low-volume-pass.ts` owns the reviewed fallback cohort, including USDFC. It fills only still-missing prices after earlier passes, preserves admitted supply and original clocks, and never overwrites an accepted price.

Timestamped low-volume fallback quotes must pass `isFreshFallbackObservedAt`: the registered seven-day age budget and at most 120 seconds of future skew. Present invalid clocks are rejected, not converted to local fetch time; only absent upstream clocks retain local-fetch behavior.


HBD's low-volume recovery preserves the real CoinGecko observation clock and carried supply. TRYB's expired observations and below-floor venues do not become admissible through a price-gap acknowledgement; a genuinely newer observation may recover through the existing seven-day lane without a review. `STABLECOIN_PRICE_GAP_REVIEWS` in `worker/src/lib/stablecoin-publication-coverage.ts` owns any applicable acknowledgement and expiry, not a permanent TRYB exception: a valid review suppresses alert eligibility only, an admitted quote clears the gap, and obsolete expired reviews are removed after verified recovery. Neither the TRY FX reference nor Hive's conversion peg is substituted for a market price.

The lane is registered in `shared/lib/pricing-source-registry-aggregators.ts` with a 7-day `maxTrustedAgeSec` and `defaultWeight: 0.5`. Downstream treatment is intentionally weaker than primary `coingecko`:

- `priceConfidence: "fallback"` flows into `priceValidationModeForAsset → "fallback_enrichment"` and `classifyPrimaryDepegTrust → "confirm_required"`, so the low-volume lane publishes for display but cannot single-handedly open, extend, or confirm a depeg alert.
- The new source belongs to the same CG lineage family in `worker/src/lib/price-publish-policy.ts` and `worker/src/lib/depeg-trust-policy.ts`, so severe-downside corroboration still requires an independent non-CG source.
- Hourly fallback recoveries retain `priceConfidence: "fallback"` when staged in `price_cache`; every later publication revalidates the candidate before it can fill a missing row, and it remains non-depeg-authoritative.

Retained supplemental quotes preserve existing fallback confidence when no primary candidate replaces them. A retained `coingecko-low-volume` quote always stamps fallback confidence, including a previous generation whose confidence was incorrectly promoted; stamping the current publication generation does not promote a low-volume observation to single-source confidence.

Strict primary CoinGecko admission everywhere else is untouched.

### Zephyr Scanner supplemental lane

`zsd-zephyr-protocol` and `zys-zephyr-protocol` are native Zephyr-chain assets, so Pharos cannot derive supply from a supported EVM/Solana contract. The supplemental fiat-CoinGecko path fetches `https://zephyrprotocol.com/api/v1/livestats` once per run when either asset is active:

- ZSD uses official `zsd_circ` for circulating supply and keeps CoinGecko as the preferred market price when available; if CoinGecko is missing, the protocol's reported `zsd_price` is used as a scoped `zephyr-scanner` fallback.
- ZYS uses official `zys_circ` and `zys_price` because neither CoinGecko nor DefiLlama exposes the yield-share wrapper.
- The emitted `zephyr-scanner` pricing source is registered as protocol telemetry with no dedicated circuit breaker and is only reachable from this narrow supplemental path.
- Scanner-supplied `captured_at` / `block_timestamp` / `timestamp` values are validated against the registered `zephyr-scanner` trust window before they become price or supply provenance. A present-but-invalid clock, a timestamp future-skewed beyond the shared 10-minute pricing-source allowance, or one older than the four-hour window makes the scanner price ineligible, not an undated/local-fetch quote. Rejected clocks leave supply provenance `null`; ZSD still publishes official circulation valued using an independently admitted preferred market price or its existing $1 supply fallback, but ZYS is withheld because its NAV valuation depends on the rejected scanner price. An admitted preferred market price retains its own clock. When all scanner clock fields are genuinely absent, the existing undated scanner-price behavior is unchanged.

The enrichment path is intentionally narrower than primary pricing:

- it exists to fill holes, not overrule good consensus
- fallback results are validated before they can enter hourly corroboration provenance or later claim an asset during publication
- the global price cache stores publication-safe continuity plus hourly fallback candidates with explicit fallback confidence; fallback candidates are revalidated on read and remain non-depeg-authoritative, while the verified CMC targeted cache remains a separate provider-local lane that never refreshes the original observation time or upgrades fallback confidence. Its staleness budget is calibrated against the producer cadence instead of equal to it: live targeted and category quotes stay admissible for one fetch cadence (one hour) plus a five-minute grace because CMC rolls `last_updated` hourly and the consumer fetch lands just after each roll boundary, and a cached verified quote bridges up to two cadences plus grace so a single missed CMC roll or one rotation-skipped fetch hour (the 25-slug request cap rotates candidates) does not blank the asset; anything older still goes missing rather than publishing stale. The `coinmarketcap` registry entry's publication `maxTrustedAgeSec` (two hours twenty minutes) covers that bridge plus one 15-minute publication slot, so a quote admitted during the :09 probe survives the staging handoff to the :15 publication instead of being discarded as `sourceExpired` after it had already suppressed the fresh retrieval that would have replaced it
- verified CMC continuity is applied only after fresh bounded category/targeted collection, or when collection is blocked, quota-suppressed, capped, failed or has no admissible replacement. An admissible cached quote never removes the original recovery cohort from the next available UTC-hour producer opportunity; replay preserves its upstream clock and missing-first rotation/request caps remain unchanged.
- ordinary replay is bounded by the six-hour `PRICE_CACHE_TTL` in `worker/src/cron/sync-stablecoins/post-enrichment.ts` and every component source's registry `maxTrustedAgeSec`, whichever is smaller; any non-replay-safe component makes the cache entry immediately ineligible
- previous-trusted continuity now merges the last authoritative stablecoins publication with fresh replay-safe `price_cache` rows, so a temporarily `low` or unusable publication does not make an already-confirmed severe depeg forget its prior corroborated state on the next run
- replay-safe cached fallback is applied to any asset that is still missing after post-validation, including assets that became missing later in the same sync run because a current-run candidate was rejected
- invalid or severely depegged single-source fallback prints are dropped instead of poisoning later runs

### Current Operator Limitations

NXUSD has no admitted deterministic ordinary-holder redemption/exact-pool adapter. Its separately allowlisted CoinGecko low-volume display recovery does not establish executable redemption, independent corroboration or depeg authority.

Specialty protocol and exact-pool routes are availability paths, not price guarantees. Kava, Citrea, Ethereum, Avalanche, or Plasma API/RPC failure; stale heads; identity, wrapper, or code-hash drift; unavailable or underfunded redemption; unavailable trusted parent prices; and insufficient executable depth all leave the asset missing and keep `activePriceCoverage` incomplete. Operators should investigate the route-specific diagnostic instead of extending replay age, filling nominal parity, or lowering global liquidity thresholds.

## Data Integrity Guardrails

Legacy Solomon USDv contract quotes are rejected before supplemental on-chain supply valuation when they alias replacement-token identity; exact legacy mint counts do not make a replacement-token price admissible. Missing admissible valuation leaves supplemental supply unavailable.

Temporary missing-price reviews expire normally. Mento source closures use the separate reviewed calendar and post-reopen observation gate in [ADR-43](../architecture.md#adr-43); missing rows and measured streaks remain visible throughout.

Exact-address routing hints are invalidated only when their matching chain/address receives a definitive answered miss (404 or no admitted quote), allowing canonical rediscovery next refresh. DexScreener emits per-target misses, including under-liquidity responses; mixed batches retain resolved hints. Capped, blocked, failed or locally aborted work retains hints. Answered address-price misses record `missing-quote` without treating coverage-only 404 as an outage.

CoinGecko discovery ticker flow is a price-evidence weight only. It cannot become TVL for supply valuation, pool challenge, primary DEX admission, UI/depeg trust or exact execution. Primary-missing withholding and genuine-pool TVL floors remain fail closed even when a zero-TVL exchange price row is published; the separate curated ticker/CEX primary lanes keep their existing source-family independence rules.

## Primary collection bookkeeping

Primary provider fetch results are independent of circuit-outcome bookkeeping. After admission, each completed fetch records its success/failure once through best-effort outcome persistence; a circuit-storage failure emits `circuit_outcome_write_failed` without relabeling a successful quote as a provider failure or discarding it. Required circuit-admission reads remain fail-closed, and cancellation still propagates.

Inline GeckoTerminal corroboration remains disabled at the Worker memory boundary. Final `sync-stablecoins` metadata retains `gtProbe.inlineDisabled = true` and `gtProbe.isolationReason = "worker-memory-boundary"`; the real authoritative-override progress boundary also carries that provenance. Disabled work has no dedicated progress stage. Primary collection, authoritative overrides, shared completion and price validation retain their real progress boundaries.

Primary planning, depeg hydration and pending confirmation each use one `loadDexPrices()` query for aggregate and source maps. Source freshness uses the caller's pre-await plan/run clock; row publication and original family `observedAt` ages at `DEX_PROTOCOL_SOURCE_FRESHNESS_SEC` are included. Original `aggregateObservedAt` must also be within the inclusive `DEX_FRESHNESS_SEC` budget. Both original clocks must be finite, positive and nonfuture; missing/invalid clocks never fall back to row `updated_at` or legacy source `updatedAt`. Admitted sources project their original family clock as internal `updatedAt`. Aggregate rows remain intact for downstream policies. NULL JSON is absent; stale/malformed JSON retains decoder telemetry. Only missing tables return empty maps; other database errors propagate.

### Reserve NAV and challenger implementation

Reserve NAV quotes use the shared decoder in `worker/src/lib/reserve-nav-price.ts`. It accepts only registered NAV adapters, independently checks the successful snapshot fetch age and upstream NAV evidence clock, and rejects invalid/nonpositive NAV, missing or malformed metadata, and stale or excessively future evidence. JPMorgan JLTXX uses the exact issuer Token Class transaction NAV with the shared five-day business-day NAV source-age policy; Chainlink and Superstate retain their existing source-specific policies. Supply admission can read the same matched successful snapshot before a new NAV asset has a previous stablecoin cache row: `fiat-cg.ts` values positive native on-chain supply at that observed NAV and applies the existing trusted FX conversion for non-USD classes. This valuation does not itself fabricate a published market quote; primary reserve-NAV consensus supplies the live price. A missing/stale NAV and absent alternative trusted price leave the candidate out, rather than assigning nominal $1.

Both primary collection and supplemental supply use `decodeCurrentReserveNavPrice()` to require the current semantic configuration fingerprint and canonical successful snapshot/attempt linkage for every NAV adapter. Fresh evidence from prior same-adapter oracle/token/method parameters is unavailable. A later failed attempt does not retire an otherwise current Chainlink/Superstate success; JPMorgan retains its stricter current issuer-class/latest-attempt binding. Fetch and source-age checks remain independent of configuration identity.

Challenger publication preserves protocol diversity before applying its 95% qualifying-TVL coverage target: it first retains the highest-TVL qualifying pool from each protocol, ordered by total qualifying protocol TVL, then fills remaining slots from the global pool-TVL order until the coverage target or 50-row hard cap is reached. If more than 50 protocols qualify, representatives from the 50 largest protocol groups are retained. This prevents a dominant venue from consuming the coverage budget before a smaller independent protocol can reach the multi-protocol replacement check; it does not change the per-pool TVL floor, freshness rules, validation, or replacement thresholds. Challenger pools come only from the retained set that already passed the discovery-time pool-price coherence admission ([DEX Liquidity](https://pharos.watch/docs/dex-liquidity/#discovery-cron)), so a provider row whose tracked-leg price is incoherent with its own pair ratio never becomes challenge or DEX-bridge evidence.

Every challenger selection step uses the same projected quote support, `min(retained TVL, priceEvidenceTvlUsd ?? retained TVL)`, and attribution follows `priceSource ?? source`. V2 publication sequence separates exact membership from source snapshot and original quote observation clocks. A same-clock shrink or empty publication replaces membership, never unions payloads. Readers join accepted head/payload coherently; staged rows are invisible, authoritative empty suppresses fallback, and missing/stale/incomplete selected snapshots retain explicit legacy fallback.

Pool replacement collapses `allPrices`, `observedAtBySource`, and `observedAtModeBySource` to `pool-tvl-weighted`, using the minimum contributing observation time and `local_fetch`. `agreeSources` becomes only that pool source; `candidateSources` retains earlier candidates and adds it, while `disagreeSources` becomes every other candidate. Downstream candidate-price checks therefore cannot reuse pre-replacement prices even though the diagnostic source roster is retained.

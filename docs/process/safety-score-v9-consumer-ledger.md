# Safety Score V9 Consumer Ledger

> **Status:** advisory migration inventory. An `OPEN` row records unfinished
> consumer work or review; it does not block the owner's V9 activation
> decision. Safety Score v8.17 remains the only active public model; the
> read-only unlisted V9 feedback preview remains a shadow consumer.

This ledger tracks the consumer contract in
[`safety-score-v9-rollout.md`](./safety-score-v9-rollout.md#consumer-contract).
It covers first-party runtime and public consumers of Safety Score score,
grade, V8 dimensions, raw report-card inputs, history, or compact caches. A row
is complete only when its V9 disposition is implemented, its mismatch behavior
is tested, and its material V8/V9 diff has a reviewed artifact.

## Contract Rules

- V9 `score` and `grade` may replace V8 overall score and grade only after the
  consumer's thresholds, defaults, and derived formulas are reviewed.
- V9 pillars are not aliases for the five V8 dimensions. Consumers must use
  `backing`, `exit`, and `control` explicitly or remove the dimension feature.
- Every materialized output must retain model, schema, methodology/policy,
  evaluation-build, base-input, and publication-generation identity.
- A mismatch must fail closed or return an explicit degraded result. It must
  never trigger a V8 recomputation after V9 activation.
- Activation and rollback boundaries are not organic score movements.
- `Diff: pending` means no reviewed consumer-specific V8/V9 evidence is yet
  recorded and therefore the row remains `OPEN`.

The temporary `/v9-preview/` reviewer page and its stable read endpoint are
deliberately outside the migration dispositions below. They
render only the strict `lifecycle: "shadow"` projection, retain a V9-specific
query identity, and expose no V8 fallback or admin data. Their existence does
not close C01 or C02: external compatibility, active-route behavior, full
frontend cutover, and reviewed consumer diffs remain open.

Move your electronic money at blockchain speed

CoinVertible offers you a strategic advantage in capital efficiency. It serves as a high-precision vehicle for asset deployment, merging the reliability of fiat with the limitless potential of smart financial ecosystems.

  • After a successful onboarding process by Societe Generale-Forge, an institutional investor (e.g. exchange or market maker) can submit a subscription for USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible
  • The institutional investor can buy USD CoinVertible, 1:1 by sending USD to Societe Generale-Forge bank account or buy EUR CoinVertible, 1:1 by sending Euro to Societe Generale-Forge bank account
  • Societe Generale-Forge sends USD CoinVertible or EUR CoinVertible to the institutional investor’s wallet
  • USD CoinVertible or EUR CoinVertible can be redeemed 1:1 against fiat USD (for USD CoinVertible ) or EUR (for EUR CoinVertible)

Technical implementation through the CAST* Framework provides streamlined solutions that are compatible and compliant with traditional financial practices, e.g. know-your-customer (KYC) and anti-money-laundering (AML) regulations.

Frequently asked questions

  • What are USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible tokens?

    USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible tokens are crypto-assets that are pegged to a fiat currency (USD for USD CoinVertible and EUR for EUR CoinVertible) on a 1:1 basis and that circulate across a blockchain like other crypto-assets.
    USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible are labelled as stablecoins as they are pegged to a fiat currency. All USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible are fully backed by collateral assets (see white paper and website for details).

  • How do I access USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible?

    Institutional investors can subscribe to USD CoinVertible or EUR CoinVertible after successful onboarding with Societe Generale-Forge.
    USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible are also available from digital asset exchanges, brokers and market makers.
    U.S Selling Restrictions apply (*).

  • Are USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible compliant with MiCA regulation?

    Yes, EUR CoinVertible is fully compliant since the inception of the MiCA stablecoin regulation on July 1st 2024. USD CoinVertible is fully compliant since launch on June 25th 2025.

  • Are USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible redeemable 1:1 for USD and for Euro?

    Yes, through Societe Generale-Forge or Preferred Partners (see white papers for details).

  • Who can use USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible?

    USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible enable business (for ex. exchanges, payment processors, financial services) and individuals to seamlessly use regulated electronic money on blockchain

  • On which blockchain are USD CoinVertible and EUR CoinVertible available?

    USD CoinVertible (Etherscan) and EUR CoinVertible (Etherscan) available on Ethereum public. EUR CoinVertible is also available on Solana (Solscan), soon to come for USD CoinVertible. EUR CoinVertible is also available on XRPL (Bithomp)

  • What is Societe Generale-Forge?

    Societe Generale-Forge is a fully integrated subsidiary of Societe Generale Group, regulated as an investment firm under EU MiFID2 Regulation, as an Electronic Money Institution under MiCA (Markets in Crypto-assets) Regulation and as digital asset service provider under French law.
    Societe Generale-Forge provides issuers and investors, either Corporates or Financial Institutions, with end-to-end services to issue and manage digital-native financial products registered on the blockchain (Security Token). Societe Generale-Forge is the issuer of the USD CoinVertible and the EUR CoinVertible.

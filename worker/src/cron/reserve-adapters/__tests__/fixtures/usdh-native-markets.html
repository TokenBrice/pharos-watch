How is USDH backed?

All USDH is fully backed 1:1 by US Treasuries, cash, and cash equivalents. Offchain reserves are managed by BlackRock and custodied with JPMorgan Chase, while USDH onchain reserves are managed by Superstate and custodied in Fireblocks infrastructure. USDH is designed by Native Markets and issued by Bridge, a US-regulated entity.

> [Design context](docs/design-language.md#context) owns audience, posture and principles; it wins over this compact skill bootstrap. [Design Language](docs/design-language.md) owns reusable UI rules; [Editorial Style](docs/editorial-style.md) owns sentences.

## Register

product

## Users

Crypto-native DeFi practitioners monitoring stablecoin pegs, backing, control, liquidity and risk. They value precise, dense evidence and fast verification. Discovery welcomes newcomers without softening analytical data.

## Product Purpose

Free, open-source stablecoin intelligence: research dossiers and monitoring that let a reader verify a claim and its evidence quickly. Vigilant, precise, distinctive; calm by default and explicit when risk rises. A lighthouse, not a promotional trading product.

## Anti-references

- Web3 marketing pages (purple gradients, glassmorphism, buzzwords)
- Corporate fintech sterility (bank-app blandness)
- Generic SaaS dashboards (interchangeable KPI tiles, big empty cards)
- Reskinned DefiLlama / generic trading-terminal clones
- Consumer-app over-softening of data surfaces (no mascots or chunky illustrations inside data)

## Legend

- **Confidence: High** — Sourced from attestation report, SEC filing, or official transparency dashboard
- **Confidence: Medium** — Sourced from protocol docs, third-party analysis, or on-chain data
- **Confidence: Low** — Estimated from collateral descriptions, no public breakdown available
- **SKIP** — No reserve breakdown found; coin should keep using template default

## Risk Classification Reference

| Risk | Assets |
|------|--------|
| `low` | U.S. Treasuries (<=1yr), overnight repos, FDIC deposits, regulated MMFs, cash |
| `medium` | ETH, BTC, wstETH, WBTC, corporate bonds, regulated stablecoins as collateral, tokenized treasuries (BUIDL, USYC) |
| `high` | Altcoins, perp futures, unsecured loans, exotic DeFi, <1yr track record |

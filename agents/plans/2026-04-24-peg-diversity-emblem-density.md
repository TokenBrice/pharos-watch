# Peg Diversity Emblem Density Plan

## Assumptions

- The atlas should keep the same authored map/sky composition, but the stablecoin logos can be smaller because hover cards carry the detailed labels.
- The map should render on small screens without depending on a horizontally scaled scene as the default interaction.
- The size legend should describe the smaller logo scale with more than three reference points.

## Success Criteria

- Fiat and sky emblems no longer overlap on current live stablecoin data.
- The atlas renders at mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.
- Map ticker pills are hidden; hover cards remain available for details.
- The fiat logo size key has five market-cap steps.
- Focused tests and browser screenshots verify the result.

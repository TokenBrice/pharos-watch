# KIMI-FIAT-TBILL ledger — 2026-07-27

## Scope and decision rule

This is the serial-curation ledger for the 151 bounded mechanism-review items in
`measurement-2026-07-27/lane-fiat-tbill.json`: 90 assets, ordered by frozen lane supply
(66 fiat-cash and 24 tbill). The D3 standard is applied literally: an overlay component is
added only when the cited primary source meets its evidence class for that exact component.
Every `BLOCKED(standard-insufficient)` row has **D3 evidence class: none** for the missing
component; issuer disclosure, a stale report, a license, a marketing page, or a non-itemized
dashboard is recorded as context rather than promoted to a quality claim. No fiat/tbill
numeric metrics were created (`metrics: {}` remains true for the two newly completed tbill
entries).

`APPLIED(D3-adequate)` is used only for the two Spiko duration/liquidity claims. Their shared
4 May 2026 formal UCITS prospectus is a dated primary prospectus that sets binding limits for
the exact component. It is not treated as a current measured WAM/WAL, and it is not enough for
`strong` because no current independent monthly NAV/reserve reconciliation is pinned.

The adverse-pinned exclusion is absent from this lane and was not read, edited, or otherwise
touched. `krwq-iq` and `eur0-usual` carry no `gapId`; their score-projection task IDs are
recorded as registry anomalies rather than invented mechanism gaps.

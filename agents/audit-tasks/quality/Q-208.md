# Q-208 — M0 adapter metadata comment `cashUnits: 'milli-usd-to-micro-usd'` is factually wrong
**Low** · effort **S** · _naming_ · pillar **quality**
**Location:** `worker/src/cron/reserve-adapters/m0.ts:L63, L171`
**Found by slice:** `wc-reserve-adapters-2`
## Problem
The comment at L102–107 correctly states "The live dashboard exposes `totalCash` three decimal orders below the treasury fields" and multiplies by 1 000 (`M0_CASH_SCALE`). The metadata field `cashUnits: 'milli-usd-to-micro-usd'` (L171) contradicts this: multiplying by 1 000 converts *milli-USD* to plain *USD*, not to *micro-USD* (which would require ×1 000 000). Downstream dashboards or consumers reading this metadata field to understand the units will be misled.
## Proposed remediation
Change L171 to `cashUnits: "milli-usd-to-usd"` (or `"1000x-scale"`) to match the prose comment at L102–107 and the actual applied scale.

# R-075 — parseDigestParagraph duplicates bold-header stripping already done in consumers of lib/digest
**Medium** · effort **S** · _clone_ · pillar **redundancy**
**Location:** `src/components/daily-digest.tsx:L67-L72`
**Found by slice:** `sc-toplevel-4`
## Problem
`parseDigestParagraph` (local to daily-digest.tsx) extracts a `**bold**` header from a paragraph string. `digest-archive-client.tsx:L69` does the same strip with a raw `.replace(/\*\*.*?\*\*\s*/, '')`. The pattern exists in at least two places and belongs in `src/lib/digest.ts` alongside `splitDigestParagraphs`.
## Proposed remediation
Move the extraction logic into a named helper in `src/lib/digest.ts` and replace both in-file copies. The digest archive teaser strip can then reuse it rather than an ad-hoc regex.

# Pharos External Link Opportunities - 2026-04-19

## Assumptions

- Pharos should be positioned as a public-good stablecoin analytics and risk research dashboard, not as a stablecoin issuer, token, wallet, or trading product.
- The goal is qualitative inbound links and entity recognition, not raw directory volume.
- No submissions were made during this research pass because most credible targets require owner accounts, profile ownership, business email verification, or final launch assets.
- Impact is qualitative: topical relevance, likely editorial trust, referral audience, and durability of the page.

## Partnership / Earned-Link Targets

These should not be pitched as "please link to us." They need a useful asset or clear integration story.

| Target | Best ask | Why | Effort | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bluechip | Ask for a neutral partner/use-case mention: "Pharos surfaces Bluechip safety ratings." | Pharos already integrates Bluechip ratings and Bluechip has publicly posted about Pharos. | Low-medium | Must avoid implying endorsement beyond actual integration. |
| CoinGecko | Pitch a case study: "public-good stablecoin risk dashboard using CoinGecko / GeckoTerminal data." | CoinGecko publishes case studies and attribution guidance. | Medium | Need clean attribution and no implied partnership unless approved. |
| DefiLlama | Propose a docs/example issue or PR showing a public dashboard using stablecoins endpoints. | Pharos uses DefiLlama heavily and is open-source. | Medium | Could be rejected as promotional unless framed technically. |
| Stablecoin Insider / Stablecoin Standard | Pitch a data-driven research note or member-resource listing. | Very high topical overlap; Stablecoin Insider is Stablecoin Standard's media partner. | Medium | Some routes may be paid/sponsored; keep earned angle clear. |
| Academic/policy pages | Send a stable dataset or API endpoint, not the homepage. | Stablecoin research needs incident/failure/depeg data. | High | Slow payoff; requires reproducible data and licensing clarity. |

## Linkable Assets To Build Before Outreach

| Asset | Why it earns better links than a homepage | Minimum viable version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stablecoin Cemetery dataset | Failure datasets are more citable than live market-cap tables. | CSV/JSON export with coin, cause, dates, peak cap, source links, uncertainty flags, and a methodology page. |
| DEWS incident briefings | During stress events, journalists need "what moved first" with timestamps. | Static post per major depeg covering price-source divergence, liquidity erosion, mint/burn changes, blacklist signals, and limitations. |
| Dependency / contagion map export | Systemic dependency is differentiated and relevant to risk teams. | Static share page plus CSV/API export of dependency edges, assumptions, and version. |
| Public API collection | API directories need machine-readable or executable examples. | Postman collection + OpenAPI spec linked from `/about/api/`. |

## Avoid For Now

- Wikipedia/Wikidata: do not add Pharos directly until independent third-party coverage exists. Risk of removal and COI tagging is high.
- Generic paid SEO directories: not worth the signal dilution.
- AI-tool directories such as There Is An AI For That: only relevant if Pharos has a real AI-first product surface.
- Datarade/Monda: only worth considering if Pharos becomes a commercial data vendor.
- Wallet dapp stores such as TokenPocket: poor fit because Pharos is not a wallet-connected dapp.

## Recommended Execution Order

1. Same day: Blockscan/Etherscan, Ethereum Ecosystem, StableCoin Hub, llms.txt directories.
2. Week 1: Ethereum Dashboards PR, awesome-list PRs, Alchemy, DappRadar, The Grid.

## Theme-Based Changelog

### Product And UX

- Added end-to-end pre-launch stablecoin support: new status and launch-phase modeling, 7 seeded pre-launch assets, dedicated pre-launch detail layouts, homepage upcoming modules, command-palette labeling, editorial summaries, and logos.
- Expanded that work into a full `/upcoming` route with milestones, curated data helpers, and Telegram launch alerts.

- Added or refined major user-facing elements including SSR detail-page headers and H1s, a PSI regime bar, confidence-weighted typography, unified chart tooltips, a safety score calculator, a redesigned grade-history module, a collateral section on stablecoin detail pages, and better empty/loading states.
- Fixed several interaction and rendering issues including stale route recovery, yield scatter clipping, horizontal table scrollbar visibility, table setting ergonomics, filter shortcut removal, and page ordering/layout regressions.
- Reverted a short-lived feature-page accent-border treatment after deciding it did not add useful signal.

### Pricing, Liquidity, Reserves, And Methodology

- Pricing got a major source and integrity upgrade: Kraken, Bitstamp, Jupiter, and Chainlink were added; consensus and source admission were hardened; disagreement logging improved; peg-aware depeg confirmation logic was tightened; and fallback recovery, freshness provenance, FX carry-forward behavior, and cache writes were corrected.
- DEX liquidity moved well beyond crawler-only coverage. The repo added Fluid, Balancer, Raydium, and Orca direct API fetchers, bridged per-protocol prices into pricing consensus, surfaced per-pool prices and richer pool detail, and added drift/evidence telemetry plus improved confidence semantics.
- Live reserve coverage expanded aggressively, culminating in 114 live-enabled coins across 26 adapters. The sync pipeline was hardened around parsing, storage, validation, scoring, display blocks, adapter quality, and operational resilience.
- Redemption backstop modeling was expanded and corrected across confidence tranches, fee handling, queue semantics, live buffers, and eligibility rules, with matching API/test/doc updates.
- Blacklist tracking was substantially upgraded with provenance and amount semantics, Tron-specific handling, aggregate helpers, a new summary API, amount-gap telemetry, and remediation controls for attribution gaps.
- PSI handling was tightened through replay and view-model work so presentation and historical recomputation stay consistent across API, digest, OG image, and frontend consumers.
- Mint/burn flow coverage and reliability improved through expanded Ethereum support, price healing, roundtrip classification improvements, hardened persistence and sync state, and a D1 bind-parameter overflow fix.
- Yield intelligence received a large safety remediation pass spanning discovery coverage, deterministic edge-case overrides, config evaluation/publication logic, history APIs, source-link handling, and tests.

### Coverage And Content

- Added DUSD and USSD as tracked stablecoins and added USBD coverage.
- Rewrote all 174 AI editorial summaries against the fuller Pharos data model.
- Added collateral usage information to detail pages and refreshed about-page/team content.
- Improved KAU and KAG ticker extraction, fixed Kinesis supply parsing, corrected Circle live reserve parsing, and refreshed multiple stablecoin sync/content paths.

### Ops, CI, SEO, And Docs

- Release safety improved through stronger CI and local validation: `npm run test:merge-gate` was enforced pre-push, Pages deploys were gated on UI smoke tests, workflow paths were optimized, and worker deploys were skipped on frontend-only pushes.
- Pages-build analytics support was restored, and several hotspot-baseline and merge-gate issues were cleaned up to keep automated validation green.
- SEO/shareability improved through OG image work, FAQ structured data, sitemap fixes, server-rendered stablecoin H1s, and worker share-card rendering fixes.
- Documentation was continuously realigned with product and pipeline behavior. `api-reference`, methodology docs, timeline docs, pricing docs, live-reserve docs, testing docs, worker docs, and page-specific docs all saw repeated sync passes.
- Dependabot updates landed for `jsdom`, `github/codeql-action`, grouped minor/patch dependencies, and `actions/checkout`.

# Selector Editorial Examples

Checked-in prose examples for `shared/lib/selector/__tests__/editorial-policy.test.ts`.

## Treasury

USDC ranks highly for a treasury profile because it combines deep liquidity, broad venue coverage, and strong current peg readings. Watch issuer concentration and reserve-report cadence before treating it as a policy fit.

## Yield

USDe appears as a yield-profile candidate when the user accepts structured-yield exposure and monitors dependency risk. Watch source concentration, redemption assumptions, and recent peg pressure before sizing any workflow around it.

## Active Trading

USDT can appear in active-trading profiles when liquidity depth and venue coverage dominate the selected constraints. Watch freeze permissions, chain-specific settlement risk, and recent peg movement before moving flow across venues.
